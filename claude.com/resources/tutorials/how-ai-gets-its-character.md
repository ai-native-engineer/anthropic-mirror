# How AI gets its character

An AI assistant's personality — how wordy, polite, or agreeable it is — isn't random. Those behaviors were trained in, across two stages: pretraining, which builds a document completer, and fine-tuning, which shapes the model into an assistant. Each stage leaves fingerprints on the finished system, and this video shows you where to look for them.
