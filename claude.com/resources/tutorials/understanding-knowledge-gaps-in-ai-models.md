# Understanding knowledge gaps in AI models

An AI model only knows what was in its training data, and that training has a cutoff date. Oftentimes a model’s knowledge is broad but tends to be uneven — solid on frequent, well-covered topics; thin on anything rare, recent, or niche. This video maps where the gaps are, so you know when to trust an answer, when to verify it, and which product features fill the holes.
