# What happens when you talk to AI?

When you send a message to an AI, there's a moment where it appears to be “thinking.” But what's actually happening? This video walks you through what's going on under the hood. AI models like Claude work by prediction. But the mechanism is much more complex than you’d think. An AI writes one word at a time, but it doesn't think one word at a time. This video breaks down the prediction process behind every AI output, and how to better interpret the responses you get back.
