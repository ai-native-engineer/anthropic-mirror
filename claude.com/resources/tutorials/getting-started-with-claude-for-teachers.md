# Getting started with Claude for Teachers

This article explains Claude for Teachers, a program that gives verified US K-12 educators free access to professional Claude accounts, with teaching skills and a curriculum connector built for classroom prep.

## **Availability**

Claude for Teachers is available to verified K-12 school staff in the United States on claude.ai (web, desktop, and mobile).

This program is for educators. There are no student accounts and no student-facing capabilities.

Educators who sign up before June 30, 2027 will receive at least a full year of free access.

## **Who’s eligible**

Any staff member at a US K-12 school or district can verify, including:

* Classroom teachers
* Instructional coaches and aides
* Librarians and media specialists
* Special education staff
* School and district administrators

The program is named Claude for Teachers, but eligibility covers all verified K-12 school staff. You must be 18 or older.

International educators are not eligible at this time.

## **How to get verified**

1. Sign in to your Claude account at claude.ai, or create one. You must use your school email.
2. Go to [**Claude for Teachers**](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers) and select **Get verified.**
3. Complete verification through Goodstack. You’ll be asked for documentation to verify your school/district affiliation.
4. You can continue using Claude while you wait for verification, but the K-12 educator terms will only apply after verification is complete. Verification usually takes less than 24 hours, but in some cases can take up to 3 days. You can check your verification status in your Claude settings.
5. Once verified, you’ll receive a confirmation email. Your account upgrades automatically to premium Claude capabilities. You may need to refresh if you are already logged in to begin the educator onboarding.

‍

**Already have an individual Claude Pro subscription?** Verify on your existing account and it will move to the free educator plan. You’ll receive a prorated credit for any unused portion of your subscription.

You will be asked to re-verify periodically to keep your educator access active.

## **What’s included**

**Increased usage limits**

Full access to a professional Claude account with Claude for Team usage limits.

**Teaching skills**

Skills built for lesson planning and differentiation.

**Learning Commons curriculum connector**

Connects Claude to K-12 academic standards and learning components, as well as high-quality curricula from Illustrative Math and OpenSciEd that are only available to verified educators.

**Educator onboarding and sidebar page**

A short setup that asks what you teach and where, so Claude tailors its responses to your classroom. You can revisit your educator settings anytime in the sidebar.

## **Start using Claude for Teachers**

After verification, you’ll see a brief educator onboarding the next time you open Claude. From there, try one of the suggested prompts:

* **Plan a lesson.** Tell Claude your grade, subject, and where your class is in the unit. Claude draws on Learning Commons and your state standards to draft a plan and student-facing materials for your review.
* **Differentiate.** Share a lesson and the range in your room (reading levels, IEPs, English language learners) and Claude produces scaffolded variants.
* **Build an assessment.** Ask for a do-now, quiz, or exit ticket tied to a standard, with an answer key.
* **Draft family communication.** Claude drafts the email; you review and decide what’s right for your families.

In **Claude Cowork** (research preview), you can hand off a multi-step job — like building a full set of lesson materials or revising materials already saved on your computer — and come back to finished slide decks, student packets, and exit tickets saved as files. Learn more in [**Getting started with Cowork**](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-getting-started-with-cowork).

You can also connect to a range of other educator services. Please see the connector directory for the [full list of connectors](https://claude.com/connectors).

## **Privacy and student data**

* **View our privacy documentation**: teacher terms of service and a [plain-English explanation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041) of how we handle student data.
* **We never train our models on your conversations.** Training is off for verified teacher accounts.
* **Student information is protected by our K-12 Data Processing Addendum,** written to comply with FERPA.

## **For districts and administrators**

Individual educators verify themselves through the steps above - there’s nothing a district needs to provision.

Schools and districts are eligible for discounted Claude for Nonprofits pricing. If you’re a district or school administrator looking to set up Claude for your organization with admin controls, SSO, and centralized management, see [**Getting started with Claude for Nonprofits**](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12893767-getting-started-with-claude-for-nonprofits).

## **Frequently asked questions**

**Can students use this product?** No. Claude for Teachers is for K-12 educators. There are no student accounts. Users under 18 are prohibited from using Claude, and we use [age assurance services](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15171100-age-assurance-on-claude) to enforce this policy.

**How long will it stay free?** Claude for Teachers will be free for at least a year for educators who sign up before June 30, 2027. We will provide updates if the offer is extended.

**Do I have to teach from Illustrative Math or OpenSciEd to benefit?** No. The Learning Commons connector grounds Claude’s drafts in state standards and widely used open curriculum as a reference. You’re not locked into any program, and Claude does not reproduce curriculum wholesale.

**What if my verification doesn’t go through instantly?** Goodstack may require up to three days to review documentation (such as a school ID or pay stub). Follow the prompts in the verification flow. If you encounter an issue with verification, contact **Goodstack** [**support**](https://support.claude.com).

**Can I use my personal email?** No. Verification requires your professional email.

**Where can I receive guidance on AI use in education?** Teachers can access our [AI Fluency for K-12 Teachers](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-pk12-educators), a course co-created with Teach for America, and a train-the-trainer module co-created with the American Federation of Teachers. The guidance is model-agnostic, Creative Commons-licensed, and practical, covering which classroom tasks AI is suited to and how to use it responsibly with students.

**I’m at a university or college. Is this for me?** Claude for Teachers is for K-12. For higher education, see [**Get started with Claude for Education at your university**](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11139094-get-started-with-claude-for-education-at-your-university-for-owners-admins).
