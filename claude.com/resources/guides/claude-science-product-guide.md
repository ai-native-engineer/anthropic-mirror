# Claude Science product guide

Claude Science (in beta) is Anthropic's answer to the toil tax on every experiment: an application for every digital step of life science, built to run next to the scientist's data and produce results that can be traced, reproduced, and defended.

This guide covers which Claude surface to reach for when, then goes deep on deploying Claude Science inside a research organization, with customer examples, a rollout roadmap, and answers to the questions CIOs and IT leaders ask.
