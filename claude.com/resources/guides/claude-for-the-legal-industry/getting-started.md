# Getting started

2 min read

2 min remaining

The Claude ecosystem for legal spans connectors, plugins, partner MCP apps, and pre-built agent roles for in-house, law firm, and compliance work. All of it runs on the [enterprise controls regulated organizations require (opens in new tab)](https://trust.anthropic.com/), including SSO, SCIM, [audit logs (opens in new tab)](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9970975-access-audit-logs), [custom data retention (opens in new tab)](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10440198-custom-data-retention-controls-for-enterprise-plans), and [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certification (opens in new tab)](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-achieves-iso-42001-certification-for-responsible-ai).

To explore how Claude fits your firm or legal department, visit [our legal solutions page (opens in new tab)](https://www.claude.com/solutions/legal) or contact your Anthropic account team at [sales@anthropic.com (opens in new tab)](mailto:sales@anthropic.com).
