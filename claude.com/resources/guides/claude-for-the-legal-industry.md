# Claude for the legal industry

The legal profession is at an inflection point. The work is getting more demanding: matters are more complex, clients expect faster turnaround, and hiring is harder than it used to be. At the same time, AI has matured from a curiosity into a practical tool that can help with all three. Firms and legal departments that adopt it thoughtfully now will be better positioned for what comes next.

This guide walks through each product, offers customer examples from across the legal industry, and shares tips on how to roll out Claude inside your organization.
