# Front office: Portfolio management and deals

3 min read

33 min remaining

Portfolio managers, and the deal partners who lead transactions at private-markets firms, work with Claude as a chief of staff for the book or the portfolio. Claude ranks the morning’s news by exposure, answers questions about positions and portfolio companies, and runs scenarios in the analyst’s model, so sizing and pricing decisions are based on the team’s numbers.

## Main use cases

### Generating a morning brief, ranked by exposure

A scheduled task runs overnight, compiling market updates that affect the book: news, broker commentary, pre-market moves, portfolio-company updates, and model changes. It ranks them by position size and move, and sends a one-page brief to the team’s preferred channel, such as email, Slack, or a dashboard, all before the day starts.

### Answering questions about positions and portfolio companies

In Claude Enterprise, portfolio managers and deal partners can ask questions in plain English, such as “What’s my beta-adjusted net in European cyclicals?” or “Which portfolio companies are behind plan this quarter?” Claude answers using context from the firm’s position, portfolio, and risk systems through connectors, inside a project scoped to that team.

### Running what-if scenarios in the analyst’s model

For a scenario like “gross margin down 200 basis points and the buyback halves” or “exit at 9x in year four,” Claude flexes the drivers in the analyst’s Excel model and lists exactly which cells changed. The team can check the math cell by cell and focus the discussion on whether the assumptions hold.

### Checking diligence files for completeness

Before an idea or a deal reaches committee, Claude reads the associate’s diligence file against the firm’s checklist and past deals and lists what’s missing, thin, or unsupported. The partner’s review starts from that list.

### Creating dashboards on demand

Portfolio managers ask Claude for interactive dashboards built on the firm’s connected data, such as cross-asset moves around an event or a roll-up of portfolio-company metrics.

#### Outcome: Portfolio managers and deal partners spend the first hour of the day on the brief’s top-ranked positions and companies, and Claude answers scenario questions in the analyst’s model.
