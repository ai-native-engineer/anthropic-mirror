# Back office: Investor relations

3 min read

15 min remaining

Investor relations and fundraising teams work with Claude to draft due-diligence questionnaires and investor letters, to prepare for meetings, and to produce materials.

## Main use cases

### Drafting questionnaires from a governed answer bank

Claude drafts each answer to due-diligence questionnaires and requests for proposal into the allocator’s template, using approved language and prior responses and citing a source for each answer. It routes any questions without an approved answer to subject-matter experts. Claude handles questions about the firm’s own AI governance the same way.

### Drafting investor letters and quarterly commentary

Claude drafts investor letters using performance, attribution, portfolio-company updates, and portfolio managers’ notes from the reporting pack. It then follows a Marketing Rule skill to give the draft a first-pass check.

### Building decks and factsheets

Claude Design (beta) builds factsheets and one-pagers on the firm’s design system, and Claude Slides (beta) drafts pitchbooks and annual-meeting decks. Both can be exported to PowerPoint or PDF. Claude can also refresh slides in the existing template directly from the Excel pack.

### Preparing for meetings and handling investor requests

Before an allocator meeting, an agent compiles the relationship history, recent communications, and likely questions. Afterward, it logs the notes and follow-ups in the CRM. When investors request exposure cuts, look-through data, or capital-account reports, Claude prepares them in the investor’s template using systems of record.

#### Outcome: Questionnaires and data requests go out sooner, in consistent, sourced language. Teams produce decks and factsheets in-house.
