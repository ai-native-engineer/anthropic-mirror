# Middle office: Compliance and legal

3 min read

24 min remaining

Compliance teams work with Claude to prepare alert queues, review expert-call transcripts, answer policy questions, run a first pass on marketing materials, and track regulatory change. Counsel ask it to review trading and deal agreements and side letters, and to draft answers to the business's questions about fund documents for counsel to review.

## Main use cases

### Reviewing surveillance alerts and expert-call transcripts

Surveillance systems flag trades and messages that might break a rule, and most flags turn out to be false alarms. Claude reads each flagged item, rates how likely it is to be a real problem, and drafts a recommendation to close or escalate it, with the reasons why. The compliance officer makes the call from that write-up. Claude also flags possible material nonpublic information (MNPI) in expert-call transcripts for the compliance officer to review.

### Relaying pre-clearance decisions and answering policy questions

Employees ask Claude, “Can I trade this?” The firm’s pre-clearance system checks the restricted list and holding-period rules and makes the decision, and Claude relays the outcome and logs the exchange. The same assistant can also answer questions about the compliance manual, citing its sources.

### Reviewing agreements and tracking investor obligations

Claude checks trading and deal agreements against the firm’s playbook, flags deviations, and drafts redlines. Anthropic’s open-source legal plugin for Claude and Claude Code includes a ready-made command for this review. For side letters and limited partnership agreements (LPAs), Claude extracts the terms into an obligations matrix, which the team uses for most-favored-nation elections, fee checks, and investor reporting.

### Keeping records of AI use, reviewing marketing materials, and tracking regulatory change

The firm captures prompts and responses in its compliance archive, through its cloud logging or Anthropic’s Compliance API (on Enterprise plans). Claude gives outbound decks and letters a first-pass check against the SEC Marketing Rule and maps new regulatory releases to the compliance calendar and the policies they affect.

#### Outcome: Compliance officers can focus on deciding cases instead of assembling them, counsel can spend more of their time on negotiation, and the firm has a record of its AI use.
