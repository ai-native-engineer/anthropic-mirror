##  Create an account

1Go to [claude.ai (opens in a new tab)](https://claude.ai)

2Select **Continue with Google** or enter your email address

3Follow the prompts to complete registration

All plans have access to the [Connectors Directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory). Free plans can also add one [custom connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/remote-mcp). Compare [Claude plans and pricing (opens in a new tab)](https://claude.com/pricing).

Claude for Government access is set up by your agency’s administrators, so you don’t need to create an account. See the [Claude for Government guide](https://claude.com/docs/government/overview).
