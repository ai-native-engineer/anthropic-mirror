# Life Sciences Verification Program

The Life Sciences Verification Program (beta), gives qualifying organizations expanded access to Mythos 5.1 for biology and drug discovery, and Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5 with a refined bio safety classifier.

LSVP is currently available to verified organizations on Claude Enterprise and Team plans, and the Claude Platform (API). Free, Pro, and Max plans are not eligible at this time. Please have your org’s admin complete the following form to register your interest. From there, we will assess your organization's eligibility to participate.

---

Completing this form does not guarantee your organization access to the program. If eligible, we’ll be in touch with more details. For more information or questions, please reach out to your account team.
