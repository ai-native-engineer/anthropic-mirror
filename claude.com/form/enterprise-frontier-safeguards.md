# Enterprise Frontier Safeguards

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) is rolling out to eligible customers in phases, with the goal of making it broadly available this fall.

EFS combines the privacy of zero data retention (ZDR) with state-of-the-art safeguards for detecting misuse. EFS works by storing data in cloud infrastructure controlled by the customer, not Anthropic.

Please complete the following form so we can confirm your organization's eligibility as we expand access to EFS.

Completing this form does not guarantee your organization access to the controls. For more information please reach out to your account team.
