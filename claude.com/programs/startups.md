FAQ

### Who can apply?

Startups founded in the last 5 years or funded in the last 2. You’ll need a Claude Console account, a company email that matches your website’s domain, and a short description of what you’re building. We review every application. Applicants must comply with Anthropic’s supportability policies.

### What do members get?

One year of Claude Team free for up to five Premium seats if you’re new to Team, $1,000 in Claude API credits, up to $45K in offers through the Claude Startup Stack, office hours with Anthropic’s Applied AI team, and access to Claude Startup events like Founder House, hackathons, and meetups.

### Do I need VC funding to apply?

No. The program is open to bootstrapped, pre-seed, and venture-backed startups. If you’re backed by a VC in our partner network, you can also get up to $100K in additional API credits, claimed through your VC.

### How do I apply?

Sign in to the Claude Console and fill out the application.

### What is the Claude Startup Stack?

Offers from AI-native companies building on Claude, across sales, design, data, meetings, and engineering. Members redeem each offer with a code in the Claude Console.

### Do members get Claude API credits?

Yes. Every approved company gets $1,000 in Claude API credits and one year of Claude Team free for up to five Premium seats, if you’re new to Claude Team. Founders backed by a VC in our partner network can get up to $100K in additional API credits through their VC.

### Does the program include higher API rate limits?

Yes. Members who receive credits through the program automatically get higher API rate limits.

### If I already have a Teams plan, what happens?

Only orgs that are new to Teams qualify for this offer.

### Can I use credits on AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI?

No. Claude Startups credits apply to the first-party Claude API through Claude Console. They can’t be used on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, or other third-party platforms.

### How does the free year of Claude Team work?

Approved members who are new to Claude Team get one year free for up to five Premium seats. After you’re accepted, you’ll get an email from us with a link to the Claude Startups page in Claude Console. Sign in there to claim your Claude Team offer.

### When do I get my API credits, and do they expire?

Every approved company gets $1,000 in Claude API credits. Once you’re accepted, sign in to the Claude Startups page in Claude Console and claim them there. Credits expire six months after they’re granted.

### I’m already a member. Do I need to reapply?

No. As a member of our Claude for Startups program you are now eligible to redeem the offers in the console.

### How long does an application review take?

We review every application. Most are decided within minutes; the rest go to a manual review, which usually takes 2–3 business days. You’ll get an email as soon as there’s a decision.

### How do I book office hours?

Applied AI office hours are 45-minute live sessions for members, held every other week. Accepted members get a link to register by email and in the Claude Startups portal.
