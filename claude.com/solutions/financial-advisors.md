### Products and features

### What is Claude for Financial Advisors?

Claude for Financial Advisors is a [plugin (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-financial-advisors), or a bundle of skills, that you install from the plugin directory in Claude. It is not a new model or a separate app. The plugin is tuned for your advisory practice with connections to the systems you already use, ready-made workflows for meeting prep, follow-up, portfolio reviews, and compliance checks with a guided setup.

### Which systems does it connect to?

Claude for Financial Advisors [connects (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/connectors) to your custodian, portfolio, planning, and CRM systems: Addepar, BlackRock, Charles Schwab, Envestnet, iCapital, Orion, SS&C Black Diamond, Vanguard, Wealthbox, Wealth.com, and Zocks. It also works with Microsoft 365, Salesforce, Docusign, and Box.

### Do I need IT to set it up?

Setting up Claude for Financial Advisors is easy to do, and you don’t need IT to help. [Install the plugin (opens in new tab)](https://academy.claude.com/tutorials/install-financial-services-plugins-for-cowork) in Claude and start with the prompt “Onboard me to Claude for Financial Advisors.” Claude’s guided setup then walks you through connecting your tools. You choose which ones to connect.

### What if my custodian or CRM isn’t on the list yet?

If your custodian or CRM isn’t on our list yet, [let us know (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/contact-sales/financial-services). The directory of connectors keeps growing, and we’d love to hear which platforms matter most to your practice.

### Which plan do I need?

The plugin works on [paid Claude plans (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/pricing), but we recommend Enterprise for Registered Investment Advisor as it is the only plan that includes audit logs and compliance controls for a regulated firm.

### Does it work with Claude or Claude Cowork?

Claude Cowork is now just Claude, so you don’t have to choose where to get work done. The update is rolling out to Pro, Max, and Ultra plans, with more to follow. If you’re on a different plan, you’ll be walked through the update in the product. We hope you enjoy the simpler experience. Read more about [Claude Code (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-claude-code-and-claude-cowork-to-government) and [how Claude Cowork changed (opens in new tab)](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude).

### Compliance and data privacy

### Does Claude give investment advice?

Claude does not give investment advice. It pulls the information together and drafts the analysis for your review. The advice and the decision stay with you.

### Can Claude place a trade, move money, or email a client on its own?

No. Claude doesn’t place trades or move money. Notes, emails, and CRM updates are drafted and held until you approve them.

### How does it fit with our compliance review?

Claude fits into your advisory workflows by having review steps built in and keeping a record of what was done. The compliance workflow pre-checks client-facing language against the SEC Marketing Rule and flags what your CCO should see. It’s a pre-check ahead of your review, not an approval.

### Is client data safe, and do you train on it?

Claude only reaches the systems you connect, with the access you already have in them. Each platform stays the system of record for its own data. On Team and Enterprise plans, we [don't train our models (opens in new tab)](https://trust.anthropic.com/) on your data by default.
