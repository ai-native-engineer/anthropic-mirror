Glean is the context platform for enterprise AI. It connects company knowledge, people, decisions, and workflows across systems, giving employees and AI agents the context they need to get work done.

With Claude models, Glean Assistant helps employees find answers and make decisions using company knowledge. Glean Agents use that context to complete multi-step tasks and take action across connected apps. Glean respects existing source permissions so information stays within the access boundaries your organization has set.

## What you can do

* **Ground AI in company context:** Glean’s Enterprise Graph connects company knowledge with the people, projects, and decisions behind it, helping Claude understand how work gets done.
* **Find answers and make decisions:** Glean Assistant brings together information from connected systems to help employees answer questions and make informed decisions.
* **Complete work with agents:** Build or use Glean Agents that pair Claude’s reasoning with company context to complete multi-step tasks across connected apps.
* **Get the right model for each task:** Glean Model Hub provides access to Claude models. Glean Intelligence matches tasks to models based on performance and cost, while giving teams visibility into AI usage.
* **Keep AI within guardrails:** Glean enforces existing source permissions, and Glean Protect adds sensitive-content detection and AI security controls.
