Zoom Agentic Search for Claude gives you grounded, permission-aware responses using Zoom’s unified context layer. Instead of analyzing raw transcripts or chaining together multiple tools, you can ask questions directly within Claude and get a synthesized response using your latest meeting summaries, work conversations, documents, and connected data sources as context.

## What you can do

* **Ask in plain language:** Ask a natural-language question and get a synthesized response drawn from your meetings, notes, chat threads, and documents.
* **Surface what’s buried:** Find decisions, action items, and other work context buried deep in meetings, chats, and documents.
* **Search across sources:** Search your Zoom conversations and connected data sources in one place.
* **Permission-aware by design:** Retrieval respects your existing permissions and comes with admin controls.
