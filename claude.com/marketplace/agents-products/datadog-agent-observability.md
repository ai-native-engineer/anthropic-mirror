Datadog is an autonomous observability and security platform used by engineering and security teams to monitor applications, infrastructure, agents, and models in one place. Datadog provides out-of-the-box Anthropic and Claude Agent SDK integrations that automatically capture traces and performance telemetry, making Claude-powered agents easier to monitor and optimize. Everything runs in your Datadog environment, where your metrics, traces, logs, and other telemetry already live.

## What you can do

* **Keep AI systems reliable without adding another silo:** Bring AI builders into the same platform SRE and DevOps teams already use.
* **Ship reliable AI agents faster:** Build, validate, and benchmark prompts, models, and configurations with structured experiments and evaluation datasets generated from production traces.
* **Operate agents confidently in production:** Trace every workflow step and monitor output quality, latency, token usage, and errors to catch regressions, detect drift, and control costs.
* **Resolve issues with full-stack context:** Correlate agent telemetry with APM, logs, infrastructure, and RUM to pinpoint issues across prompts, models, tool calls, and downstream dependencies.
