BlackLine is the Agentic Financial Operations Platform for the Office of the CFO, helping finance and accounting teams connect data, automate complex processes, and orchestrate work across the financial ecosystem. Powered by Verity AI and Claude, BlackLine combines advanced AI capabilities with deep financial process expertise and built-in controls to drive greater efficiency, accuracy, and confidence across financial operations.

## What you can do

* **Unify financial data**: Connect financial information across ERPs, applications, and systems to establish a consistent foundation for AI-powered financial operations.
* **Orchestrate financial workflows**: Coordinate AI agents, people, and processes across complex financial operations, reducing manual effort while keeping finance teams in control.
* **Accelerate the financial close**: Automate reconciliations, transaction matching, consolidation, and other record-to-report activities to reduce manual effort, resolve exceptions faster, and improve financial accuracy.
* **Optimize invoice-to-cash operations**: Streamline collections, cash application, credit and risk management, and dispute resolution to improve working capital, accelerate cash flow, and reduce days sales outstanding.
* **Govern AI with confidence**: Maintain visibility, oversight, and control over AI-driven activities through built-in governance and human-in-the-loop processes that support trust and accountability.
