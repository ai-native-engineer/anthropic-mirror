Charlotte AI is CrowdStrike's agentic security analyst, delivering agentic capabilities across the Falcon platform. Used by SOC teams, threat hunters, and IT admins, it enables teams to automate workflows across the security lifecycle, and orchestrates response actions across their security stack; combining turnkey CrowdStrike agents, AgentWorks for custom agent development, and Agentic SOAR for cross-platform orchestration and natural language interactivity. Powered by Claude, Charlotte AI brings advanced reasoning to complex investigations — pairing autonomous intelligence with the battle-tested judgment of an experienced responder. Every answer stays grounded in your own telemetry, detections, and security policies.
