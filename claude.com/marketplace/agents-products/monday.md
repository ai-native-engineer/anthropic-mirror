monday is the AI workspace for getting the big things done, used by PMO, IT, marketing, finance, and operations teams across the enterprise. With Claude models, monday’s agents work right where your work lives, so they already know your projects, owners, and deadlines. It all runs on your own docs, boards, meetings, and data, and you decide what AI can access, sign off on, and spend.

## What you can do

* **Run an unlimited workforce for any job.** Ready-made or custom agents join your team and start working right away. Assign and review them like any teammate.
* **Get a personal assistant for every task.** Sidekick knows what you’re working on and helps you get it done.
* **Build any business app in minutes.** monday vibe turns your ideas into live, working apps on your data.
* **Turn meetings into action.** AI Notetaker turns every meeting into notes and action items your team and agents can act on.
* **Automate any process, end to end.** Build an AI workflow from a simple description and decide where people step in, so you scale with full control.
