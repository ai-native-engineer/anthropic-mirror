Runway Creative is an AI platform for generating and editing video, images and audio, used by more than 65 million creators. At its center is Runway Agent, powered by Claude, which turns a brief into a plan, builds the assets and assembles the finished piece. It all works from your own footage, product shots and brand assets.

## What you can do

* **Create from a brief:** Describe your product and audience. Runway Agent finds the angle, then builds ads, product videos and social posts sized for every placement.
* **Edit by asking:** Want a punchier opening, a different voiceover or a new look? Tell Runway Agent and it revises the piece.
* **The right model for every task:** Runway Agent picks the best image, video and audio model for each job, from Runway and other leading labs, so you don’t have to.
* **Scale what works:** Runway Agent turns a winning ad into new hooks, formats and languages for every channel.
* **Finish what you start in Claude:** Export animations from Claude Motion to Runway to add generated footage, voiceover and music.
