Asana is the operating system for human-agent teams. Enterprises use it to run critical work across people, AI agents, and business systems, from plan to outcome. Claude powers Asana AI Teammates, so agents can reason through multi-step work inside your team’s workflows. Everything runs on the Asana Work Graph, so agents start with your team’s goals, projects, and history.

## What you can do

* **Ready-to-go AI Teammates:** Start with 30+ prebuilt AI Teammates for marketing, ops, and IT. They learn from your team’s feedback, so every workflow starts smarter than the last.
* **Human-agent handoffs:** Agents do the work and send decisions that need judgment to the person who owns them. When people finish a step, agents pick up the next one.
* **Asana Dash:** Surface priorities across meetings, emails, tasks, and conversations, so you know what needs your attention next.
* **AI automations:** Build intake, routing, approvals, and status updates in AI Studio, with no code required.
* **Built-in governance:** Every agent gets an identity, scoped permissions, spend limits, and an audit trail, so IT, Legal, and Finance can say yes.
