Give your team the power to build what they need with Claude, from internal team tools to customer apps, and then deploy them to Netlify, a flexible platform you can oversee. With everything teams need to build, iterate on, and deploy apps, you can go from prototype to production and beyond in one place.

## What you can do

* **Deploy from Claude to production**: Ship apps, sites and agents straight from Claude Code or Claude artifacts to a global edge network, with preview URLs on every change.
* **Give every team a secure, governed platform**: SSO, role-based access, audit logs and enterprise-grade security so AI-built projects meet the same bar as everything else you ship.
* **Add backend capabilities without infrastructure**: Serverless functions, edge functions, forms, identity and data storage come built in, so Claude-generated apps work end to end on day one.
* **Scale with enterprise support and SLAs**: Dedicated support, uptime guarantees and expert onboarding for teams moving from prototype to production at scale.
* **Connect to the tools you already use**: Integrates with GitHub, GitLab, major CMS, commerce and data platforms, and the Netlify MCP server, so Claude can build, deploy and manage sites inside your existing workflows.
