Connect Claude to Bloomberg Law MCP for access to a market-leading collection of federal and state court dockets. Search across cases, parties, courts, judges, filings, and keywords. Retrieve docket details and filing information; monitor matters with alerts and tracks; and surface litigation insights. Built for legal teams that need reliable docket research, case monitoring, and workflow integration powered by Bloomberg Law.
