More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://docs.chatbase.co/developer-guides/mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:support@chatbase.co)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.chatbase.co/legal/privacy)

Chatbase lets teams build and operate AI agents trained on their own business content, and run support end to end - chat widgets, help-desk tickets, and the sources those agents are trained on. This connector gives an MCP client direct access to a connected Chatbase account: list and inspect agents, create and update knowledge sources, update agent configuration, export conversations, and read, create, and update help-desk tickets and their messages.
