Give your AI agent a contract reader. Revalidata reads leases, mortgages, insurance policies, HOA documents, purchase agreements, construction contracts and loan modifications, and lists each deadline and critical date it finds: the date, what happens if it is missed, and the passage it was read from where one was captured. It can miss dates; how often is measured and published at https://revalidata.com/accuracy. Remote MCP server with OAuth sign-in. The first 3 contracts are free, then $0.50 each.
