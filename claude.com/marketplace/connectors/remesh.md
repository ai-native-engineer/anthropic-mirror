Remesh is a research platform for structured conversations with hundreds of real participants at once, combining open-ended responses with polls, rankings, and audience segmentation.

The Remesh connector securely links your Remesh workspace so you can work across the full research loop:

Design and launch. Turn a research objective into a discussion guide, build and refine questions, recruit targeted audiences, and schedule conversations.

Analyze and synthesize. Explore results across topics, sentiment, poll statistics, and audience segments. Pull verbatim responses and findings from one or many studies into summaries, briefs, and reports grounded in what participants actually said.

Access is permissioned through your Remesh account, so you only see the workspaces and conversations your account can access. Requires a Remesh subscription.
