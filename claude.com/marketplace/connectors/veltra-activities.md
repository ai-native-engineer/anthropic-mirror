Veltra Activities brings VELTRA's catalog of genuine local experiences into

your AI chat. Search and book tours, food experiences, and cultural

activities across Japan, Hawaii, Southeast Asia, and other destinations —

Tokyo food tours, Kyoto tea ceremonies, Mt. Fuji day trips, Honolulu luau

dinners, and more. Curated results include prices, ratings, durations, and

direct booking links on Veltra.com. The connector turns travel-planning

conversations into bookable plans without leaving the chat. Read-only and

unauthenticated; no account required to use.
