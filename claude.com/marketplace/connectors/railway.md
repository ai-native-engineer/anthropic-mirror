The Railway connector lets Claude work with your Railway workspaces directly.

Claude can take actions: create new projects, deploy services from your connected GitHub repositories, redeploy services, manage feature flags, and publish self-contained artifacts (a static page, a React component, a diagram) to a public URL in seconds via Railway Functions.

It can also list your projects, services, and deployments, check the live status of an environment, and pull build, deploy, and HTTP logs to help you debug production issues without leaving the conversation.

For complex, multi-step operations, the connector includes railway-agent, a conversational bridge to Railway's own infrastructure agent, which can inspect services, diagnose failures, and apply configuration changes on your behalf. Destructive actions are gated behind explicit confirmation, and everything is scoped to the projects and workspaces your Railway account can access.
