Merchkit is an AI-Native product catalog built for agentic commerce. Connect it to Claude and work on your live catalog in conversation - no exports, no spreadsheets, no switching tabs.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Search and inspect your catalog. Find products & data gaps by SKU, attribute value, or missing data across products, variants, images, vendors, categories.

• Enrich at scale. Create and update products and attribute values one at a time or in bulk, and let Claude fill the gaps that make products invisible to shoppers and AI agents.

• Audit channel readiness. See exactly which attributes a product is missing for Wayfair, Amazon, Google Shopping, or any channel you sell on - with the specific issues to fix.

• Manage your taxonomy & schema. Inspect and edit attribute definitions, acceptable values, AI generation instructions, and attribute classes.

• Track and audit changes to your product catalog across team members.

BUILT FOR SAFE AGENT OPERATION

Every write tool previews the exact change before it applies — nothing is written until you confirm. Permissions are scoped when you connect, so you can grant read-only access to an agent that should only inspect the catalog. Every tool is workspace-scoped and runs the same validation as the Merchkit API.

WHO IT'S FOR

Ecommerce teams at brands, distributors or retailers managing 500 to 5M SKUs across marketplaces, retail partners, and AI shopping surfaces.

REQUIREMENTS

A Merchkit account. Connect in one click with OAuth — there's no API key to create or paste, and you can revoke access anytime from Settings → API keys → Connected apps.

Full documentation: https://docs.merchkit.com/developers/mcp
