Control your Sonos system directly from Claude. Play music and radio from your connected music services, or your Sonos favorites and Sonos playlists. Group and ungroup rooms for synchronized multi-room listening, move the music from one room to another, and see what's playing anywhere in your home. Set volume and mute on a speaker or group.

All you need to enjoy Sonos, without leaving the conversation.
