Trussi AI connects your Trussi roofing CRM to Claude so your team can run the business in plain language.

Ask about your work:

- Find and open projects and customers, with full job detail (status, quotes, files, measurements)

- Check your sales pipeline, dashboard, and leaderboard

- Pull month-by-month revenue, AR/collections, and marketing-channel performance

- See calendar, labor, and delivery schedules for any day or date range

- Review profit analysis, worksheets (signed value / collected / balance due), and insurance supplements

- Read project and per-user activity timelines, and run your saved reports

Take action (with your permissions):

- Add comments, create calendar events and quotes, and move projects through the pipeline

Built for security:

- OAuth 2.0 sign-in with PKCE - Claude only sees data your account already allows

- Every request is account-scoped and capability-checked, exactly like the Trussi app

- Read and write are separate scopes you approve when connecting

Scoped automatically by role and branch, so reps, production, and owners each see the right data.
