Tixel is the safe, fair way to buy and sell tickets to live events — concerts, festivals, sports, theatre, and comedy. Every ticket is verified to prevent fraud, sellers are identity-checked, and resale prices are capped near face value so fans never overpay. Browse real-time availability, buy instantly, or join a waitlist for sold-out events. Trusted by fans worldwide and partnered with leading venues, festivals, and promoters across the US, UK, and Australia.
