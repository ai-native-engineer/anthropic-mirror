Run your event operations with RSVPify and Claude, end-to-end. This connector lets you build event websites, create registration forms, manage guests, seat guests, send campaigns, and generate reports without leaving the conversation.

You can use the RSVPify connector to:

• Build a branded event page: "Create a new event website for our Q1 leadership summit on March 12 in Chicago, brand-fetched from acme.com, with a two-question RSVP form."

• Manage the guest list: "Import the attached CSV of 972 guests into the Leadership Summit event. Tag anyone from Acme's exec team as VIP and seat them at the head table."

• Run a campaign to new registrants: "Draft a welcome email to everyone who registered in the last 48 hours and is traveling from the New York"

• Generate a custom report or wrap-up: "Give me a wrap-up for the Leadership Summit – attendance, top registration questions, dietary requirements, and revenue"

• Automate manual actions: "For every new registrant, tag them by company from their email domain, tag anyone whose title contains CEO, CFO, or Founder as C-Suite, tag dietary restrictions from the intake question, and flag anyone from our top 20 accounts as VIP."

Every action RSVPify's in-app Cue assistant can take is available here, so Claude can drive your event program end-to-end.

RSVPify is the event operations platform built for organizers who need simplicity, speed, and powerful automation across the entire event lifecycle.
