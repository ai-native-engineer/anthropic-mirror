More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://helpcenter.flourish.studio/hc/en-us/articles/16487012057999-Flourish-MCP-connector-an-intro)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:support@flourish.studio)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://flourish.studio/privacy/)

Flourish Connector turns Claude into a chart-making assistant for real Flourish projects. Give it data and describe the insight you want to highlight or the story you want to tell. It chooses a suitable template, prepares the data, creates the visualization in your Flourish account, and gives you an edit link. You stay in control of the final polish and publishing in Flourish. No screenshots or throwaway code. Just interactive, editable charts with the access, collaboration, branding, and publishing features your plan includes.

## Tools

* flourish\_list\_templates
* flourish\_list\_visualisations
* flourish\_get\_template\_details
* flourish\_get\_visualisation\_data
* flourish\_get\_visualisation\_settings
* flourish\_create\_visualisation
* flourish\_duplicate\_visualisation
* flourish\_update\_visualisation\_data\_bindings
* flourish\_update\_visualisation\_data
* flourish\_update\_visualisation\_settings

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
