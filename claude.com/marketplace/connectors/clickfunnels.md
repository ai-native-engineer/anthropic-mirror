Connect Claude to your ClickFunnels account to work with your business data conversationally. Ask Claude to look up contacts, review store orders, check funnel performance, update course content, manage form submissions, draft email campaigns, and more — across every major area of ClickFunnels, including funnels, contacts, courses, emails, store, forms, blogs, sites, and workflows.

The connector uses a discovery-based design: Claude first explores what's available in a category, then reads, creates, or updates records on your behalf. Every write action is performed through the ClickFunnels V2 API with your permissions.

Access and security: When connecting, you choose exactly which workspaces Claude can access and grant per-category read/write permissions. Access is scoped to those grants on every request — Claude can never reach workspaces you didn't authorize. Connections are made via OAuth; no passwords or API keys are shared with Claude.

Requirements: A ClickFunnels account with team admin access is required to connect.

* read\_action

* update
