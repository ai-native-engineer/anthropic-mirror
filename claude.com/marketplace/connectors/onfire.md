Onfire is an AI revenue-intelligence platform for technical-buyer GTM. Its Account Intelligence Graph blends your first-party data (CRM, trial signups) with Onfire's proprietary external data: the public footprint of 50M+ engineers, community and event activity, granular technographics, and real-time buying signals (including users posting anonymously), refined by AI to surface ICP-fit accounts and high-intent buyers. This connector brings that into Claude: run AI prospecting to rank buyers and champions, enrich contacts with verified emails and phones, build account research briefs and source accounts in your territory, and analyze community sentiment and intent.
