More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://www.healthdata.org/explore-gbd-2023-study-estimates-claude)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:engage@healthdata.org)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.healthdata.org/gbd-mcp-privacy-statement)

Ask Claude questions about the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation's Global Burden of Disease (GBD) study — the largest and most comprehensive effort to quantify health loss across places and over time — for any country, region, cause, or risk factor. The connector retrieves results directly from the GBD database; it does not model or generate new values.

Query deaths, DALYs, YLLs, and YLDs; risk factor exposures and attributable burden; life expectancy, healthy life expectancy (HALE), child mortality, population, and fertility; and detailed injury, impairment, and pathogen data — with Socio-demographic Index (SDI) context for development comparisons.

Beyond direct queries, five ready-made profiles generate narrative overviews with tables, charts, and maps: Location, Location Comparison, Disease, Sub-cause Comparison, and Risk-Outcome. Ask naturally, then narrow, broaden, or challenge the results through follow-up — no special commands required.

Example questions:

• What are the top causes of death in Nigeria?

• How has life expectancy in China changed over time?

• Which risk factors drive the most cardiovascular disease in Latin America?

• Compare child mortality across India's states.

Think of it as a fast, conversational front-end for exploring GBD data — complementing IHME's GBD Compare and Results Tool rather than replacing them.
