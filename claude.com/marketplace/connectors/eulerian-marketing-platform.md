Eulerian Marketing Platform MCP connects Claude directly to your Eulerian Technologies account, giving you conversational access to the full suite of Eulerian's marketing analytics, attribution, and activation tools.

Analytics & Reporting

Query real-time and batch analytics data across any dimension or metric. Run flat aggregate reports, explore data-mining exports (sales, shopping carts, outbound clicks, search engine data), schedule automated reports, and generate AI-powered insights from your traffic and conversion data.

Attribution

Access multi-touch attribution models, marketing mix modeling (MMM) with saturation curves, incrementality testing, view-augmented attribution, and full customer journey analysis. Manage media categories and real-time attribution model configurations.

Audiences

Build, search, and manage audience segments and external segments, configure the audience engine, run saved queries, sync partner segments, and manage server-side user data workflows.

Activation

Deploy and monitor server-to-server (S2S) and tag management system (TMS) activations, clone and estimate campaigns, and troubleshoot activation errors.

Collect & Tracking Configuration

Manage the full tracking setup: campaigns, publishers, products, goals, funnels, profiles, sections, labels, custom parameters, SEM accounts/campaigns/creatives, websites, and tracking hostnames.

Consent & Cookie Management (CMP)

Configure cookie consent rules, TCF v2 vendors, consent categories, and query the Cookiepedia database for vendor and tool information.

Data Warehouse (EDW)

Launch, schedule, and monitor EDW jobs and templated data exports.

Documentation

Ask natural-language questions against Eulerian's official documentation to get guidance on report queries and platform usage.
