Control Plane runs containerized apps across AWS, GCP, Azure, Oracle Cloud, and your own hardware as one platform. Describe an app and Claude writes, builds, and deploys it to a live HTTPS URL, or builds one from your GitHub or GitLab repository. For an app that keeps records, files, or sign-ins, it plans the database, storage, and sign-in with you before writing any code. Give an app a custom domain, add PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, MongoDB, or Redis, and let it autoscale. Diagnose failures from logs, events, metrics, and traces, then restart, roll back, or promote a workload, and compare used against reserved CPU and memory to find the over-provisioned workloads inflating your cloud bill. Workloads reach AWS, GCP, and Azure through your registered cloud accounts without stored keys, and you can install services from the Template Catalog and export your setup to Terraform. The connector acts only on the organizations you grant when you connect, and secret values are generated by Control Plane or entered by you in the Control Plane Console, never in the chat.
