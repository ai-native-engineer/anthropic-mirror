The Attentive MCP server connects your Attentive account to Claude and other AI assistants. Marketers can build, launch, and analyze messaging programs by describing what they want in plain language. Available channels and features depend on your Attentive account and enabled capabilities.

If you manage more than one account, you can switch between the Attentive accounts you have access to. The MCP server instructs AI assistants to request your confirmation before creating or changing content, scheduling sends, or changing live programs.

Key features:

Campaigns: Create campaigns and message groups, write SMS, RCS, push, and email content, and schedule, pause, or resume sends.

Journeys: Build automated journeys from templates or supported triggers. Add and edit steps and branches, and publish, pause, or archive journeys.

Segments: Create and update audience segments from your account's subscriber and product attributes, and check segment sizes before you send.

Email design: Create and edit email templates, apply brand kit styles and fonts, and use your existing brand assets.

Copy generation: Draft message copy that follows your brand voice settings or guidance you provide, and create tracked short links.

Reporting: Query campaign and journey performance using the metrics available for your account.
