TextToQuant turns a plain English trading idea into a real backtest on real market data. Describe a strategy in words asset, timeframe, entry and exit rules, stops and targets and it parses it into exact rules, runs the backtest, and returns a graded report with honesty flags (zero fees, missing stops, thin sample) so a result is never over-trusted.

What you can do from Claude:

• Run and rerun backtests across crypto, forex and equities, then see the full report: price chart with trade markers, equity and drawdown, trade ledger, Monte Carlo and the metrics board.

• Stress test a strategy: parameter sweeps, walk-forward tests, multi-asset checks and an overfitting verdict (Deflated Sharpe, PBO).

• See where the edge lives: performance by market regime and by indicator context.

• Compare strategy variants side by side and build shared-capital multi-asset portfolios.

• Author custom indicators in Pine Script, JavaScript or Python and use them in strategies.

• Scan the crypto market, set alerts, export a strategy to Pine Script, and share any run as a public interactive report.

Research and education only: nothing is traded and nothing is sold inside Claude. Runs bill from the same plan and credits as your TextToQuant account.
