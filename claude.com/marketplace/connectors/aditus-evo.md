ADITUS Evo is an intelligent MCP connector that transforms complex event data into actionable insights. By seamlessly connecting AI assistants with the ADITUS Evo platform, it enables natural-language access to event operations, attendee analytics, ticketing, access control, and real-time business intelligence. Designed for speed, precision, and scalability, the connector empowers users to explore data, automate workflows, and make informed decisions through simple conversational interactions—turning complexity into clarity.
