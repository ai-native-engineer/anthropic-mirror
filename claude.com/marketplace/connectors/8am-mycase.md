The 8am MyCase MCP is 8am's official connector bringing MyCase practice management data into Claude. Attorneys and staff can ask Claude in plain language to look up a matter, review time entries and billing, check tasks and deadlines, review leads, or read client portal messages, with every read carried out directly against MyCase under that user's existing MyCase permissions.
