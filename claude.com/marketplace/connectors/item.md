Browse, summarize, update, and analyze customer data directly from Claude. Connect live context from contacts, companies, deals, lists, saved views, notes, and relationship history so your assistant can answer account questions, find the right records, and help move work forward without jumping between tabs. Turn scattered CRM data into usable context for research, planning, follow-ups, and customer-facing work.
