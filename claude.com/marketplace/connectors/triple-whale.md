Connect Claude to Triple Whale and ask questions about your business across every channel in plain language. Pull KPIs, compare channels, break down performance, and understand what changed and what's driving results, all without writing SQL or switching dashboards. Every answer is grounded in your Triple Whale data and scoped to your store, timezone, and currency, so Claude works from the same source of truth your team already trusts.

* explain-metric-change
* find-sql-examples
* get-available-tables
* get-date-range
* get-shop-info
* get-summary-kpis
* get-table-schemas
