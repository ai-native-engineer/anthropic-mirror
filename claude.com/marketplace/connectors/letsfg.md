The cheapest ticket is not always the cheapest trip, and the same hotel room is not the same price everywhere. LetsFG checks before you pay, then books your flight or your hotel for you.

Hotels: the same room, for less. In our last check of 15 well-known hotels in Paris and New York against a major booking site (same dates, same guests, taxes included), LetsFG was cheaper on all 15, typically by 20%. Photos, reviews, breakfast, free cancellation and the total you pay.

Flights: ask Claude in plain words. LetsFG then:

1. Searches every airline in the world, low-cost carriers included, plus the major booking sites and travel agencies.

2. Opens the map: every airport in both cities, and split tickets when two one-ways beat a return.

3. Prices the whole trip: bags, airport transfers and Uber into the city, so a cheap flight to the wrong airport does not fool you.

4. Checks the flight's history: its on-time record over its last 14 departures and how often it is cancelled.

5. Hands you three picks instead of three hundred: best overall, most convenient, cheapest, each with the reason.

Booking: you press Approve in one e-mail, and a LetsFG booking agent buys the ticket or room in your name and sends the confirmation. Flights and hotels are separate bookings, not a package: after you book one, the other can be booked 24 hours later. LetsFG tracks delays and cancellations, sends rebooking options and checks your compensation.

Cheap flights, plane tickets, airfare, hotel deals, business class, refundable fares, flexible dates, direct flights, last-minute trips, city breaks.

Your money:

- The price shown is the total you pay.

- Nothing is booked without your Approve click in an e-mail from LetsFG.

- Your card is charged only once the booking is confirmed. If a price changes, LetsFG asks first.

- Payment runs on letsfg.co, outside the chat. Neither Claude nor LetsFG sees your card details.

Works in 15 languages: tanie loty, vuelos baratos, günstige Flüge, vols pas chers.
