MyChatBot gives your business an AI sales agent that talks to customers on your website, Telegram, WhatsApp, and Instagram — answering questions, recommending products, capturing leads, and taking orders around the clock.

This connector lets you build and run that agent from your Claude chat, in plain English, without opening a dashboard.

Set it up. Point Claude at your website and it drafts the agent — name, greeting, and how it should sell — then shows you one card to approve. Nothing is created until you say so. Add a chat widget to your site, or connect Telegram, WhatsApp, and Instagram with one link each.

Teach it your business. Tell Claude your shipping rules and returns policy and it writes the FAQ your agent quotes from. Point it at a product feed and it imports the whole catalog — indexed, searchable, and filterable by the attributes your customers actually ask about.

Sell, don't just answer. Turn on order taking and the agent collects what it needs — name, phone, delivery address — and records the order in conversation, instead of handing the customer to a human.

Run it day to day. Ask how the shop did this week and get orders, revenue, and the channels they came from. Read any customer conversation, see your newest leads, and check what your agent has learned.

Reach out. Send one customer a message on their channel, or launch an outreach campaign to a whole audience — Claude previews exactly who it will reach and waits for your approval before anything sends.

Improve it safely. Rehearse changes in a private test chat, and approve every change to your agent's instructions on a before/after card before a customer ever sees it.

You need a MyChatBot account; connecting from Claude signs you in and can create one on the spot. No API keys.
