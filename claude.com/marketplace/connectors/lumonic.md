Lumonic connects Claude to permission-aware portfolio monitoring and reporting data. Use it to discover entities, fields, dimensions, and metrics; build portfolio tables; summarize reporting status; identify missing submissions; validate LX expressions; generate refreshable Excel formulas; and inspect source context or traceback links. Claude can only access the Lumonic workspaces and data available to the authenticated user.
