Retrieves and visualises data from The Economist’s Trump tracker. Information is available on the president's popularity at a national and state level, as well as on various issues, including the economy, health care and immigration. Answers can be broken down by age, education, race and sex. The tool returns the latest figures and analyses trends where time-series data are available.
