More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://help.dropbox.com/integrations/connect-docsend-mcp-server)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://help.docsend.com/hc/en-us/requests/new)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.docsend.com/privacy-policy/)

Connect DocSend to Claude to manage document workflows, analyze engagement, and take action using natural language.

Use the DocSend connector to support workflows such as:

- M&A and due diligence: Create and manage data rooms, add content, grant or update access, create secure links, review buyer activity, and identify the most engaged parties.

- Fundraising: Share pitch decks and fundraising materials, track investor engagement, identify interested investors, and prepare personalized follow-ups.

- Sales: Create links for proposals and presentations, manage prospect access, understand how buyers are engaging, and prioritize your next steps.

- Financial services: Manage sensitive client and stakeholder workflows, control access to shared materials, monitor engagement, and quickly answer questions about your DocSend data.

The connector lets Claude work with your authorized DocSend Spaces, content, links, permissions, and engagement data while respecting your existing access controls.
