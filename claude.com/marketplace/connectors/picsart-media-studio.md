More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://picsart.github.io/picsart-mcp-cli-docs/guide/media-studio/)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://support.picsart.com)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://picsart.com/privacy-policy/)

Create, edit, and render videos and images in Claude. Picsart Media Studio turns plain-language requests into editable scenes using your clips and photos, Picsart Drive files, text, and built-in templates, looks, and transitions. Results can be saved to your Picsart Drive.

Use Picsart Media Studio to:

Start from a Template:

"Use the end-card template to make a spring sale outro with a 'Shop now' button."

Use Your Own Media:

"Upload a photo or pick one from my Picsart Drive and put it in a heart-shaped frame."

Edit and Assemble Videos:

"Join these three clips into a 9:16 video with a transition on every cut."

Add Captions:

"Transcribe this interview and add captions that highlight each word as it's spoken."

Reframe Videos:

"Reframe this video to 9:16, following the speaker instead of center-cropping."

Preview and Export:

"Show me a few frames, then render it as an MP4 and a GIF."

Every result is an editable scene you can refine and render again.
