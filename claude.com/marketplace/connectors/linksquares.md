LinkSquares brings contract lifecycle management and contract intelligence into Claude. Create agreements, manage legal requests, search your contract repository, and take action on legal work without switching applications. Purpose-built for legal and business teams, LinkSquares helps you move work forward faster with trusted contract data and AI-powered workflows, directly within Claude.
