Windsor.ai for Social Media is a focused workspace for organic social. It connects your social accounts, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile, so you can ask questions in plain language: compare reach, engagement, and follower growth across channels; and see which posts performed best. Where the platform supports it, you can also publish posts (for example on Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile), always after you confirm. It connects only your social sources, so the tools stay on task. Windsor.ai is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms it connects to.
