More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://developer.print.com/page/mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:info@print.com)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.print.com/en/privacy-at-print-com/)

Print.com brings its full online-print platform into Claude. Get inspired by the product catalogue, configure any product down to its finishing options, and get real-time pricing and shipping estimates all through natural conversation. Whether you're a print reseller pricing a job, a franchise business ordering a run of flyers, or a creative professional ordering a banner, the connector turns "what would this cost and when could it arrive?" into an answer you can act on in a couple of messages.

Key features

- Get inspired. Explore the full product catalogue and ready-made presets to discover what's possible and find the right starting point for your project.

- Full catalogue access. Browse every product available to your account and drill into details, including the complete set of configuration options for each SKU.

- Accessories & finishing. Discover and add relevant add-ons to any product.

- Real-time pricing. Get an exact price for any specific product configuration, so quotes are accurate before you commit.

- Shipping estimates. See available shipping options with carrier, service, price, and delivery date.

- Shareable product links. Generate a shareable selector URL for a configured product the same link the website uses to pass to a colleague or customer.

- Manage orders. Ask details about order details or information about your order history at Print.com.
