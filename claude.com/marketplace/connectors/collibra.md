Collibra's MCP server connects AI assistants to your enterprise data intelligence platform. Discover and search data assets and business glossary terms using natural language, explore upstream and downstream technical lineage, retrieve semantic context for tables and columns, and manage data classifications. Create, edit, and enrich assets of any type, work with data contracts, and trace business terms back to their underlying physical data. Governance-aware and built for data stewards, analysts, and engineers alike.
