Jetpost is a shared inbox for you, your team, and your AI. Send messages, notes, and docs to teammates straight from Claude, email anyone (even people who aren't on Jetpost yet), and keep memory and context in sync across Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, OpenClaw, Hermes, Muse, Devin, Gemini, Cursor, and the other AI tools you use.

What you can do with Jetpost in Claude:

- Message your team: send a note, update, or summary to one teammate or the whole team without leaving the chat. It lands in their Jetpost inbox and they get notified.

- Send email: share any note by email, so clients, partners, and new hires can read it without an account.

- Share notes and docs: turn a conversation into a clean, formatted doc with tables, checklists, and collapsible sections, then share it with a link or invite people to comment.

- Collaborate: teammates reply, comment, and edit in the same note. Claude can read the replies and pick up where the thread left off.

- Hand off work: start a task in Claude, hand it off to a teammate or to another AI tool, and nothing gets lost. Every note carries the full context.

- Shared AI memory: decisions, plans, and research live in one place that any AI assistant can read, so you stop re-explaining your project in every new chat.

- Search your knowledge base: find past notes, decisions, and conversations across your workspace.

- Workspaces and permissions: organize notes by team, control who can view, comment, or edit, and see who wrote what (you, a teammate, or an agent).

Great for: daily standups and status updates, meeting notes, project briefs, research write-ups, client updates, team announcements, and AI handoffs between Claude, ChatGPT, and other assistants.

Try asking Claude:

- "Send this plan to my team on Jetpost."

- "Email this summary to sarah@company.com."

- "Save what we decided today so I can pick it up in ChatGPT."

- "What did my team say about the launch note?"
