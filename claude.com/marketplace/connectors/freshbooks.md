FreshBooks — Ask Claude how your business is really doing

Skip the spreadsheets and log-ins. With the FreshBooks connector, you can ask Claude about your business finances in plain language — and get clear, accurate answers pulled directly from your FreshBooks account.

Know Your Profitability, On Demand

Ask Claude how profitable your business was last month, last quarter, or all year. Get a clear breakdown of your revenue, expenses, and profit — no reports to run, no dashboards to dig through.

Understand Where Your Money Goes

Ask Claude to break your expenses down by category so you can see what's driving your costs and spot trends before they become problems.

Track Revenue and Cash Position

Get a real-time read on money coming in and going out. Ask Claude to compare periods, flag slow months, or summarize your overall cash position.

Your Data, Just Easier to Understand

The FreshBooks connector is read-only — it answers questions about your business, it doesn't make changes on your behalf. Your financial data stays exactly where it is; Claude just helps you make sense of it faster.
