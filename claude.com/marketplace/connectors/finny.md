More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://finnyai.notion.site/Connecting-FINNY-to-Claude-3fb199582de480fab4bbe681cd442c37)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:support@finny.com)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://finny.com/privacy-policy)

The FINNY connector lets FINNY users work with their FINNY account by asking in plain language. With the connector, you can:

\* See your campaigns, newest first, with each one’s status, channels, and launch date. You can also search campaigns by name.

\* Review a campaign’s results as FINNY’s campaign page shows them: people contacted or scheduled, plus messages sent, opens, replies, clicks, and social connections, with rates by channel and by step.

\* Upload prospects to FINNY. Claude previews the list first and shows which rows FINNY can use and which it can’t. Nothing is uploaded until you approve.

\* Follow the progress of your recent uploads.

\* Search the FINNY help center and read full articles.

## Tools

* get\_campaign\_performance
* get\_help\_article
* get\_upload\_status
* list\_campaigns
* preview\_prospect\_upload
* search\_help\_center
* upload\_prospects
