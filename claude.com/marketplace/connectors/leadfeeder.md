Leadfeeder shows you which companies are visiting your website and how well they fit your ideal customer. Connect Leadfeeder to Claude and just ask: find the right-fit companies showing interest this week, see what they viewed, enrich them with the right contacts, build and tag lists, and check your usage — all in plain language. Start from the demand your website already creates, not a cold database. You sign in with your own Leadfeeder account and stay in control of what the assistant can do.

Leadfeeder provides business contact data from its B2B database, built from publicly available sources. The legal basis for processing this personal data is Leadfeeder's legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in providing and marketing our services to our users and clients. Individuals can request removal, object to processing, or opt out at https://privacycockpit.leadfeeder.com/ or by contacting privacy@leadfeeder.com. More information available on our Privacy Center: https://www.leadfeeder.com/privacy/privacy-center/
