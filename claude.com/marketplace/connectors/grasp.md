Grasp brings the most exhaustive private-market database into Claude — 100m+ companies, 500k+ buyers and 500k+ deals, with data straight from the source. Run deal research end to end: build target, buyer and transaction lists; refine with structured research; analyse financials and ownership; query and export exhaustive data tables; and pull company profiles or decision-maker contacts. Move from a broad investment thesis to final insights — surfacing the opportunities no one else can.
