Orshot is an API for automated image, PDF and video generation from templates. Design a template once in the Orshot studio (or via Claude), then render on-brand images, PDFs and videos from it with new data.

With the Orshot connector, Claude works across your whole template workflow:

- Create and refine studio templates from a brief, with interactive inline previews

- Render any template with your data: OG images, ad creatives, certificates, invoices, carousels, reels

- Render true video: per-element animations and transitions, soundtracks from your brand kit, and free motion previews before you spend a render

- Start instantly with ready-made utility templates: tweet screenshots, website captures, bar and comparison charts, app store screenshots, phone mockups, background removal and more, no design needed

- Resize one design to every social format in a single call with Smart Resize

- Batch render personalized variations, like certificates for a list of names

- Pull free stock photos and video straight into your designs

- Keep everything on brand with your brand kit: colors, fonts, images, videos and audio

- Organize templates in folders, share them with your team, and switch between client workspaces

- Mint signed dynamic URLs for OG images and embeds

- Draft, schedule and publish social posts, learn your best time to post from your own engagement data, and read back reach and engagement analytics

- Build and run automation workflows that keep producing content on a schedule

Rendering is deterministic template rendering: your template plus your data produces the same output every time. Every action happens in your own Orshot workspace, isolated to your account via OAuth.

Getting started takes a minute: connect, authorize with your Orshot account (100 free credits every month, no credit card needed) and ask Claude to list your templates or render your first image.
