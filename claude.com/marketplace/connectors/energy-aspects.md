Energy Aspects gives AI agents direct access to expert energy market intelligence — research reports, real-time analyst commentary, and timeseries data across oil, gas, power, and related markets.

Energy Aspects is an independent research firm trusted by traders, investors, and energy professionals worldwide. The connector draws on three content types: in-depth research publications with analysis and forecasts; EA Live posts with intraday market commentary from analysts; and a library of quantitative timeseries covering supply, demand, inventories, flows, and prices — including point-in-time queries against historical releases.

## Tools

* get\_ea\_live\_posts
* get\_insight\_and\_data
* get\_publications
* get\_timeseries\_data
