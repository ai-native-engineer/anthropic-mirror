Webex Suite MCP gives you secure access to your Webex meetings, messages, and Vidcast content right inside Claude, everything you need is available where you work.

Meetings - View your upcoming and past meetings, check who attended and their status, create or update a meeting, get meeting summaries, and access recordings and transcripts all through a simple ask.

"What meetings do I have this week?"

"Summarise my last team meeting."

"Schedule a 30-min call with my team on Friday at 2pm."

"Show me the transcripts from yesterday's product review."

Messaging - Search across all your Webex spaces and messages, read or send messages, create and manage spaces, and share or download files without opening Webex.

"Find all my unread messages for today”

"Create a new space called 'FY27 Planning’”

"What's the latest update in the launch-readiness space?"

Vidcast - Browse your own videos, find content shared with you, search across org-wide Vidcasts, and get transcripts or highlights from any video so you can get the key moments from any video.

"Show me videos that were shared with me in this week.”

"Get the highlights from the yesterday’s all-hands video."

"Find Vidcast videos about the new product onboarding."

All of this works securely through your existing Webex account, no new logins, no extra setup required.
