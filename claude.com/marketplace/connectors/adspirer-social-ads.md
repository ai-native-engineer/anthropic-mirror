More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://www.adspirer.com/docs/introduction)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:abhi@adspirer.com)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.adspirer.com/privacy)

Adspirer Social Ads is your paid social and social media advertising agent — an AI performance marketer for Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok Ads that works like a member of your team. It plans, launches (paused) and autonomously runs social advertising campaigns, with full campaign read and write access.

Create campaigns through conversation, not dashboards. Turn an objective like "more purchases under a $30 CPA" into well-structured campaigns, ad sets and ads — then keep working after launch. Adspirer builds and refines audience targeting with lookalike and custom audiences and retargeting, tests ad creative and catches creative fatigue before it costs you, writes and refreshes ad copy and primary text, manages bids and bid strategies, paces spend so budgets land where you want them, shifts money toward what's converting, and flags anything that needs a human call.

Competitor ads research is built in. Pull any brand's live Meta and TikTok ads from the public ad libraries, see what creative and angles are running, and brief new concepts against them.

Deep analytics and funnel diagnostics. Cross-platform reporting on ROAS, CPA, CPM, CPC, CTR, frequency and spend; conversion-funnel analysis from impression to purchase; and conversion-tracking audits across the Meta Pixel, Conversions API and TikTok Pixel so you know the numbers are real before you act on them.

Built for agencies as well as in-house teams. Manage multiple brands and client ad accounts from one place, switch between accounts in conversation, and run PPC and paid media reporting across your whole book.

You set the strategy and approve the moves — new campaigns start paused, so nothing goes live without you.
