Financial Datasets gives Claude access to structured, real-time stock market data for 27,000+ active and delisted U.S. tickers. Research companies and markets with real-time and historical prices, primary-source SEC filings, financial statements and metrics, earnings, operating KPIs, news, insider and institutional ownership, index fund holdings, interest rates, and stock screening.
