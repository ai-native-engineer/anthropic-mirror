More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://developers.webflow.com/mcp/v1.0.0/reference/overview)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://support.webflow.com/)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://webflow.com/legal/privacy)

Webflow is a visual platform for building and running professional websites, and Webflow MCP connects Claude directly to it. Describe what you want in plain language, and your agent makes the change in your actual site, working from the design system, content, and data already there.

What you can do:

- Design and build: Create responsive pages, build reusable components, and manage the variables that hold your colors, fonts, and spacing.

- Manage content at scale: Set up and restructure CMS collections, the databases behind repeating content like blog posts and case studies, then write and update items in bulk.

- Bring it to life: Add motion and interactivity to a page, then fine-tune the result yourself on a visual timeline.

- Analyze and measure: Ask about traffic, top pages, and engagement over time, and get the answer in conversation.

Control stays with you. Agents work inside the roles and permissions your workspace already has, they follow the Agent Instructions you write to tell them how your site should be built, and every action is recorded in the site activity log. Point an agent at a page branch, a private copy of a page, and your live site stays untouched while you work. Anything an agent builds opens in Webflow's visual editor, where you review and adjust it before publishing.

New to Webflow? You do not need to learn the product first. What your agent builds is a real website with production hosting and a content management system behind it, not a code export you are then responsible for maintaining. Open the visual editor whenever you want to take over by hand.
