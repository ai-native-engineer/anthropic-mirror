The Breezy MCP server connects Claude directly to your Breezy Hire instance to view and interact with position and candidate data. The integration enables users to complete a wide range of tasks using natural language, from viewing candidate and position data to emailing candidates and analyzing hiring pipelines and workflows. This conversational approach streamlines and simplifies recruiting functions across your hiring team.
