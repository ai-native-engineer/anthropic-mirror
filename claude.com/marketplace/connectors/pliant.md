Pliant is a corporate card and spend management platform. Query and manage your Pliant data directly in Claude, within your existing Pliant permissions.

Key features:

• Spending: Explore transactions, account activity, and trends.

• Receipts: Find receipts and get your receipt-inbox address.

• Transactions: Comment, categorize, assign, and split.

• Accounting: Maintain suppliers, projects, categories, and VAT rates.

• Cards: Request cards, change limits, lock or unlock, approve requests.

• Members and teams: Invite and update members, organize teams and budgets.

Security: Claude only accesses data permitted by your Pliant role. Changes require your approval, and card requests and limit changes follow your organization's approval rules. The connector cannot transfer money or initiate payments.
