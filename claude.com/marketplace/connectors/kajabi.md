The Kajabi connector lets Claude manage your creator business directly inside Kajabi: build courses and coaching products, draft email broadcasts and sequences, publish blog posts and landing pages, manage offers, search contacts and products, and update your site's theme content. Read tools (search\_contacts, list\_orders, get\_site\_summary) help you analyze your audience and revenue without leaving Claude.
