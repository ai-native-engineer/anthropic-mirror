More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://help.clio.com/hc/en-us/articles/57881711703451-Use-Clio-Work-in-Claude-and-Codex)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://support.clio.com/)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.clio.com/uk/privacy/spain/)

Bring Vincent's legal intelligence into Claude. Research the law, analyze dockets and develop case strategy with access to Clio Library and Clio Docket, with citations back to the underlying sources.

Research and reason. Search case law, legislation and commentary in Clio Library, and read the full text of any decision or statute. Hand harder questions to Vincent, Clio's legal AI agent. It plans its own research, reads the sources and returns a cited answer. Claude shows its progress and asks you before Vincent takes any step that needs your approval.

Analyze dockets. Review filings in US federal and state cases, build procedural timelines, and see how a judge or opposing counsel has handled similar matters.

Draft. Turn research into memos, letters, clauses and motions. Revise them in conversation and export them to Word or PDF.

Review documents at scale. Ask the same questions across a set of contracts or filings, and get a review table with one row per document and one column per question.

Check citations. Verify the authorities cited in a document you supply.

Work with your firm's records. Check a matter's status, parties and deadlines, find documents and notes, review tasks and the calendar, and log time in Clio Manage. Vincent asks before it creates or changes a record, and Claude puts that request to you.

Pick up where you left off. Continue earlier conversations instead of starting over.

Your matters and firm data stay securely governed in Clio. Claude reaches only what your Clio permissions allow. Requires Clio Work. Available features and jurisdictions depend on your plan.
