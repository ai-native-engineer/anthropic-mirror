Military and Veteran Family Assistant gives Claude a reviewed list of programs for military and veteran families. It can help with child care, schools and special education, jobs and training, food, kids' programs and more, and it points you to where to start and how to reach each program.

This connector is currently in pilot. It includes local resources for the Hampton Roads region of Virginia, along with programs that work from anywhere.

This connector was built in partnership with Blue Star Families: https://bluestarfam.org/

When you ask for this kind of help, Claude first loads notes written by the team that built this connector, and you can ask Claude what they say. It is free, needs no sign-in and only looks things up. It is not the VA or the military.
