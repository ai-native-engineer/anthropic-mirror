Macrobond Assist connects Claude to 290M+ licensed macroeconomic time series, enabling trusted data discovery, retrieval and analysis directly from AI workflows.

Describe what you want to investigate in plain language. Macrobond Assist finds the relevant data, shows you where it comes from, and charts it so you can check it is the right data before you use it.

Private by design.

Grounded in trusted macro intelligence.

Ready for institutional AI deployments.

* fetch\_all\_vintages
* fetch\_entities
* fetch\_nth\_release
* fetch\_observation\_history
* fetch\_series
* fetch\_vintage\_series
* get\_attribute\_information

* search\_entities
