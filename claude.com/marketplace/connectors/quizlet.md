Turn any prompt, file, or set of notes into flashcards — right inside Claude. Ask for a flashcard set on any topic and Quizlet handles the rest.

Cramming for an exam or doing a quick review between classes? Instantly generate flashcards to review within Claude, or open the full set in Quizlet for different study modes. Teaching a class? Review the flashcards then turn it into a free in-class or at-home activity in seconds.

## Tools

* generate-flashcards
* get-flashcards-generation-status
