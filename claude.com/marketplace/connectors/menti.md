Create interactive presentations for free with Menti. Engage your audience – online or in-person – with real-time polls, live quizzes, Q&As and word clouds.

Participants can join from their phone or laptop to answer questions, share ideas and contribute in the moment.

Turn an idea for your next meeting into a Menti in seconds. From town halls and workshops to lectures and classes, simply describe what you want to ask your audience and Menti will create the right interactive slides. Edit and reorder them as much as you like, then choose whether responses should be visible to everyone or kept private to reduce bias.

Turn presentations into conversations with Menti by Mentimeter.
