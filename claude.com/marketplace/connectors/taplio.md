Run your LinkedIn by talking to Claude. Schedule content, pull inspiration from 5M+ viral posts, and get your analytics as plain answers.

- "Turn my newsletter into a post and queue it for tomorrow morning."

- "Find three hooks that are working in my niche and draft each one."

- "How did my posts do this month, and what should I do next?"

Ask in plain language, and Taplio does the work for you. No tab switching, no copy and paste.

With Taplio tools, Claude \*\*researches posts that are genuinely performing in your niche\*\*, writes drafts that sound like you using your profile and preferences, attaches your images or video, queues posts for your best times, and reads your analytics back as plain answers instead of another dashboard to decode.

\*\*Four guided workflows are built in:\*\*

- \*\*Post now.\*\* From idea to queued draft in one exchange.

- \*\*Performance debrief.\*\* What worked this month, and what to do next.

- \*\*Build a series.\*\* Inspiration pulled from our 5M+ viral posts database.

- \*\*Plan your week.\*\* A full week of content, drafted and scheduled.

Media stays in the chat. Claude opens a panel showing the library you have already built, or a drop zone for a new file, and it lands straight on the draft.

It runs on your own Taplio account, connected to your LinkedIn. It is not a scraper driving a hidden browser. Nothing to install, nothing to host, no cookie or password shared.

Drafts can wait for you in Taplio, or just ask Claude to schedule them. \*\*Nothing reaches LinkedIn until you grant Claude permission to schedule or publish.\*\*

You will need a Taplio account on a plan that includes API access, with your LinkedIn connected.
