AnythingMCP turns the APIs your business already runs on into tools Claude can use — without writing a connector.

180+ ready-made connectors, one click each:

• German & European business software: weclapp, DATEV, easybill, Billbee, Xentral, Personio, Lexware, Shopware, ScopeVisio, Odoo, Bitrix24, deutsche Bahn

• E-commerce & marketplaces: Etsy, WooCommerce, Magento, Amazon Seller Central, eBay, Kaufland, Vinted, Mercado Libre

• Shipping: DHL, DPD, GLS, Sendcloud, Shipcloud

• Payments & finance: Mollie, GoCardless, Adyen, Wise, Stripe, Bundesbank, VIES VAT validation

• CRM & sales: Pipedrive, Zoho, Close, Attio, Apollo, HubSpot

• Messaging & social: Telegram, WhatsApp Business, Discord, Reddit, Pinterest, LINE

• Open data: Deutsche Bahn, Destatis, Handelsregister, OpenPLZ, Companies House, Nominatim, NINA

…and many more.

Or bring your own API. Paste an OpenAPI/Swagger URL, a WSDL, a GraphQL endpoint, a Postman collection or a database connection string and AnythingMCP generates the tools, schemas and auth. Works with REST, SOAP, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQL Server.

Try asking:

• "What is the 10-year Bund yield today?"

• "Is DE811569869 a valid EU VAT number?"

• "Show my open weclapp quotations older than two weeks."

• "Which Etsy listings sold last week?"

• "Any active civil-protection warnings in Germany right now?"

Your credentials stay yours: every call runs with the API keys or OAuth tokens you configured, inside your own workspace, with a per-tool audit log. During sign-in you choose exactly which MCP servers Claude may reach, and you can change or revoke that any time — no reconnect needed.

Self-hosted (AGPL) or cloud. EU-hosted in Frankfurt, GDPR-ready. Built in Freiburg, Germany.
