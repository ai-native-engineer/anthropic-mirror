Vasara gives Claude direct access to Lithuania's primary legal sources – with citations it can verify.

Search national legislation, EU regulations and directives, and case law from the Constitutional Court, Supreme Court, Supreme Administrative Court, the CJEU, and the ECtHR. Look up a decision by case number, search by topic, pull specific passages from long documents, or check guidance from state authorities and municipalities.

Read-only – doesn't create, edit, or submit anything. Requires a free or paid Vasara account.
