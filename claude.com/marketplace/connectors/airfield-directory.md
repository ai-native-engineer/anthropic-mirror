More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://airfield.directory/mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://github.com/thomaswitt/airfield-directory-issues/issues)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://airfield.directory/pages/privacy-policy)

Your AI co-pilot for flight planning. Search 40,000+ airfields worldwide by ICAO code, name, or city. Get live METAR/TAF weather with runway-specific crosswind analysis and hazard decoding. Compare landing fees and fuel prices from Aerops. Read community pilot reports with insider tips on transport, facilities, and local quirks. Perform geo searches. View live airfield webcams. Plan routes across regions with smart filtering by IFR capability, fuel type, runway length, and community ratings. Free, public, no account needed.
