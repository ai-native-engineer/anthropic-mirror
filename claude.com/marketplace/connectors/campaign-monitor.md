Work with your Campaign Monitor account in plain language. Ask how your sending is going, understand your audience, and draft your next campaign, all from a conversation.

Review performance

"Summarize the last four campaigns I sent and tell me what's working."

Search sent campaigns by name, subject or date. Pull opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes. Read the content that went out and see a screenshot of it.

Diagnose what changed

"Click through has been dropping. What's driving it?"

Compare campaigns across a period and read back subject lines, send dates and content to explain the pattern.

Audit before you send

"Audit my most recent draft against email best practices."

Checks subject line length, preview text, and link and image structure, and flags what looks risky. Changes nothing.

Build a segment with the numbers in front of you

"Find subscribers who haven't engaged in 90 or more days and suggest how to split them."

Lists your subscriber lists, shows which segmentation features they support, and previews the rules with an estimated size before anything is saved.

Draft in your own brand

"Draft a re-engagement campaign for subscribers inactive 90 or more days."

Creates a draft, pulls brand colors and fonts from your website, adds text, image, button and social blocks, then sets the subject and preview text. The draft lands in your account for you to review and send. You stay in control. There is no tool for sending, importing subscribers, changing billing, or exporting subscriber personal data, so there is no way to ask for them. Writes are limited to drafts and segments, and everything is scoped to your existing Campaign Monitor permissions. To connect, an administrator enables the connection under Settings, API and Integrations, then you sign in with your normal Campaign Monitor credentials. No API keys or passwords are shared with the AI Assistant.
