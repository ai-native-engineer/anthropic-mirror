More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://tracyinterview.ai/help/claude)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://tracyinterview.ai/support)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://qode.world/privacy-policy)

Tracy AI Interviewer lets you set up and run one-to-one video interviews from the chat. Tracy, Qode's AI interviewer, holds a two-way, natural conversation with each participant; you decide what happens next.

Tell Tracy AI Interviewer who you are interviewing and what you want to find out. It suggests a title, 5 to 8 questions and the fields each result should report, using one of eleven templates (first-round hiring, founder, user research, customer feedback and more) or a blank interview. You review and edit the questions in the chat, then save the interview.

When you are ready, invite participants by email or turn on an open link you can post anywhere. Tracy AI Interviewer always shows you a preview of who will receive what before it sends anything, and acts only after you confirm. Each participant gets a private link by email from tracy@qode.world.

Ask for results at any time. For each participant you see whether they completed the interview, and their result: a summary of their answers against the fields you chose. Tracy AI Interviewer does not score or rank people, and it never makes a decision about a participant. Review every result yourself before you act on it.

You need a Tracy AI Interviewer account; signing in from Claude the first time creates one. Tracy AI Interviewer reads and changes only your own interviews.

Every invitation and interview starts with a fixed notice that the participant is speaking with an AI, and the host cannot remove it.
