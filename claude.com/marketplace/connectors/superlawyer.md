More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://lawcompany.notion.site/MCP-3dbd75a82a3080bfb968fc580a7afde5)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:superlawyer@lawcompany.co.kr)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://lawcompany.notion.site/Privacy-Policy-3d5d75a82a3080c0ac93d6cfa44e6662)

Put SuperLawyer’s expertise in Korean law to work in your Claude conversations. The SuperLawyer MCP server answers questions on Korean statutes and case law with cited sources, from applicable provisions and their interpretation to similar Supreme Court and lower court precedents and counterarguments to opposing claims. It also supports analysis over documents in your case knowledge bases, such as case records, documentary evidence, and briefs, and lets you list the cases in your workspace so you can pick the one to ask about. Built by Law&Company; a SuperLawyer account is required.
