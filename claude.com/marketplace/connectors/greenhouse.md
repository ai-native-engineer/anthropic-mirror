More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/52090956642075-Greenhouse-MCP)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://support.greenhouse.io)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.greenhouse.com/ai-addendum)

The official Greenhouse MCP server connects Claude to your entire Greenhouse recruiting workspace, with more than 150 tools to search, analyze and update your hiring data in plain language.

Candidates and prospects

Search and update candidates, track education and employment history, manage tags and attachments, add notes, merge duplicates, and work with prospect pools.

Applications and pipeline

See every application by job and stage, move candidates forward, reject or unreject with the right reason, convert prospects, and record hires.

Jobs and job posts

Create and update jobs, openings, hiring teams and job posts, including post locations, and review each job's interview plan and job boards.

Interviews and scorecards

Schedule and update interviews, see interviewers and interview kits, and read or create scorecards and their answers.

Offers and approvals

Create and update offers, run approval flows, and manage approver groups.

Organization setup

Manage users, job permissions, departments, offices and custom fields, and look up roles, sources, referrers, email templates, close and rejection reasons, and pay inputs.

Reporting and compliance

Pull EEOC and demographic data, AI opt-out statuses and rejection details for reporting.

Put it to work: "Which candidates for the backend engineer role have been in Onsite for more than two weeks?", "Summarize the scorecards for Priya's final round", or "Create an opening for the new Design Lead role in our London office."
