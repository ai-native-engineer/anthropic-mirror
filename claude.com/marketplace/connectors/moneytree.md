Connect Moneytree to Claude to bring all your finances into one place, ready to explore by asking.

- Track spending and save: See exactly where your money goes across every account, and cut back on what you don't need.

- Plan ahead with confidence: Know whether you're on track to stay financially healthy, and prepare for the milestones that matter, from marriage to kids to retirement.

- Make the most of your investments: See how your portfolio is performing, then explore how rebalancing could change your risk and return.

- Keep sight of the whole picture: Never miss the small things, like a subscription that quietly gets more expensive over time.
