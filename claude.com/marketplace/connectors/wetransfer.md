Send files straight from your conversation with WeTransfer.

Connect your WeTransfer account and share any file from your chat — documents, images, exports, generated assets — without leaving the conversation. Just ask, and the connector uploads the files to WeTransfer and returns a clean, shareable download link. Prefer to send it directly? Add recipients and WeTransfer will email the transfer for you, sent from your own account.

What you can do

- Share one or many files at once and get a ready-to-share download link

- Email a transfer to one or more recipients, with an optional title and message

- Send large files without bloating the conversation

How it works

Every transfer is created in your own WeTransfer account, so it appears in your transfer history and follows your plan's limits and expiry. You sign in securely with your WeTransfer account — the assistant only ever gets permission to send files on your behalf, and never sees your WeTransfer password. You can disconnect at any time to revoke access.
