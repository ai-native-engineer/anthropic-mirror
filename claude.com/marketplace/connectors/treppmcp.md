Trepp brings four decades of trusted commercial real estate and structured finance intelligence to your AI.

The Trepp MCP connector is a single gateway to Trepp's data, knowledge, and analytics — the same intelligence relied on by lenders, investors, servicers, rating agencies, and regulators. Rather than approximating, your AI responds with sourced, institutional-grade facts and reasoning grounded in datasets Trepp independently sources and maintains.

Data. Authoritative coverage across commercial properties, loans, CMBS deals, and bonds. Resolve a property by address, screen populations by geography, property type, performance, vintage, or deal structure, and drill into financials, occupancy, leases, loan terms, servicing and watchlist status, collateral exposure, and bond-level detail.

Knowledge. Trepp's research, market commentary, and methodology — the context behind the numbers — so your AI can interpret data the way the market does and point back to Trepp's published perspective.

Analytics. Benchmarking, comparables, market aggregates, risk and performance measures, and cash flow analysis — exposed as callable capabilities your agent can compose into real analytical workflows, not static reports.

Built to grow. Every tool shares a consistent schema and common identifiers that link property, loan, and securitization data together, so multi-step workflows compose naturally and results reconcile across domains. Trepp is continuously extending the connector across all three layers, and new tools plug into the same framework — the workflows you build today carry forward as coverage deepens.
