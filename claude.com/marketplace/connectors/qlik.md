Connect to your Qlik Cloud tenant to work with trusted, governed data and analytics.

Search and explore apps, datasets, and data products; inspect schemas, profiles, and trust scores; query and filter data with natural language; and build dashboards by creating sheets, charts, and KPIs. Manage governed assets like glossary terms, master dimensions and measures, and trace lineage end-to-end.

Qlik MCP combines Claude's reasoning with Qlik's analytics engine so decisions are grounded in your organization's approved data.
