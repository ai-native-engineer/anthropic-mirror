NOVASOL helps travelers discover holiday homes, holiday apartments, and vacation destinations across Europe.

Search thousands of bookable holiday properties across 18 European countries, all within a conversation.

Tell claude where you want to go, when, how many guests, your budget, and any must-have features, and it will surface a ranked carousel of matching homes with names, locations, prices, review scores, bedroom counts, and pet policies. Refine on the fly: change dates, shift your destination, tighten your budget, or add amenities and the results update instantly. Standout properties are highlighted so you can go straight to the card to see photos, full amenity lists, and booking options. This app covers self-catering holiday rental search only. It does not cover hotels, flights, car hire, package holidays, city apartments for short-term urban stays, aggregate price statistics, or any booking or payment action.
