Strand AI builds AI models that predict missing biological readouts from the data researchers already have. The first capability available through this connector is Lattice, which predicts spatially resolved protein-marker channels (virtual multiplex immunofluorescence) from routine H&E histology slides, so teams can recover spatial proteomics from archival tissue without extra staining or sample consumption.

In Claude, researchers can upload a de-identified whole-slide image, browse samples and available markers, estimate credits before submitting, run or cancel an inference job, request cell segmentation, and download OME-Zarr or OME-TIFF results.

OAuth binds each connection to one Strand AI user and organization. Reads, marker discovery, segmentation, and dry-run estimates are credit-free; a real submission reserves the displayed estimate. Slide and result bytes transfer directly between the client and Strand AI-managed storage. Slides must be de-identified. Research use only. Not for clinical or diagnostic procedures.
