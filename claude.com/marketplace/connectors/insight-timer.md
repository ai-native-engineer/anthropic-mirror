Used by 32 million people worldwide, Insight Timer is home to the world's largest free library of meditation, sleep tracks, breathwork, music, and spiritual talks. Want to wind down before bed, calm pre-meeting nerves, or find a Yoga Nidra session for tonight? Just ask and Claude will surface the right tracks, teachers, or talks. With Insight Timer in Claude you can: ask for guided meditations, breathwork, ambient soundscapes, or dharma talks and play them in Insight Timer. Find a teacher you love and hear more from them. Refine by duration, language, voice, or simply ask for "more like this."
