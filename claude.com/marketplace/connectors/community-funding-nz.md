Community Funding NZ, by All Too Human, gives your AI direct access to a free database of more than 380 New Zealand community grant funders: councils, community trusts, gaming trusts, foundations and government programmes.

For each funder it knows what they fund, where, who is eligible, typical amounts, the funder's own priorities and preferred language, common mistakes that get applications declined, required documents, and in many cases the actual application form questions. Funding rounds with opening and closing dates are included where we have them.

Three built-in prompts guide the common jobs: find funders for a project, prepare an application by working through the funder's own questions, and draft the application in the funder's language. Or just ask in plain English and let your AI call the tools it needs.

Feedback tools let you report how an application went, suggest a funder we are missing, or flag a detail that is wrong. Everything submitted is reviewed by a person before it changes anything.

There is no login and no cost. The data is kept fresh by our own crawler and reviewed by All Too Human, a small technology company in Motueka, Te Tauihu, that builds tools for iwi, community groups and local businesses. Deadlines and criteria can change, so always confirm on the funder's own website before applying.
