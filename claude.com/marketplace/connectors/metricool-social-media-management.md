Metricool connects Claude to your social media accounts so you can schedule and manage posts across your networks, including Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Bluesky, Threads, Twitch, and Google Business Profile.

Ask for AI-powered Best Time to Post recommendations based on your own historical data, pull analytics and reports on followers, reach, engagement, and impressions, track your Meta and TikTok Ads performance, and switch between brands and accounts, all through natural conversation. You can also benchmark competitors on Instagram, Facebook, X, and YouTube, create content, and publish it straight to your social accounts.

Free skills take it further: Instagram Benchmark, LinkedIn Benchmark, and YouTube Benchmark score your accounts against real ones in your size tier, and Social Analytics Agent turns your data into a shareable HTML report.

With Claude Code, you can also generate carousels and other assets and send them directly to your social platforms.

Automate the repetitive part of social media management.
