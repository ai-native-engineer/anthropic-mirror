Connect Claude with Cashfree and manage your entire payments ecosystem through natural conversation. Access merchant dashboard data instantly — including payments, orders, refunds, settlements, payouts, payment links, reconciliation reports, transaction activity, and workflows — without jumping between tools or dashboards.

Built for both operational visibility and developer workflows, the connector is evolving beyond dashboard actions to support the full suite of Cashfree, Payouts, SecureID, PPI, and Partners APIs used in real-world integrations. From tracking live payment flows and debugging transactions to managing settlements and analyzing business performance, Claude becomes your unified AI-powered Cashfree assistant for every payments-related task.
