Link Claude to your athenahealth medical record using the athenaPatient MCP connector to better understand your health information. Ask Claude to explain a lab result, review what happened at a recent visit, or create charts of lab values or vitals (like blood pressure and heart rate). Claude helps you understand your existing records; it doesn't diagnose conditions or make clinical decisions, and always points you back to your care team for medical advice. You decide what information Claude can access, and you can update or revoke that permission at any time.
