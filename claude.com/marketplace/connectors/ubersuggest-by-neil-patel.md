Ubersuggest brings SEO research directly into Claude. Ask questions in plain language and get real search data from the same dataset that powers ubersuggest.com — no dashboards, no exports.

What you can do:

• Keyword research — search volume, CPC, SEO and paid difficulty, related keyword suggestions, Google autocomplete ideas, search intent and content ideas.

• Domain analysis — traffic estimates, domain authority, ranking keywords (organic and paid), top pages, top countries and estimated traffic value.

• Competitor research — find organic competitors, compare keyword overlap and uncover the pages driving their traffic.

• Backlinks — backlink and referring-domain data, anchor texts, backlink overview metrics and backlink opportunities from competitors.

• SERP analysis — see who ranks for a keyword, with page-level metrics and estimated click distribution.

• Site audits — run a crawl of your site, track its progress and get the list of technical issues and affected URLs, plus PageSpeed performance audits.

• Projects and rank tracking — create projects, add tracked keywords and competitors, check ranking positions over time and review suggested SEO opportunities.

• AI Search Visibility — see how your brand appears in AI assistant answers, with per-prompt visibility reports.

Connect your Ubersuggest account to get started. Data access follows your Ubersuggest plan limits, and the connector never deletes projects or publishes anything on your behalf.
