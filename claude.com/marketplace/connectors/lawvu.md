LawVu is the legal operating system for in-house corporate legal teams, combining matter management, contract lifecycle management, spend, intake, documents, and a knowledge base in one unified platform.

Connect Claude to the operational data your legal team relies on every day. Search and retrieve matters and contracts in natural language, generate status summaries and reports, retrieve answers grounded in your internal legal knowledge base, create new matters or intake requests, update matter status, and create tasks - all without leaving Claude.

You can also combine LawVu data with information from other connected systems, like CRM or HR platforms, for cross-system reporting and analysis.

Access is scoped to each user's own permissions and record access in LawVu and provides a full audit trail.
