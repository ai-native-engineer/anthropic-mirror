Light is the finance platform for your company's spend, bills, cards, and accounting. This connector lets Claude work with your Light data on your behalf, scoped to your account and your permissions.

Once connected, you can ask Claude to:

- Search bills and vendors and manage payables

- Look up cards and card transactions and edit draft transactions

- Draft expenses and submit them for reimbursement

- Search the chart of accounts and tax codes

- Find team members and see pending approvals

- Answer questions from your company policy and Light help articles

- Run and edit financial reports scoped to your company
