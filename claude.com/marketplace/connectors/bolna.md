Bolna is a voice AI platform for building and running phone-calling agents. This connector lets Claude manage your Bolna agents: list and inspect agent configurations, review call history and full transcripts, view call batches, check your account info and wallet balance, and create or update agents.

This connector cannot place calls, start batches, purchase phone numbers, or spend balance.

Read tools: list agents, get agent details, list call history, get call details and transcript, list call batches, get account info and wallet balance.

Write tools: create an agent, update an agent's prompts and settings and deleting an agent.

Sign in with your Bolna account via OAuth. Requires a Bolna account.

## Tools

* create\_agent
* delete\_agent
* get\_agent
* get\_execution
* get\_user\_info
* list\_agent\_executions
* list\_agents
* list\_batches
* update\_agent

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
