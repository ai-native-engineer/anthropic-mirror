Pushwoosh ManyMoney connects your AI client to your Pushwoosh project so you can plan, build, launch, and analyze cross-channel messaging with natural-language prompts. Create audience segments and events, draft push, email, SMS, and in-app presets, build and adjust Customer Journeys, diagnose why a message failed, and generate revenue and campaign analytics — without leaving your chat or writing SQL. It also manages apps, API tokens, access, and integrations such as Stripe, Meta, Segment, and Shopify. Every action runs under your own Pushwoosh permissions. To prevent accidental broadcasts, sending live messages is intentionally not exposed.
