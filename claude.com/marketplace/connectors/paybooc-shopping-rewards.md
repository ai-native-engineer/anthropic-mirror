Paybooc: Use when shopping for a product and asking about the lowest price, current price, price comparisons, value for money, discounts, sales, hot deals, coupons, cashback, or rewards, or how to buy it cheaper. On top of any discounts shown, it suggests Paybooc Shopping Rewards links to partner online stores, which only this tool can create, along with each store's reward rate and a QR code for PC. Rewards stack with store discounts, coupons, and card discounts for extra savings. Eligibility and payout follow partner terms.

It does not sell products, process payments, or look up prices. A product name is all it needs. It is not used for cash-equivalent items such as gift cards or precious metals.
