The Oxford Economics MCP connector gives Claude access to Oxford Economics’ research, reports, and economic data and forecasts, helping users answer questions about economies, markets, sectors, and regions with trusted insight.

The connector includes two tools: EconomicAnalysis, which provides narrative answers grounded in Oxford Economics reports and research, and EconomicData, which returns numerical economic data and forecasts for single indicators or cross-region comparisons. Use it to generate concise explanations, summaries, report-backed analysis, assumptions, risks, conclusions, and data tables from Oxford Economics sources.

Contact Oxford Economics about upgrading your organisation's account for MCP access: https://www.oxfordeconomics.com/ai-data-solutions/

## Tools

* EconomicAnalysis
* EconomicData

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
