For any business user who needs to work with contracts or their data, Juro's MCP server enables you to access contract intelligence directly from Claude.

Search and analyse contracts, build reports, create contracts from approved templates, edit and download documents - all without leaving Claude.

Bring the power of the world's leading frontier AI lab into your contract repository - and let your business colleagues work in their AI platform of choice. Connect Juro's MCP server with Claude to agree contracts faster today.

## Tools

* create-contract
* download-document
* fetch
* graphql-search
* request-upload-url
* update-smartfields
* upload-document-version

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
