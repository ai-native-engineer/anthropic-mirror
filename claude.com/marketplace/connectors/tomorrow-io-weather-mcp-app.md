Tomorrow.io Weather Widgets brings rich, interactive weather visualizations straight into your Claude conversations. Ask about the weather, air quality, pollen, or wildfire risk for anywhere in the world and get a live, at-a-glance widget instead of a wall of text.

Available widgets:

• Weather Forecast — current conditions plus a 6-day outlook for any location

• Weather Summary — a quick snapshot with a 24-hour natural-language forecast and a today-vs-yesterday comparison

• Multi-Location Weather — compare current conditions across up to 6 cities side by side

• Air Quality Index (AQI) — current air quality alongside weather, ideal for deciding whether to head outside

• Multi-Location Air Quality — compare AQI across up to 6 locations

• Pollen Forecast — tree, grass, and weed pollen levels for allergy planning

• Pollutant Breakdown — detailed concentrations of PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NO₂, CO, and SO₂

• Fire Risk Index — wildfire danger (Fire Weather Index) and wind conditions across up to 6 locations

Powered by Tomorrow.io's global weather intelligence platform, every widget uses real-time data and works for any location you name. Just ask — "What's the weather in Tokyo?", "Compare air quality in LA and Beijing", or "Is there wildfire risk near me?" — and Claude renders the right widget automatically.
