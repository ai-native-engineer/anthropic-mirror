Connect Claude to Loopio, the AI-powered RFP and proposal response platform. Securely access and manage your content Library, and coordinate and respond to Projects with Claude.

- \*\*Effortless Evaluations:\*\* Deploy the knowledge to your team's hands. Easily find approved answers using content your team already trusts.

- \*\*Faster Responses:\*\* Respond to entire RFPs, security questionnaires, and DDQs faster by responding using approved content, track progress, and review results, with visibility into the content sources answers are drawn from.

- \*\*SME Collaboration:\*\* Easily coordinate work as a team by browsing your Loopio projects, review questions and entries, and coordinating workloads across your team.

- \*\*Respond Easily:\*\* Meet your experts where they are. Refine answers and save them to project entries directly from Claude.

Make Loopio a native part of your AI tech stack - providing a governed backbone for your sales and response workflows.

An active Loopio subscription is required.
