Make faster, better tactical decisions within the supply chain you have today and design the supply chain you need for tomorrow. Optilogic connects Claude to a platform that does both. Work with a live digital twin of your current supply chain powered by agentic AI that combines mathematical optimization, simulation, and demand modeling to support decisions across the full horizon, from day-to-day operational response to long-term network, transportation, inventory, and production strategies.

- Run what-if scenario analysis on network design and sourcing models

- Analyze demand at the SKU and location level, and trace how it's served across your network

- Answer strategic and tactical routing questions to drive cost savings and service level improvements

- Place safety stock across your full network and see what it costs to hit a given service level

- Score risk across your network: single-source dependencies, demand/facility/supplier concentration, geographic exposure, capacity, and transport-time risk, so you know where to focus before a disruption

- Model the impact of tariff scenarios

- Design and compare network strategy across sourcing, manufacturing, distribution, and nearshoring scenarios

- Stress-test a plan against real-world variability such as demand swings, delayed lead times, and capacity shocks

- Compare multiple scenarios, sensitivity analysis at-scale, with output and critical insights auto-generated

- Translate optimization model outputs into decision-ready formats for planners, finance, and executives

Best for: S&OP/IBP leaders, network design teams, transportation teams, supply chain modelers and analysts, operational planners as well as executives who want a straight answer to a "what if" question without needing modeling expertise.

Requires a free or paid Optilogic account. You will be redirected to Optilogic to sign in and authorize access. Your data and models stay secure and isolated in your own account.
