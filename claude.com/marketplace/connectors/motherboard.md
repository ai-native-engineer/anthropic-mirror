Motherboard is the service knowledge platform for field service teams that maintain complex equipment and robotic fleets. This connector gives Claude read-only access to your company's Motherboard workspace, so you can ask questions about your own operation and get answers grounded in your records.

With it, Claude can look up and search service tickets, equipment, parts and assemblies; follow relationships such as which parts go into an assembly and how many; read your company's SOPs and the exact pages of service manuals and source documents, including their page images; and search service knowledge across all of them. Every answer can cite the exact record, SOP or document page it came from, with a link back to Motherboard.

The connector is strictly read-only: it cannot create, change or delete anything, take actions, or post messages. Claude sees only what you are allowed to see in Motherboard, because every request runs under your own account and role.

To connect, enter your company's Motherboard workspace name (the first part of your Motherboard web address) and sign in with your Motherboard account. You can disconnect at any time from Settings > Connected agents in Motherboard. Requires a Motherboard account provided by your company.

* read\_source\_page
* search\_objects
