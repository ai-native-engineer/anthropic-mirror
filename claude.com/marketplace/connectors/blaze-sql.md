BlazeSQL lets Claude query your database or warehouse with all the necessary context to get the correct answers.

Connect to Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Microsoft SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, AWS Athena, MariaDB, SQL Anywhere, Databricks, Clickhouse, and Oracle.

With context management, observability, and comprehensive access controls, BlazeSQL turns claude into an enterprise-ready AI Data Analyst for reliable self-service across your entire company.

As a comprehensive Business Intelligence layer, BlazeSQL also enables visualization, dashboards, advanced statistical analysis, predictive analytics, scheduled reports, and custom PDF or interactive HTML reports.

## Tools

* describe\_tables
* execute\_sql
* graph\_sql\_result
* list\_dashboards
* list\_saved\_queries
* list\_schemas
* list\_tables
* read\_dashboard
* read\_saved\_query
* search\_dashboards
* search\_saved\_queries
* suggest\_schema\_context\_update
