MindMeister is the refreshingly simple mind map tool to explore ideas and visualize information. Map them, store them, share them — trusted by millions since 2007.

The MindMeister MCP server connects your MindMeister workspace to your AI assistant. Once authorized, your assistant can:

• Build mind maps from any prompt — turn a topic, outline, or brain dump into a structured visual map

• Search and summarize existing maps so you can bring map insight into any conversation

• Convert documents and notes into maps — upload a file or paste an outline and get a clean, organized structure

• Run planning frameworks visually — SWOT, OKRs, lesson plans, meeting agendas, concept maps, and more

• Share and export maps as view-only links or as PDF, PNG, DOCX, or TXT

Get started fast with our template skills — pre-built workflows for brainstorming, OKR planning, strategic frameworks, lesson plans, team meetings, and more.
