Revolut X helps Claude look up the signed-in user's exchange balances and working/finished orders, inspect a single order and its fills, and read live market catalog, tickers, candles, order-book depth, and public trades. It does not place, replace, or cancel orders.

## Tools

* revx\_get\_balances
* revx\_get\_active\_orders
* revx\_get\_historical\_orders
* revx\_get\_order
* revx\_get\_order\_fills
* revx\_get\_transactions
* revx\_get\_transaction
* revx\_get\_currencies
* revx\_get\_pairs
* revx\_get\_tickers
* revx\_get\_candles
* revx\_get\_order\_book
* revx\_get\_all\_trades
