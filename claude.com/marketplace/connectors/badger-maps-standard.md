Badger AI brings a new layer of intelligence to the platform your team already uses. Opt into the features that make sense for your workflow — from AI-built routes to automated CRM updates — and let Badger handle the busy work so your reps can spend more time selling.

AI Route Planner: Your optimal week, built automatically

Stop deciding who to see and when. Badger AI analyzes your accounts and builds your entire week automatically — prioritizing your stops by last visit date, follow-up status, and opportunity size, all built around the existing meetings in your calendar.

Automated Follow-Up Emails: No deal goes cold. No follow-up gets missed.

Deals die in the silence between meetings. Badger AI reads your meeting notes and automatically drafts and sends personalized follow-up emails to every customer — so the right message goes to the right person at the right time, without you lifting a finger.

Automated Scheduling Emails: Scheduling, without the back and forth

Before you even leave for the field, Badger AI analyzes your routes and sends targeted email blasts to prospects in the areas you're visiting — so by the time you're on the road, meetings are already being set up around your existing schedule.

The old way of field sales is over.

Reps planning routes by hand. Hours of admin after a long day in the field. Follow-ups that fall through the cracks. Badger AI eliminates all of it — so your team can focus on what actually moves the needle.

The future of field sales is automated. Start today.
