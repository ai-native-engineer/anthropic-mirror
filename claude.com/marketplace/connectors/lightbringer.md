Lightbringer brings patent and invention management into Claude. Connect your Lightbringer organisation and work with your IP directly in the chat.

With Lightbringer connected, you can:

- Search and read your portfolio — inventions, patent applications, strategies, and reports.

- Draft new invention disclosures against the Lightbringer authoring template, validate them against the schema, and update or submit them for review.

- Run Lightbringer's patent-analysis suite on a disclosure to get structured feedback on clarity, problem framing, and completeness.

- Collaborate on patent reviews — list reviews, read the document and comment threads, respond, and add or reply to comments anchored to specific passages.

Because Claude can use all of your connected tools in one conversation, it can pull in the engineering work you've already done — code, design docs, tickets, and chat — and help you capture the inventive parts straight into Lightbringer.

Access is scoped to your authenticated Lightbringer organisation via OAuth, with separate read and write consent.
