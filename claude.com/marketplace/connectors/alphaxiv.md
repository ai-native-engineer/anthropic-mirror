Fast retrieval (both keyword and embedding search) tools across the millions of papers in the arXiv corpus. Give your agent context on the latest research papers.

## Tools

* answer\_pdf\_queries
* get\_paper\_content
* read\_files\_from\_github
* embedding\_similarity\_search
* full\_text\_papers\_search
* agentic\_paper\_retrieval
