Turn your Claude agent into a full B2B prospecting platform. Vibe Prospecting finds any company or professional right inside your chat.

Go beyond basic contact lookups: search company profiles, org charts, and company hierarchy to identify decision-makers and their reporting lines, run company search and contact search by name or industry, and track business events like funding rounds, hiring signals, and growth signals as they happen. Find actionable contact info from emails and numbers to social profiles.

Underneath, Vibe Prospecting covers the full stack sales, recruiting, and GTM teams expect: firmographics, technographics, intent data, buyer intent, website traffic, and industry classification by LinkedIn Industry, NAICS or SIC code, plus employee count, revenue data, funding events, and workforce signals for 150M+ companies and 800M+ professional profiles from 50+ live sources, including third-party and alternative data.

Use it to build lead lists, run people search, person lookup, and phone number lookups, source candidates for recruiting and candidate sourcing, and craft personalized cold email, outbound sales, phone number waterfall, and sales engagement sequences. Enrich any CRM or ATS record with fresh contact data and company data, run an email finder plus email verification, append missing fields, and resolve duplicate entities with entity and identity resolution — all without leaving Claude. Layer in market, competitive, and revenue intelligence to sharpen list building and sales enablement.

It's a modern alternative to Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay, RocketReach, Clearbit, and Cognism: one connector for prospecting, lead generation, account-based marketing, sales intelligence, email customization, and revenue operations, so outreach starts the moment you find a qualified lead in the contact database. Export enriched lists straight to CSV or your CRM and keep every pipeline generation and ICP workflow running on live b2b data.
