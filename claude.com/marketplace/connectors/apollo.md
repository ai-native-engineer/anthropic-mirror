Apollo is a B2B sales intelligence and sales engagement platform, connected to Claude so your team can research, enrich, and reach your ideal prospects from a single conversation, with no copy-paste between tools. Search Apollo's B2B contact database of people and companies to find prospects that match your ideal customer profile and build targeted lead lists.

Use Apollo as an email finder and phone number finder to enrich individual records or bulk-enrich entire lists with verified emails, direct dials, and firmographic and technographic data. Pull company job postings to spot hiring signals and buyer intent before you reach out. Then build a cold outreach email sequence with personalized messaging and push it straight to Apollo, all without leaving Claude.
