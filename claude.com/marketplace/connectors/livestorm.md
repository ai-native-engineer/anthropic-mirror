Manage your Livestorm events, sessions, and attendees directly from Claude. Create and update events, register and segment participants and monitor engagement. Pull attendance, chat and contact data from sessions that already ran to measure event impact and feed it into your workflows. Claude cross-references registration, attendance, and session activity in a single conversation, turning raw event data into next steps for marketing and sales.

Event & Session Management:

"Create a product demo event for next Wednesday with a morning and afternoon session"

Attendee Registration:

"Register these 50 signups for Thursday's product webinar'"

Engagement & Lead Scoring:

"Show me which registrants attended more than 30 minutes of yesterday's webinar and rank them by engagement"

Event Impact Analysis:

"Pull attendance and registration numbers for last month's events so I can calculate the show-up rate"

Team Management:

"List all team members with admin role across our Livestorm organization"
