SlickTrip watches travel prices so you do not have to refresh a search.

Search flights and hotels with live prices, see the cheapest days for a route on a calendar, look up which seats are still open on a specific flight, and read a trip someone shared with you.

Then set an alert and SlickTrip keeps checking on its own: a specific fare, a hotel stay, a seat or a row with two seats together, or a bucket list for a route or a place with flexible dates and a price you are willing to pay. When the price drops or a seat opens, SlickTrip emails or texts you.

Ask what you are tracking at any time, change an alert's price bar or channel, stop one, or share a trip as a link. Searching works without an account. Alerts need a free SlickTrip login, which you complete inside Claude the first time you set one.

SlickTrip does not sell tickets and takes no payment. When you are ready to buy, it lists the sellers currently offering that itinerary or stay and hands you to the airline's or seller's own checkout.
