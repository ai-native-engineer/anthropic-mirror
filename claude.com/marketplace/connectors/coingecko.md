CoinGecko connector brings live crypto market data into Claude. Get real-time prices, market cap, trading volume, and rank for 20,000+ coins, then chart price history over any date range or look up what a coin traded at on a specific past day. Track the overall market with a global snapshot, see which sectors and narratives like DeFi are leading, and spot today’s trending coins and top gainers and losers. Compare 2–4 coins side by side on price, market cap, FDV, and performance, pull project fundamentals, and catch up on crypto news globally or for a single coin.
