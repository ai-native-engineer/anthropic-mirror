Run async standups, team updates, check-ins, polls, surveys, and get progress summaries in Slack and Microsoft Teams.

Geekbot is an assistant for Slack and Microsoft Teams that helps remote and hybrid teams automate standups, daily updates, team check-ins, status reports and any kind of polls or surveys (eg employee feedback, developer pulse, leadership trust and more).

Connect Geekbot to Claude to submit standup reports, answer daily check-ins, create polls and surveys, review team updates, read status reports and summarize weekly recaps.

Geekbot helps engineering managers, people/HR managers, team leads, and remote teams ask questions like 'Collect team blockers', 'Monitor employee engagement', 'Run a pulse survey', 'Submit my daily update' and more.

This feature requires a Geekbot paid plan.
