The Clinical Trials Connector gives Claude access to ClinicalTrials.gov, the NIH/NLM registry of FDA-regulated clinical studies conducted worldwide.

## Tools

* get\_trial\_details
* search\_by\_eligibility
* analyze\_endpoints
* search\_investigators
* search\_trials
* search\_by\_sponsor
