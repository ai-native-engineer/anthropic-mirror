MeisterTask is the work management platform transforming how teams get work done. Hosted in Germany and built on simplicity, it brings together all your projects, tasks, and documentation in one place.

The MeisterTask MCP server connects your MeisterTask workspace to your AI assistant. Once authorized, your assistant can:

• Plan and structure projects from a brief or goal, creating sections and tasks in seconds

• Search across your work — tasks, projects, and notes — and pull the right context into any conversation

• Capture meeting notes as MeisterTask Notes, automatically turning action items into assigned tasks

• Run daily standups and weekly reviews by summarizing overdue, due, and recently completed work across your projects

• Read and edit Notes to keep your team's documentation up to date as work progresses

Get started fast with our template skills — pre-built workflows for the most common MeisterTask use cases, from project kickoff to task breakdown.
