General Legal is a law firm that combines world-class attorneys with AI-native execution. With the General Legal connector, you can kick off new legal projects right from the conversations and workflows you're already in--leveraging the same elite counsel in a new, seamless package. Open matters, ask questions, respond to your attorney's comments, and download the redlined document, all through Claude.

What you can do: start a matter from a question or a document · upload .docx/.pdf contracts via a secure one-time link (file contents never pass through the model) · track review status across your matters · message your attorney in the matter thread · download reviewed, released versions.

Sign in with your General Legal account or create one while connecting. Your documents are only visible to your own organization.

## Tools

* confirm\_upload
* download\_contract
* list\_contracts
* list\_deals
* list\_thread\_messages
* reply\_to\_thread
* start\_deal
* upload\_document
