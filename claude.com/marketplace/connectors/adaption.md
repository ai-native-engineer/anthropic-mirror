Adaption connects Claude to your Adaption account so you can prepare training data and fine-tune models from a conversation.

Datasets

- Import datasets from Hugging Face, Kaggle, or Google Sheets

- Adapt them with Adaption's processing pipeline and review quality evaluations

- Augment with synthetic domain or general rows

- Translate and localize rows for other languages and countries

- Combine compatible datasets and get export links

Fine-tuning

- Browse available base models

- Get recommended hyperparameters for a dataset

- Launch AutoScientist training runs and monitor their progress

Invent

- Generate synthetic datasets from a natural language description, with no seed data

Every tool that spends credits (adaptation, augmentation, translation, localization, generation, training) supports an estimate mode, so Claude can show the cost before anything launches. You sign in with your Adaption account through OAuth and choose which organization Claude can access; no API key is needed.

Example prompts:

- "List my Adaption datasets"

- "Import this Hugging Face dataset and adapt it for fine-tuning"

- "Start fine-tuning on my adapted dataset and pick a suitable base model"

- "Generate 1,000 customer service examples with Invent"
