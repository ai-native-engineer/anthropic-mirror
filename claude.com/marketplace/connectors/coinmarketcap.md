CoinMarketCap MCP provides professional tools for comprehensive cryptocurrency market analysis. Access real-time price quotes, global market metrics including Fear and Greed Index, and BTC/ETH dominance data. Perform technical analysis with moving averages, MACD, RSI, and Fibonacci levels. Stay informed with the latest crypto news, trending narratives, and upcoming macro events. Explore on-chain metrics including address distribution, whale tracking, and transaction fees. Monitor derivatives data such as open interest, funding rates, and liquidation volumes. Powered by CoinMarketCap, the world's most-referenced cryptocurrency data platform.
