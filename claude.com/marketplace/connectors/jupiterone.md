JupiterOne is a agentic risk management platform that maps your cloud resources, identities, devices, vulnerabilities, and the relationships between them into a queryable graph. This connector lets you work with that graph in natural language: run J1QL queries against your asset inventory, explore entity types, triage active alerts, create and evaluate detection rules, review integration ingestion status, and build dashboards. Every action runs with your existing JupiterOne user permissions.
