By the time a government RFP posts, it's usually wired for a vendor who has been in the room for months, favoring relationships over merit. Pursuit helps sellers generate more pipeline by surfacing verifiable buying intent 6-18 months before a solicitation exists. We monitor 170,000+ public sector entities across council meetings, budget documents, strategic plans, and FOIA procurement data to surface buying signals to GTM teams. And we don't just signal opportunities, we route them into your workflows so you’re constantly generating pipeline.
