Legal Data Hunter is trying to build the world's first omni-jurisdictional legal database, giving Claude access to 23M+ documents from 160+ jurisdictions in one MCP server. We ingest directly from official registries : Légifrance, EUR-Lex, BOE, RIS, the Bundesgerichtshof, the European Court of Human Rights, and 180+ more, covering EU consolidated law, supreme and constitutional court case law, and official doctrine. Resolve loose citations (ECLI, CELEX, case numbers), search with hybrid semantic + keyword filters, and fetch full document text. Authoritative primary sources only, no scraped web copy.

## Tools

* search
* resolve\_reference
* get\_document
* discover\_countries
* discover\_sources
* get\_filters
* report\_source\_issue
