MT Newswires' MCP server delivers real-time, low-latency financial news across equities, fixed income, commodities, FX, and macroeconomics. Structured and machine-readable, it integrates seamlessly into AI, trading, and analytics systems, providing scalable, reliable, event-driven intelligence from MT Newswires' global newsroom trusted by leading banks, brokerages, and data providers.
