Adobe for Claude unlocks more ways to create and get work done by bringing in creativity and productivity capabilities across Acrobat, Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator, Firefly, Premiere, Adobe Express, InDesign, and Stock directly into your conversation. Describe what you want to make or change, and Adobe can help turn ideas, images, videos, and documents into polished outputs — edit multiple photos, create assets, edit and organize PDFs, and resize video.

Key capabilities:

• Create and customize designs: Find templates for marketing materials, proposals, invitations, and more. Personalize text, replace and resize images, change colors, and refine designs in a live editor.

• Create, review, and refine PDFs: Turn designs into shareable PDFs, organize pages, edit content, redact sensitive information, add annotations, and review documents in an interactive viewer.

• Edit photos individually or in bulk: Adjust color and lighting, blur or remove backgrounds, straighten images, and expand or crop photos.

• Search, organize, and summarize content: Find Creative Cloud assets, summarize documents, and manage files directly from your conversation.

• Adapt assets for different channels: Resize and crop images for social platforms and other formats, then download and share the finished assets.
