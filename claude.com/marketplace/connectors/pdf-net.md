Connect pdf.net to edit, generate, and organize PDFs in Claude. Your pdf.net library connects directly — no re-uploading every time.

Edit PDFs in place: change text, fix dates, fill forms. Generate new PDFs from a prompt — invoices, contracts, proposals. Merge up to 20 PDFs, split by page range, compress, and convert to Word, Excel, or PowerPoint. Translate full documents while keeping the layout. Organize your library into folders.

---

Working with PDFs can be slow. You convert to Word to edit, switch tools to merge, pay extra to translate, and dig through folders to find the file. pdf.net puts all of it in one chat.

Edit PDFs in place — no Word roundtrip. Generate invoices, contracts, and proposals from a prompt. Merge up to 20 files, split by page, compress, and convert to Word, Excel, or PowerPoint. Translate full documents while keeping the layout. Your pdf.net library stays connected, so files are one message away.
