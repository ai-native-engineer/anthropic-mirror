Teamtailor brings your hiring work into the conversation. Ask about your open jobs, see how candidates move through each recruitment pipeline stage, and get quick summaries of a candidate's profile, application, and activity history.

You can also act on what you find: move applications between stages, add tags, set custom fields, manage todos, and leave the data ready for your next hiring decision. On the job side, update postings with screening questions, required skills and traits, and interview kits — everything a recruiter needs to keep candidates moving toward an offer.

Typical things to ask:

"Which jobs are actively hiring right now?"

"Show the hiring pipeline for the Backend Engineer role"

"Summarize the candidates in the interview stage"

"Who applied this week?"

"Move Anna to the next stage and tag her as top-candidate"

Works with your existing Teamtailor account and respects your user's permissions.
