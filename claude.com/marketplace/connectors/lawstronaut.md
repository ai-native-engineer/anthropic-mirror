Connect Claude to the Lawstronaut MCP — a fast, developer-friendly Model Context Protocol server. Access legislation, regulations, case law, regulatory guidance, enforcement actions, legislative history, and more across 170+ jurisdictions. Built for AI-powered legal research, compliance workflows, and enterprise automation, Lawstronaut delivers continuously updated legal data with comprehensive jurisdictional coverage, so every result is traceable back to its official source.

Plans start at just $15 per user/month. Research, draft, automate compliance, and build legal AI workflows using legal data that stays up to date as the law changes.
