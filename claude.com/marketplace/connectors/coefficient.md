Coefficient SuperAgent is the data agent built for everyone. It answers questions about your company's business data, such as pipeline, revenue and customers, from the systems your company already uses. It shows the results as charts, dashboards and data tables right in Claude.

Make your company fluent in numbers. Ask a question in plain language, such as "What was new pipeline by region last quarter?", and SuperAgent answers it the way your data team would.

Your business context in every answer. SuperAgent learns your systems, metrics, business rules and fiscal calendar. Your data team can review and certify that context, so every answer reflects how your business actually operates.

Analysis you can trust. For every answer, SuperAgent shows which source data it queried and which filters and calculations it applied. Open the rows behind an answer in a sortable, searchable data table and check the numbers yourself.

Charts and dashboards in the chat. See charts in Claude, and open your dashboards with working filters. Run your team's Playbooks for work you repeat. Share an answer or chart in Slack or by email; Claude asks you to confirm first.

Enterprise ready. SuperAgent connects only to the systems your admin approves, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Snowflake, Databricks and Tableau. It enforces source-level access for every user and keeps an audit log of every question and answer. SOC 2 Type II.

To get started, sign in with your work account. Your workspace needs at least one connected data source, and SuperAgent can give you the link to connect one.
