Use Zacks Investment Research’s trusted data right in your AI chat — and give your AI assistant an edge. Zacks Connect AI is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that plugs the Zacks Rank, estimates, fundamentals, and market news straight into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other MCP-compatible agents.

## Tools

* get\_balance\_sheet
* get\_cash\_flow
* get\_company\_snapshot
* get\_income\_statement
