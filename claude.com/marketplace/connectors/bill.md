Connect BILL to Claude for instant visibility into your company's payables, receivables, and spend. Search bills, payments, invoices, and card transactions, and summarize what's due, outstanding, or trending. Access is read-only and runs as each signed-in user: same data, same access, same permissions as BILL itself.

## Tools

* payables\_find\_payment\_by\_confirmation
* payables\_get\_bill
* payables\_get\_payment
* payables\_list\_bills
* payables\_list\_payments
* payables\_list\_recurring\_bill\_templates
* receivables\_get\_invoice
* receivables\_list\_invoices
* spend\_list\_budgets
* spend\_list\_transactions

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
