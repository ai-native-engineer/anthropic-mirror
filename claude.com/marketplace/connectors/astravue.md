Access your Astravue workspace directly from Claude to search, create, and

update tasks, projects, and spaces. Track overdue work, review blockers and

dependencies, log time, manage checklists and comments, and get a live view

of team workload without leaving the conversation.

Every request runs with your own Astravue permissions, so Claude sees exactly

what you see — nothing more. Write actions are explicit and scoped to the

work items you name.

Interactive task and project views open inline for review before you act.

Complements your Astravue workspace with tools spanning tasks, subtasks,

dependencies, custom fields, statuses, tags, time tracking, saved filters,

and workspace members — alongside the workflows your team already uses.
