Civic IQ helps companies that sell to US state and local government find and win public-sector work, from inside Claude.

Ask in plain language and Civic IQ searches its government data:

- Open and past solicitations (RFPs, RFQs and bids), filtered by keyword, agency, state, agency type and status

- Pre-RFP signals: projects agencies discuss in public council and board meetings, often months before a solicitation is published

- Decision-maker contacts at an agency, by name and job title

- Vendor spend: which vendors agencies pay, and for what, line by line

- Public contracts and purchase orders

- Government agencies near a location

- Signals from your saved Civic IQ digest workspaces

Results link to the source document, so every answer can be checked.

Civic IQ can also save a search as an alert and email you new results daily or weekly. Managing your own alerts is the only thing it changes; everything else is read-only.

Requires a Civic IQ account. A free plan is available with no credit card; it includes RFP search, signal search and limited contact lookups. Vendor spend, contracts, nearby agencies, digest workspaces and alerts are part of paid plans.
