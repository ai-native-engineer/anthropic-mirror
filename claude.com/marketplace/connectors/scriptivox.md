Scriptivox turns recorded audio and video into accurate text in 119 languages, with speaker labels and timestamps.

Ask Claude to transcribe a Google Drive or Dropbox link, drop a file into the upload box in the chat, or send a bot to record a Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Webex call. Claude can then read the transcript and write minutes with owners and due dates, show notes with chapters, or captions.

In the chat you get a transcript with its audio (click a line to play from there), progress while a file transcribes, your library of past transcripts, and a subtitle editor that downloads SRT or WebVTT.

Scriptivox uses your own account. When you connect, you choose your Scriptivox plan (the same minutes and limits as the website) or your API credits, and how much Claude may do. Claude never pays for anything: on API credits it can hand you a checkout link, and you pay on that page yourself. Every connected app is listed on your Scriptivox Connected apps page, where you can see when it was last used, switch AI access off, or disconnect it.
