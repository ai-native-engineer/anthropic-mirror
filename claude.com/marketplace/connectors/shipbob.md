Manage and get answers about your fulfillment operations in seconds, without opening a dashboard. The ShipBob connector gives Claude live access to orders, inventory, products, and shipments across every channel and fulfillment center on your account. Ask questions and get back the data you'd otherwise build a report for. Manage orders and inventory without switching tools. Spot SKUs about to stock out. Investigate orders in exception. Pull transit breakdowns of last week's shipments. Built and maintained by ShipBob. Included for every ShipBob merchant. Uses OAuth, so your data stays secure and scoped to your account.
