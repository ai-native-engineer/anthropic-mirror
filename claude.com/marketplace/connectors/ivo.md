Connect Claude to Ivo to ask contract questions, compare terms, and retrieve extracted data with supporting quotes. Build renewal calendars with the renewal-calendar skill, or create AI-field definitions and extract missing field values in accessible rooms through Ivo Assistant. Existing answers are skipped; contracts and existing field definitions are not edited or deleted. Each query and Ivo Assistant’s reply are saved as a chat thread in your Ivo workspace, identified by chatId.

Requires a paid Ivo account with Intelligence access and permission to the relevant contract rooms. Connect using your workspace’s MCP URL and OAuth.

## Tools

* GetRoomRows
* GetRooms
* PollPromise
* QueryRepository

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
