Connect your AI assistant to iubenda to handle website legal compliance from start to finish. Create and manage sites, generate privacy and cookie policies tailored to the services you use, add data-processing services from iubenda's catalog, set up cookie consent banners, and draft terms and conditions, all through natural language. Run cookie scans to detect trackers, then update your policies to match as your site changes. Built for founders, agencies, and developers who need to meet GDPR, ePrivacy, US state privacy laws, and privacy requirements across markets, without the manual overhead.
