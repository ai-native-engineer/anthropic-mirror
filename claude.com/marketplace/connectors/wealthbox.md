Wealthbox is a CRM built for financial advisors and wealth management firms. This connector brings your Wealthbox data directly into Claude.

Search and update contacts, review and add notes, and track opportunities across your pipeline. Manage projects and workflow templates, and look up or create tasks. Check calendar events and meetings tied to any client. Work with the custom objects and custom fields specific to your firm, add comments and tags to keep records organized, link related contacts together, and look up users, teams, and workspaces across your organization.

Built for financial advisors, enterprise RIA firms, and broker-dealers already running client relationships in Wealthbox. Ask Claude to prep for a client meeting, follow up on a deal, or update a record, and it works directly against your live CRM data.
