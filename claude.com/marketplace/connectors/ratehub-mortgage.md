Ratehub makes it easy to compare the best mortgage rates in Canada in seconds. Instantly find the lowest fixed and variable mortgage rates from the Big Banks and top mortgage lenders.

Customize your results based on home price, down payment, and location to see personalized mortgage rates you can actually qualify for. Easily compare options side by side and find the best rate for your budget.

Whether you’re a first-time home buyer, renewing your mortgage, or refinancing, this tool compares real-time mortgage rates to help save you time and money.

Try asking:

What are the lowest mortgage rates in Ontario right now?

What is the best 5-year fixed rate from top 6 banks?

Show me the best variable rate options for a $600K mortgage in B.C
