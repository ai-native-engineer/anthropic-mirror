Aptus.AI - NextOS brings the Aptus.AI legal platform into your assistant. Today it gives your assistant NextOS's legal research: ask in natural language and it finds what a named law, regulation or supervisory document provides on your point, what a court ruling holds from its citation, or which legislation and case law bear on a question you describe. Each answer is a report in your language (Italian, English or French) with what each source provides, short verbatim quotations, the in-force status of the act and a link to the full text on the Aptus.AI platform. The connector retrieves and reports; your assistant draws the conclusions.

Coverage: Italian and EU law in depth; Luxembourg in depth for financial-market regulation; plus legislation and regulatory sources from other jurisdictions, including France, the UK, the US, San Marino, Hong Kong and Singapore. Case law covers Italy and the Court of Justice of the EU. Full list of sources: https://aptus.ai/en/enterprise/our-technology/#sources

Read-only: it makes no changes to your account and never returns the full text of a source. Requires an Aptus.AI account with an active subscription.
