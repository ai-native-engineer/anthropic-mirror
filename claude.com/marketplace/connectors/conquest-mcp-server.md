Connect Conquest to Claude. You can pull up a client’s plan, check progress toward their goals, and explore strategies from Strategic Advice Manager® (SAM) without context switching. SAM, Conquest’s AI expert system, does the math, so every number stays explainable and auditable. The result is faster meeting prep, more robust plans, and deeper client conversations.
