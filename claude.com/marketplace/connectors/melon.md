Explore a variety of Melon charts, get personalized music recommendations just for you, and discover your listening habits. All in one place. You can even play songs directly in the Melon app.

• Browse detailed information about artists, albums, and tracks.

• Search for songs and playlists using Melon charts and trending keywords.

• Enjoy personalized recommendations based on the songs you love and listen to most. The service is currently available in Korean only.

An English version is scheduled to be made available within this year, with an anticipated release in October.
