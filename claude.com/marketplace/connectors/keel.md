Keel turns your ideas into systematic strategies for Hyperliquid crypto perpetual futures (perps). Connect it to Claude, describe a strategy, and Claude builds it from Keel's strategy components, backtests it on real Hyperliquid history with fees and slippage, and explains the results.

With Keel in Claude you can:

- Build and edit strategies by describing them

- Backtest them and compare runs side by side

- Start from a verified strategy in the Keel library

- Keep notes on strategies and share a result by link

- See how the strategies you run in Keel are doing

Everything you create stays in your Keel workspace. Nothing is public unless you share it.

Claude can't move money, place orders, or run a strategy with real capital. You start and stop strategies in the Keel web app.

Keel measures the strategies you design. It does not give investment advice or recommendations, and backtest results do not predict future returns.
