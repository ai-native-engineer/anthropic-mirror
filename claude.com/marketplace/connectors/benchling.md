Benchling is the leading R&D platform built for AI-first science. This connector brings Benchling's best-in-class scientific AI directly into Claude, giving scientists, informaticians, and R&D leaders access to purpose-built research intelligence without leaving their workflow.

Ask complex questions about your research and get back structured, traceable answers drawn from your Benchling data, with the domain depth that general AI tools can't match.

To use, AI Chat must be enabled in your Benchling account.

## Tools

* benchling\_agent
* benchling\_task\_status
* benchling\_agent\_edit\_entry

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
