More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://developer.gemini.com/)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://support.gemini.com/hc/en-us)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.gemini.com/legal/privacy-policy)

Gemini MCP brings the Gemini digital-asset exchange directly into Claude. Through a single connection, your assistant gains real-time access to Gemini's public market data — live prices, tickers, order books, candles, and trade history across spot and derivatives — as well as prediction-market events and their tradable contracts. Ask a natural-language question and get grounded, answers drawn straight from Gemini's APIs, with no copy-pasting, custom scripts, or manual data wrangling.

For account-level insight, Gemini MCP adds secure, read-only access to your own trading data including balances, positions, open and historical orders, account summaries, and your trades. The server never stores your credentials or moves funds. The result is a safe, transparent bridge that lets AI assistants reason over both the market and your portfolio while keeping you firmly in control.
