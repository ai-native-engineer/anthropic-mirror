Connect Claude to SpotDraft to work with your contracts right where you are. Pull up contract details, metadata, and version history; search and filter across your entire contract repository; look up counterparties and templates; and kick off approvals or send contracts out for signature. Claude can also query legal intake requests and tasks, and run analytics queries against your contract data for reporting.

Built for legal, sales, and ops teams who live in SpotDraft daily and want contract context without leaving their workflow.
