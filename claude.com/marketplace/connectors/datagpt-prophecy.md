Your business context. Prophecy's governed data work. Answers you can check and rerun, in Claude.

You know which accounts matter, what "on track" means and where the numbers break. What slows you down is the data work behind the answer: pulling, cleaning and joining messy data across systems. Prophecy DataGPT does that work end to end, from raw data to finished analysis and reports, without leaving the conversation.

Ask "Which regions are behind on pipeline this quarter?" DataGPT pulls CRM exports and warehouse tables, cleans and matches records, applies your rules, and returns the answer with the visual workflow behind it. Inspect any step, open the code, or say what to change in plain language.

For business teams:

Go from raw data to a trusted report in plain language, using your team's definitions

Turn messy, multi-tab Excel workbooks into clean tables on your data platform

Check any number: every answer comes with the workflow and code behind it

Rerun approved workflows on new data the same way every time, or schedule them

For data teams and IT:

Review and approve AI-built workflows instead of rebuilding them, backed by open code in Git

Run everything on the Snowflake, Databricks or BigQuery you already own, with no separate engine

Keep business-built work governed: traceable to source, separated by team, access by invite or domain

Grow a knowledge graph from your catalogs that learns from every approved workflow

About Prophecy: since 2017, Prophecy has built governed, code-backed data workflows for the world's largest enterprises. DataGPT brings that engine into the AI tools your teams already use.

Try asking:

"Reconcile this month's invoice export against payments in Snowflake and flag mismatches."

"Count anything within $1 as a match, rerun, and share the unmatched list with my AP manager."

Get started free: sign up for a Prophecy account when you connect. If your company manages connectors, your admin can add it for everyone.
