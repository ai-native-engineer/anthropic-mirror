More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://www.ashbyhq.com/support)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://www.ashbyhq.com/resources/privacy)

Connect Ashby to Claude to get instant access to your ATS data including pipeline, candidate profiles, open roles, and applications. Ask questions, dig into hiring data, and take action by adding notes, moving stages, or sending emails directly from the conversation. Ashby connects Claude to your live recruiting data through secure user-level OAuth. Every request and action respects your existing permissions and your data stays protected.

## Tools

* describe\_object\_fields
* filter\_records
* search\_records\_by\_name
* get\_record\_details
* get\_candidate
* get\_interview\_plan
* get\_interview\_details
* get\_job\_pipeline
* get\_pending\_tasks
* get\_submitted\_feedback
* get\_upcoming\_interviews
* search\_product\_docs
* add\_note\_to\_candidate
* change\_application\_stage
* consider\_candidate\_for\_job
* create\_candidate

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
