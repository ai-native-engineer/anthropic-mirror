Lukka Prediction Markets Data MCP brings prediction market intelligence to Claude, normalized across several venues, including Kalshi and Polymarket.

Ask for markets on a topic, a specific event, or a price history and get back structured data: events with their outcomes, current and latest pricing with bid/ask, open interest, traded volume and trade counts over 1-hour and 24-hour windows, and OHLC price history per outcome. Because venues are normalized behind one interface, the same question listed on two platforms can be compared side by side.

Events are classified under PMCS, the Prediction Market Classification System - a hierarchy from category through supersector, macrosector and midsector down to subsector - so you can navigate by subject matter rather than by venue-specific tags. Embedding-based similarity surfaces related markets on the same underlying question, and each venue carries its Lukka VASP identifier, classification, jurisdiction and quality grade.

Typical uses: finding what markets exist on a given topic, comparing implied probabilities across venues, tracking how a market's pricing moved, measuring open interest and volume trends, and using market-implied odds as a signal alongside other research.

Lukka is a digital-asset data company serving institutional clients, and this connector exposes the same prediction market data behind its commercial products. Access is read-only.
