august builds digital twins of your users trained on your own data to think, decide, and respond the way each person actually would.

This connector lets you interview your twins one-on-one, run full focus groups or send surveys to whole segments directly in Claude.

Pressure-test messaging, validate a positioning shift, or get a read on a launch in minutes instead of weeks. Built for product, marketing, and research teams who need user insight at the speed of the work.

Getting your twins ready

No workspace yet? Just ask to "set me up on august" and Claude will create one on the spot (name it, pick your vertical, done). From there, connect your data and let august build twins from your user data.

Try these prompts first:

"Ask a power user what almost made them cancel last month"

"Run a focus group on our new onboarding flow and show me where the segments disagree"

"Survey trial users on the one feature they went looking for and could not find"

"Ask my support lead’s twin what is eating their week"

"How would a skeptical new signup describe what we do after five minutes?"
