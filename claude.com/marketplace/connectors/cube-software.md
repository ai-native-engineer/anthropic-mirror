Cube is the agentic FP&A platform that helps finance teams shape what happens next. The Cube MCP server brings finance to where decisions happen. Claude works directly on your governed Cube data, so every answer and every change uses the same definitions, models, and numbers your team already trusts.

Understand what is happening:

- Explore your model: companies, chart of accounts, and dimensions like Department, Entity, Product, Time, and Scenario.

- Analyze performance: budget vs. actuals, forecast variance, opex by department, revenue and margin by product, headcount vs. plan, and cash burn.

- Trace to the source: drill from any number to the transactions behind it.

Evaluate what should change:

- Model scenarios: duplicate a scenario and test assumptions without touching the plan of record.

- Extend your model: create formulas, dimensions, and tags.
