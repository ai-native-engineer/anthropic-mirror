Adspirer is your performance marketing and paid media agent — an AI performance marketer that works like a member of your team. Tell it what you want to achieve, and it understands the goal, builds the right campaigns across the right platforms, and runs them day to day.

Create campaigns through conversation, not dashboards. Adspirer turns an objective like "more demos under a $50 cost-per-lead" into well-structured campaigns across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok — then keeps working after launch. It paces spend so budgets land where you want them, shifts money toward what's converting, continuously harvests high-intent keywords and prunes the negatives draining budget, catches creative fatigue before it costs you, and flags anything that needs a human call.

You set the strategy and approve the moves — new campaigns start paused, so nothing goes live without you. Adspirer manages multiple brands and accounts, and it's always adding new ad platforms.
