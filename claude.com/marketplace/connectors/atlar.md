Atlar is the AI-native treasury platform for finance teams, unifying cash management, payments, forecasting, bank reconciliation, and investments across 100+ countries of bank and ERP connections. This connector lets Claude query your live Atlar data, e.g. bank accounts, balances, cash flows, transactions, payments, forecasts, and treasury policies—with the same role-based permissions and audit trail as inside Atlar itself.
