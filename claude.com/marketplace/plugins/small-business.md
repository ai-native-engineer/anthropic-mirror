Claude for Small Business gives owners 43 ready-to-run workflows for running and growing the business, inside the tools you already use. Describe the job in plain English or run a command, and Claude does the work. Every workflow starts in approval mode: Claude drafts and stages the work, and nothing sends, posts, or pays until you say so. Once you trust a workflow, you can let it run on its own schedule. Full spec here: <https://academy.claude.com/tutorials/how-to-install-the-claude-for-small-business-plugin>

**Run the business**

- `/monday-brief`: cash, sales, pipeline, your calendar, and the one thing that most needs you, on one page

- `/close-month`: reconciles your books against your payment processors and writes the close packet for your accountant

- `/plan-payroll`: checks you'll have the cash, chases overdue invoices, and gets the run ready for you to submit

- `/pay-the-bills`, `/restock`, and `/tax-prep` handle bills, reorders, and quarterly taxes or 1099s

**Grow the business**

- `/speed-to-lead`: answers new inquiries on nights and weekends with real meeting times and logs them to your CRM

- `/grow-pipeline` and `/call-list`: find prospects that look like your best customers, write outreach in your voice, and tell you who to call today

- `/marketing-monday`: what's working, what customers are saying, and what competitors changed

- Ask Claude to "turn this into a proposal," "plan next week's content," or "win back customers who stopped buying"

**Make it yours**

Run `/smb-onboard` (or say "set me up") and Claude learns your business and helps you connect your tools. If a tool you rely on doesn't have a connector, `build-connector` links it through Zapier. `build-agent` turns a task you do by hand every week into a workflow you can run by name or on a schedule.

**Connectors**‍

Works with 37 partner connectors, including Intuit QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Shopify, Salesforce, Gusto, Square, Stripe, Xero, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and Zapier. No connectors? Upload a spreadsheet or statement and most workflows still run.
