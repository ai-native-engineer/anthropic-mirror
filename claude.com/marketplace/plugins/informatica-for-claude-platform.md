Governed catalog discovery for Informatica Intelligent Data Management Cloud (CDGC): find tables, columns, files, glossary terms and policies across the enterprise catalog, check ownership, certification, sensitivity and applicable policy, and get safe-usage guidance grounded in catalog metadata before querying any data. Requires an Informatica IDMC tenant and the Informatica Catalog Discovery connector.

You can use Informatica to:

**Find governed data:**
"Find certified tables with customer revenue data and tell me who owns them"

**Check sensitivity before querying:**
"Is the customer\_email column in the billing schema classified as sensitive, and what policies apply to it?"

**Look up glossary terms:**
"What does 'active subscriber' mean in our business glossary, and which datasets implement it?"

**Get safe-usage guidance:**
"I want to build a churn report. Which certified datasets should I use and what usage restrictions apply?"
