Prepare for client meetings, draft follow-ups, review portfolios for rebalancing, build estate and tax briefs, and run compliance checks using data from your custodian, CRM, and planning platforms.

You can use Financial Advisors for:

‍**Pre-Meeting Prep:** "Prep me for my 2pm review with the Nguyen family"

‍**Post-Meeting Follow-Up:** "Draft the recap email and CRM notes from today's meeting with Sarah Chen"

‍**Portfolio Rebalance Review:** "Show me which households have drifted from their target allocation this quarter"

‍**Estate Brief:** "Summarize the Johnson family's estate documents before their annual review"

‍**Tax Brief:** "Put together a year-end tax brief for the Martin household"

‍**Compliance Check:** "Review this client email for compliance issues before I send it"
