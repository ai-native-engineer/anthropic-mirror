The Leadfeeder plugin turns the companies visiting your website into pipeline without leaving Claude. Leadfeeder shows you which companies are on your site and how well they fit your ideal customer. With the plugin, you can ask Claude to identify high-intent accounts, research buyers, find the right contacts, personalise outreach, and add companies to your lists.

Six skills cover the work from first signal to saved account. Getting Started runs a 30-second readiness check. Daily Visitor Brief surfaces the high-intent accounts on your site. Visitor Company Brief researches a company that visited. Find My Buyer finds the right contacts there. Outreach Companion helps you personalise your outreach. Add to Pipeline adds companies to your Leadfeeder lists. The plugin also includes the Leadfeeder connector, which gives Claude access to your Leadfeeder data.

**How to use:**
Install the plugin and sign in with your own Leadfeeder account. New here? Type “Set up my Leadfeeder brief” to run the readiness check first. Then describe what you want and Claude picks the right skill, whether that’s a brief on today’s visitors, research on a company that visited, the right contacts to reach, or a draft of personalised outreach.
