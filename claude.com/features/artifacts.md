### Artifacts

### What is an artifact?

Anything Claude makes for you that you’d put in front of someone: a design, a deck, a document, a dashboard, or a small interactive tool. It opens beside your conversation. You can edit it, come back to it, and share it with a link.

### Who can see my artifacts?

Only you, until you share one. On Team and Enterprise plans, links stay inside your organization unless an Owner or Primary Owner turns on External sharing in Organization settings > Artifacts. On Free, Pro and Max plans, you can share with anyone who has the link.

### Where do I make one, and how do I find it again?

Ask for one in any conversation with Claude, or start from the Artifacts tab. Everything you’ve made is saved in the Artifacts tab.

### Claude Design

### What’s changing with Claude Design?

You can now use Claude Design in any conversation with Claude, including in Claude Code and the Artifacts tab, with Claude Tag coming soon. All of its features, including on-canvas editing, come with it.

### I already use Claude Design. Does anything change for me?

No. You can use the new Claude Design in Claude Code, Chat and the Artifacts tab, or keep using the separate experience at claude.ai/design. Your existing projects stay where they are: the Design tab at the bottom of the sidebar lists them, and each one opens in standalone Claude Design.

### Claude Slides

### I used to make slides in Claude Design. Why is Claude Slides now separate?

Making slide decks is one of the most common things people do with Claude Design. Claude Slides gives it its own starting point, so it’s easier to find.

### Can I make a deck from a report or file I already have?

Yes. Share the file or point to the conversation, and Claude drafts the deck. You can then edit slides directly or ask for changes.

### Can I open my deck in PowerPoint?

Yes. You can export your deck to PowerPoint and PDF.

### Who can see and edit my deck?

It’s private until you share it. Add people by name or email, or share with anyone in your organization who has the link, and give them view, comment or edit access. On Pro and Max plans, you can also share publicly. You need a Claude account to access an artifact that is shared with you. On Team and Enterprise plans, public sharing is off until an Owner or Primary Owner turns on External sharing in Organization settings > Artifacts.

### Claude Docs

### What is Claude Docs?

Claude Docs is for documents you write with Claude and your team at the same time. Claude can draft sections, ask questions, and comment on choices.

### How is this different from the docs tool I already use?

Claude Docs helps you write a document together with Claude starting from inside the conversation where you did the thinking. Rather than pasting in static screenshots, Claude uses your connected tools to embed interactive charts, diagrams, and graphs.

### Who can see and edit my doc?

It’s private by default. Invite people one at a time or as a group, and give view or edit access to only the people you invite, your organization, or anyone with the link. On Team and Enterprise plans, sharing with anyone who has the link is off until an Owner or Primary Owner turns on External sharing in Organization settings > Artifacts. You must have a Claude account to view and edit artifacts.
