More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://elf.care/mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](mailto:support@elf.care)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://elf.care/privacy-policy)

Connect your account to ask Claude questions about your published health results, including your doctor summary, biomarkers, imaging results, body composition data, and trends over time. Access is read-only and requires your consent.

## Tools

* get\_image
* get\_marker\_history
* get\_marker\_info
* get\_results

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
