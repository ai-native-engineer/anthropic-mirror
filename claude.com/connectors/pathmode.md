Pathmode MCP Server connects Claude Code, Cursor, and other AI agents to your product team's Intent Layer. AI agents get structured specifications (objectives, outcomes, constraints, edge cases), dependency graph analysis (critical path, bottlenecks, cycles), and workspace strategy context — so they build the right thing, not just any thing.

## Tools

* get\_current\_intent
* list\_intents
* get\_intent
* get\_intent\_relations
* search\_intents
* analyze\_intent\_graph
* export\_context
* sync\_context
* get\_agent\_prompt
* get\_workspace
* get\_constitution
* update\_intent\_status
* log\_implementation\_note
* record\_implementation\_finding
* create\_intent
* update\_intent
* query\_evidence
* create\_evidence
* link\_evidence
* verify\_implementation
* intent\_save
* intent\_export

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
