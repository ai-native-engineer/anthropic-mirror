Search, enrich and watch people and companies from a single conversation in Claude with Crustdata. Build TAM, prospect and candidate lists using 150+ search filters such as job title, industry, funding and more. You can also enrich people and company profiles with 350+ datapoints such as verified emails, full work experience, education, funding, headcount, web traffic, job postings and more.
