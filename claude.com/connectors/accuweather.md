AccuWeather® provides hyper-local weather forecasts with real-time alerts and detailed updates. Access Minute-by-Minute® precipitation forecasts, hourly and daily forecasts, and severe weather notifications.

Use features including MinuteCast®, RealFeel®, RealFeel Shade™ Temperature, and live radar to monitor current and upcoming conditions wherever you are. Search any location worldwide and explore historical weather data and climate normals.

Widget Features: Current Conditions, MinuteCast™, Today/Tonight, Hourly Forecast (12 hrs), Daily Forecast (10 days), Historical Weather, Interactive Radar Map, and Government Alerts.
