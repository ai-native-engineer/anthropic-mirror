The Gainsight Community MCP connects your Customer Community to Claude. Surface unanswered questions, identify content gaps, and pull engagement trends through natural language. Review moderation queues, browse leaderboards, and audit community structure without waiting on a custom export. Purpose-built for community managers who'd rather ask their AI than navigate multiple dashboards.
