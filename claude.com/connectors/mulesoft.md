The MuleSoft MCP server provides governed access to your entire IT landscape - APIs, Agents, MCPs & LLMs - to accelerate production ready AI, at scale.

Key features:

Scan - Automatically surface services across AWS Bedrock, Kong, Apigee, Google Vertex, and more, without manual cataloging.

Discover - Search and browse APIs, agents, MCP servers, and LLMs in one place.

Govern - Apply and enforce policies across your full landscape, including PII detection, semantic routing, and token controls, for the infrastructure you already run.

Monitor - Track traffic, cost, and performance across every service in a consolidated view.

The MuleSoft MCP server helps you scan, discover, govern, and manage your entire API, Agent, MCP, and LLM ecosystem.
