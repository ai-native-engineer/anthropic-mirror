The Meltwater MCP server connects AI tools and workflows to Meltwater’s media and social intelligence. Search billions of news articles and social posts, retrieve live metrics, trends, and sentiment, generate Boolean queries, and manage saved searches — all through natural language. Requires an active Meltwater subscription.

## Tools

* explore\_plus\_list\_searches
* listening\_create\_search
* listening\_delete\_search
* listening\_get\_search
* listening\_list\_filter\_sets
* listening\_list\_searches
* listening\_update\_search
* unified\_retrieval\_document\_retrieval\_tool
* unified\_retrieval\_query\_gen\_tool
* unified\_retrieval\_statistics\_retrieval\_tool

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
