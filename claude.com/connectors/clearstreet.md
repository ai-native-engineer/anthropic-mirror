Clear Street MCP lets you access your Clear Street account information such as balances, positions, orders, and portfolio history along with real time market data and finance news. Use it to research opportunities, analyze portfolio performance, assess risk scenarios and evaluate sophisticated trading opportunities and hedging strategies.
