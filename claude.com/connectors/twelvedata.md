Twelve Data gives Claude instant access to global financial market data through a single, AI-powered interface. Describe what you need in plain English and the connector automatically selects the right endpoint, builds the parameters, and returns clean results.

What you can do:

- Get real-time and historical OHLCV prices for stocks, forex pairs, cryptocurrencies, ETFs, commodities, and bonds across 50+ global exchanges

- Calculate 100+ technical indicators: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, EMA, SMA, ATR, Stochastic, Ichimoku, SuperTrend, VWAP, and more

- Access company fundamentals: income statements, balance sheets, cash flow, earnings history, analyst ratings, insider transactions, and key executives

- Monitor earnings calendars, IPO calendars, dividends, splits, and market movers

- Search and filter instruments by exchange, type, or country

Getting started:

A free API key is available at twelvedata.com. Paid plans unlock higher rate limits, real-time data, and premium datasets including extended fundamentals and global exchange coverage.

Twelve Data is used by thousands of developers, quant researchers, and financial professionals worldwide.
