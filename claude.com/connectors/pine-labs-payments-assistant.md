Pine Labs Payments Assistant connects Claude to your Pine Labs merchant account so you can ask questions about your payments data in plain language and get straight answers — without hopping between dashboards.

Once connected, you can:

Look up orders and transactions — find an order by Pine Labs order ID or your own merchant reference, pull orders and refunds over a date range, or search a transaction by ID, and see full status and payment-method breakdown.

Track settlements — check settlement status, amounts, and which transactions make up a given payout, by UTR or across a date range.

Monitor payment links — retrieve payment link details and current status, individually or as a date-range report.

Manage recurring payments — review subscription plans, subscriptions, and subscription presentations, with filters for status, frequency, amount, and date.

Monitor performance — check your transaction success rate over a recent window.

Ship integrations faster — generate Pine Labs checkout integration code for your stack (Django, Flask, FastAPI, Express, Next.js, or Gin) and pull the right API reference from the live OpenAPI specs.

Built for merchants, finance and operations teams, and developers integrating Pine Labs.

Access uses OAuth — you sign in with your Pine Labs credentials, and Claude can only see data for accounts you authorise. The payments, settlement, subscription, and reporting tools are read-only: they retrieve and explain your data. The integration tools read your project's dependency files to detect your stack and generate code for you to review before applying. No tool in this connector can move funds, issue refunds, or modify your account.
