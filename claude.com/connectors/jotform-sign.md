Create, share, and edit e-sign documents right inside Claude with Jotform Sign.

Generate agreements, forms, and other signable documents from simple prompts, then customize fields, recipients, signing order, and workflows through conversation. Send documents for signature and streamline approvals with legally binding e-signatures that work on any device.

Perfect for creating contracts, onboarding paperwork, consent forms, and other documents that require signatures — without starting from scratch.

Key Features

Generate signable documents from natural language prompts

Create contracts, agreements, forms, and approval documents in minutes

Add signature fields, dates, initials, and other fillable fields

Configure recipients, signing order, and approval workflows

Send documents for secure, legally binding e-signatures

Track document status and manage signed documents in one place

Sign and complete documents from any device

Create e-sign documents for all use cases, including:

Employment contracts and onboarding documents

Client agreements and service contracts

NDAs and confidentiality agreements

Consent and waiver forms

Sales proposals and approval workflows

Vendor and partnership agreements

Rental and lease agreements

Internal approvals, policies, and acknowledgments
