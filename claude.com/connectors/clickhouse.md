More[Documentation (opens in new tab)](https://clickhouse.com/docs/cloud/features/ai-ml/remote-mcp)[Support (opens in new tab)](https://clickhouse.com/slack)[Privacy policy (opens in new tab)](https://clickhouse.com/legal/privacy-policy)

Connect Claude to your ClickHouse Cloud databases. Browse organizations, services, databases, and table schemas. Run read-only SQL queries against your data and get instant analytical answers. Monitor service backups, review billing costs, and inspect ClickPipe configurations - all through natural conversation.

## Tools

* get\_organizations
* get\_organization\_details
* get\_services\_list
* get\_service\_details
* get\_organization\_cost
* run\_select\_query
* list\_databases
* list\_tables
* list\_service\_backups
* get\_service\_backup\_details
* get\_service\_backup\_configuration
* list\_clickpipes
* get\_clickpipe

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
