Craft.io is a product management platform that gives Claude persistent, structured context about your product - work items, customer feedback, and strategic OKRs - so it can reason about your product from high-level strategy down to granular execution, across every conversation.

With the Craft.io MCP server, Claude works with full awareness of your product context: it can query and manage backlog items, draft PRDs grounded in real customer evidence, and prioritize work across workspaces and portfolios - all without leaving your AI workflow.

Capabilities include:

Backlog management: Create, update, and prioritize work items across all your workspaces, including statuses, priorities, sprints, labels, and custom fields.

Specs: Draft PRDs and break them into user stories with full awareness of your feedback, OKRs, and existing work items.

Feedback triage: Submit, update and analyze customer feedback to surface feature requests, cluster themes, and connect insights directly to work items.

Strategy: Align tactical work with high-level strategy by managing portfolios, initiatives, objectives, and key results.

Execution planning: Build data-driven roadmaps, plan sprints against team capacity, and map dependencies between items.
