Roam Research is an outliner-based, networked note-taking tool. This connector lets Claude work directly with your selected Roam graphs.

Claude can:

- Read and search your pages, blocks, daily notes, comments, and backlinks

- Run structured and Datalog queries across your graph

- Create and edit pages, blocks, and daily-note entries, add comments, and reorganize blocks

- Append or capture information to your encrypted graphs

At the start of work in a graph, Claude loads that graph's guidelines, including your naming conventions, page structure, and recorded preferences, so its reads and writes match how you actually use Roam instead of imposing a generic format.

You stay in control:

- Choose which graphs to connect and what access level each graph gets

- Every change Claude makes is attributed to a distinct "(AI)" author in your graph, so AI edits are always easy to find and review

- Adjust a graph's access or disconnect it at any time

To get started, add the connector, sign in to Roam, and pick which graph(s) Claude should use.
