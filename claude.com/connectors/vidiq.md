vidIQ gives Claude access to creator intelligence and content workflows across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Research YouTube keywords, trends, outlier videos, channels, competitors, transcripts, comments, and performance data. Analyze connected channels with YouTube Analytics, generate titles, scripts, clips, and comment-reply drafts, find stock B-roll, compose and edit media, render motion graphics, and manage competitor watchlists. Discover public Instagram profiles and reels, and search Instagram and TikTok outlier content and creators.

Data for Instagram and TikTok comes from a mix of third-party sources and vidIQ’s own first-party collection. Instagram data is not sourced through Meta’s official API.
