The Cortellis CMC Intelligence MCP connects Claude to Clarivate’s trusted, human-curated global regulatory CMC content, covering both pre- and post-approval requirements for small molecule and biologic drug products. It enables users to ask natural language questions and provides accurate, referenced answers supported by authoritative regulatory sources. Users can access subscribed and licensed content and navigate complex guidance directly within Claude. Designed for life sciences and regulatory teams, this connector delivers fast, transparent, and compliant insights, helping users accelerate research, reduce manual effort, and make better-informed decisions.
