The official connector for Ninout, the platform behind Ask One, CREATIVE SURVEY, and Fan Fan Fan. Design, build, publish, and analyze forms entirely through conversation with Claude — no admin screen required.

What you can do:

- Design a form's structure, questions, choices, and branching logic interactively

- Create and publish forms for a wide range of use cases, from surveys to inquiry forms

- Analyze the structure and branching of existing forms

- Aggregate and analyze collected responses

- Configure AI-assisted input support, external data lookups, webhook notifications, and external service integrations

From a simple contact form to complex questionnaires with conditional branching and validation, Claude can build it through conversation — and once responses come in, analyze them right in the chat.

An account for Ask One, CREATIVE SURVEY, or Fan Fan Fan and a contract for the Ninout MCP option are required.
