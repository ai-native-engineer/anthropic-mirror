Smarter Drafter MCP connects Claude to your firm's document automation engine, so you can draft client and courtroom ready documents through natural conversation. Describe what you need in a prompt. Claude identifies the right automation from your Smarter Drafter account, retrieves the full field schema, and asks only the questions required to complete it. Your answers flow directly into Smarter Drafter's assembly engine, and the finished document is returned in the conversation as PDF, DOCX, or EML.

Every word in the output comes from your firm's approved precedents. Claude gathers the information and selects the template, but the document is always generated deterministically by Smarter Drafter, identical to completing the form manually. No hallucinated clauses, no fabricated citations.

## Tools

* create\_submission
* get\_fields\_by\_form
* get\_submission\_generated\_documents\_url
* search\_forms
* submit\_submission
* update\_submission\_answer
