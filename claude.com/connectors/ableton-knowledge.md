Ask Claude questions about Ableton Live, Push, Move, Note, Cloud, Link and Max for Live. Claude will search through product manuals, official release notes, knowledge base articles, and video tutorial transcripts to find relevant answers.

\*\*Experimental\*\* — This is a prototype and not an official Ableton product. Results may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Always verify important information against official documentation.

\*\*Privacy\*\* — All searches run locally on your machine. This tool does not make any network requests or API calls beyond what Claude makes itself.

\*\*We want your feedback!\*\* Share your thoughts: https://forms.gle/HtjJy7jGyuBGCtmk8
