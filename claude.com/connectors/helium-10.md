Access your Helium 10 data directly in Claude. Query keyword and ASIN data, analyze campaign performance and profitability, and extract insights from your listings and rank tracking.

Make Helium 10 a native part of your Claude workflows without switching between dashboards or exporting a single file. Built for active brands with real account data across Research, Ads, and Commerce.

Requires an active Helium 10 Diamond plan. Lower-tier accounts are not supported.
