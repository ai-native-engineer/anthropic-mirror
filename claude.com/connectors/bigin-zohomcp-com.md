The Zoho Bigin connector enables users to manage their sales pipeline and customer relationships directly through AI. It can help with:

Managing contacts, companies, deals, and pipelines

Creating, updating, and retrieving customer records

Tracking deal stages and sales activities

Managing tasks, notes, and follow-ups

Searching and retrieving customer information

Updating sales records and keeping pipeline data current

Supporting day-to-day sales and CRM workflows through natural language

## Tools

* ZohoMCP\_executeTool
* ZohoMCP\_getFeatures
* ZohoMCP\_getSchema
* ZohoMCP\_listTools
