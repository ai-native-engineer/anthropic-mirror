Use A.I. Slides by Brightdeck to create PowerPoint presentations right inside Claude. Describe the deck you need and Brightdeck generates a beautifully designed presentation with tables, charts, and illustrations that exports to native PowerPoint (.pptx) and is fully compatible with PowerPoint, Google Slides, and Keynote.

Brightdeck can also add slides to a deck you already have or edit your existing decks. New slides match your existing fonts, colors, and layouts, so the rest of the presentation stays exactly as your team built it.

Available features include:

- Generate a full presentation from a topic, outline, or prompt

- Turn documents, reports, and rough notes into a slide deck

- Add new slides to an existing PowerPoint without breaking its design

- Speaker notes on every slide

- Refine slide content in a chat-based conversation

- Export and download as PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF — fully editable shapes and text, not flat images
