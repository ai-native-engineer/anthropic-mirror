Pocket Capture turns field lead capture — QR codes, links, and forms at events, booths, and sites — into something you can just \*ask about\*. Connect it and talk to your own capture operation in plain language: who to follow up with, how a capture point is converting, whether adoption is growing, and whether anything in your setup is broken or going to waste.

It can also \*\*propose\*\* new capture forms and capture points for you — but it never creates anything on its own. Proposals land in Pocket Capture as drafts that you review and approve; nothing goes live until you say so.

The connector is \*\*read by default\*\* (every tool touching data tool is read-only), and it only ever sees your own captured leads and setup — never anything else. It's built \*\*write\*\* for the operator who wants more leverage over their pipeline, not more process.
