Tella is the easiest way to record and share great-looking videos - screen recordings, demos, tutorials, and talking-head videos.

This connector brings your entire Tella library into Claude, so you can edit and manage videos in plain language.

Ask Claude to trim a clip, cut a section by quoting the transcript, remove silences and filler words, or tighten a 10-minute recording into a 2-minute cut.

Claude can apply Tella's signature polish for you: automatic zooms, camera and screen layouts, backgrounds, blurs, highlights, overlays, and background music.

Key features:

- Edit by transcript: say "cut the part where I talk about pricing" and Claude finds and removes it

- Clean up recordings: remove silences, filler words, and mistakes in one request

- Auto-polish: generate zooms and layouts, add music, sound effects, and overlays

- Organize your library: search videos, manage playlists, tags, chapters, and thumbnails

- Share and ship: set thumbnails, add collaborators, and export finished videos without leaving Claude

Perfect for founders, educators, and teams who record often and want editing to take minutes, not hours.
