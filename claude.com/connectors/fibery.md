Once Fibery MCP is connected to your workspace, Claude can do almost anything you can do in Fibery yourself — read and write data, or reshape your workspace structure.

1. Read & query

Explore your workspace structure (spaces, databases, fields), query any database with filtering, sorting, and sub-queries, run full-text search across entities, documents, and comments, inspect activity history (who changed what, and when), and pull data from saved views with their filters applied.

2. Create & update

Create, update, and delete entities, move them through workflow states, assign people and link related records, write documents, add comments, and attach files.

3. Modify structure

Create spaces and databases, add fields of any type available in Fibery (including formulas), and build views — boards, timelines, calendars, gantt charts, forms, and more.
