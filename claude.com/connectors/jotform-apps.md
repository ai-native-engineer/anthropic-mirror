Create and customize fully functional apps directly in Claude. Simply describe what you want to build, and Jotform Apps generates a ready-to-use app that you can refine through conversation. Customize layouts, add forms and widgets, manage users and permissions, configure payments and workflows, and publish your app—all without coding or leaving the chat.

You can use the Jotform Apps connector to:

Create apps with natural language:

""Create a client portal where customers can submit requests, access resources, and track important information""

Customize existing apps:

""Open my 'Employee Onboarding' app and add a welcome page, onboarding form, and resource section for new hires""

Configure and publish apps:

""Update my 'Event App' with attendee registration, payment collection, and event resources, then get it ready to share""

## Tools

* create\_app
* edit\_app
* fetch
* get\_teams
* search
* mcp\_app\_request

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
