Isometric is the agentic certification platform for the industrial economy. Its AI agents work alongside human verifiers, reviewing every data point so expert judgment is freed for the calls that matter. From carbon removal and superpollutant reduction to low-carbon energy, fuels, and materials, Isometric brings them all onto a single platform.
