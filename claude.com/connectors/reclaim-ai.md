Reclaim.ai brings intelligent time management directly into Claude, helping you plan, protect, and optimize your schedule. With support for Microsoft Outlook and Google Calendar, it supports complex workflows like finding time to meet with coworkers, resolving conflicts on your calendar, and helping you block out time to get work done. All calendar changes are drafted for you to review holistically, with the option to visually see how your optimized schedule before applying it.
