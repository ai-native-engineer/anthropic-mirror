Connect Claude to RingCentral Team Chat to streamline your daily workplace communication, team management, and workspace organization without switching tabs. This integration is currently in Beta as we actively refine features and gather user feedback.

Key Features:

Messaging & Conversations: Draft, send, and reply to messages in teams or direct conversations directly from Claude.

Smart Team Discovery & Management: Search for relevant teams, join or leave teams, and manage team members (subject to user permissions).

Rich Content & Adaptive Cards: Send Adaptive Cards for rich, interactive, and structured message presentations in team chats.

Event & Task Management: Create, retrieve, and update shared artifacts, including team tasks, notes, calendar events, and code snippets to keep team workflows organized.

Designed for individuals and teams who want to leverage AI to automate administrative chat tasks, catch up on fast-moving discussions, and collaborate efficiently inside RingCentral Team Chat.

Note: As a Beta connector, functionality and server features may evolve based on community and user feedback.
