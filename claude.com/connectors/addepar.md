Addepar’s MCP connector brings portfolio data and intelligence into Claude, enabling investment professionals to ask questions in plain language and receive answers grounded in their firm’s Addepar data.

Analyze a household’s holdings, market value, asset allocation, and performance. Understand alternatives exposure across capital calls, distributions, NAVs, and unfunded commitments. Review transactions, identify trends across clients, or gather the data needed to prepare for a quarterly review.

Unlike tools that analyze accounts in isolation, Addepar understands the ownership structures and relationships connecting a client’s complete portfolio. This allows Claude to provide a consolidated household view across accounts and entities, including public and private investments. Permission aware access helps limit the information retrieved to what each user is authorized to see.

Simply authenticate with your Addepar account. No API keys or manual configuration required.
