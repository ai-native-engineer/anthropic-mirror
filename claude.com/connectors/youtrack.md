Connect Claude to YouTrack to find and summarize issues, create and update work, manage assignees, links, tags, and comments, log time, and search or update knowledge base articles.

Requires automatic OAuth client registration in YouTrack.

## Tools

* add\_issue\_comment
* change\_issue\_assignee
* create\_article
* create\_draft\_issue
* create\_issue
* find\_projects
* find\_user
* find\_user\_groups

* get\_issue
* get\_issue\_comments

* get\_user\_group\_members
* link\_issues
* log\_work
* manage\_issue\_tags
* search\_articles
* search\_issues
* update\_article
* update\_issue

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
