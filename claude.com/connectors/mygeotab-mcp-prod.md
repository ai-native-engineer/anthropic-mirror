Monitor vehicles, analyze trips and driver behavior, and stay on top of safety exceptions, diagnostics, maintenance, EV charging, and compliance across your fleet in MyGeotab. Bring that live data into Claude — then act on it: set up zones and rules, acknowledge faults, and generate reports without leaving the conversation. Surface speeding and utilization trends, answer fleet questions in plain language, and turn insight into action as part of your AI workflow.
