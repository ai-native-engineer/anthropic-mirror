Rally's MCP server connects Rally to your agentic recruitment workflow. Search your participant pool using natural language, manage studies and interview sessions, access recorded transcripts and survey responses, and draft new studies through conversation. All access respects your existing Rally permissions—your assistant sees only what your account is authorized to view. Designed for research teams who want to accelerate synthesis, recruiting, and study planning without leaving their AI environment.
