Connect Claude to your complete longitudinal health history and ensure every important detail is finally visible. Health Data Avatar helps you securely collect, structure, and search your medical data across lab results, handwritten letters, prescriptions, PDFs, scans, clinical notes, health journals, and soon wearables and apps. HDA parses files in multiple languages, summarises them in your preferred language, builds timelines, and turns fragmented records into AI-readable context. Review and edit transcriptions anytime, compare them with originals, and continuously enrich your health history so nothing gets lost.
