AdWhispr gives Claude competitor ad intelligence and campaign execution.

Research: ingest any brand's entire public ad library from Meta and TikTok. Find verified competitors actually running ads in your niche right now, search ads semantically by concept ("social proof", "founder story"), pull a brand's TikTok ads with the proven winners first, and get real Google Keyword Planner data for any topic.

Briefs: generate a full competitive brief on any brand longevity curve, top hooks, longest-running winners, format mix, and a positioning verdict, with a downloadable PDF.

Execution: connect your own Meta, Google, or TikTok ad account once via OAuth, then launch complete campaigns from chat, Google Search, Performance Max, TikTok video, and Meta single-image or carousel ads from your own creative library. Every campaign is created paused with a preview-and-confirm step, so nothing spends until you explicitly resume it. Read performance, adjust budgets, and pause or resume campaigns conversationally.

Free tier included, no API keys to manage. Try: "Who has the best ads in my niche?" or "Launch a Google Search campaign for my store at $20/day."
