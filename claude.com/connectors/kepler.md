Get citation-backed answers on public company financials, straight from SEC filings, earnings transcripts, and market data. Ask questions like "compare AAPL and MSFT gross margins over the last 4 quarters" or "build a 3-statement model for NVDA," and Kepler returns verified figures with every number traced back to its source document, page, and line item plus downloadable Excel workbooks for model requests.

Built for financial analysts such as buy-side analysts, equity researchers, and asset managers for all workflows where "probably right" isn't good enough.
