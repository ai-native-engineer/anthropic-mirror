Revealed is an always-on account intelligence layer for commercial teams selling into healthcare and life sciences. Connect it to Claude and pull your buying-group map, the latest change signals on your target accounts and the people in them, and ranked next-best-action recommendations — without leaving your workspace. Sales, Marketing, and RevOps teams use it to run daily triage, prep for meetings, prioritize accounts, and ground outreach in a real, recent signal. The connector is read-only: it surfaces account and signal data from Revealed into Claude and never writes back to Revealed or touches your CRM, email, or calendar. Access is scoped to your own Revealed workspace and account book. Requires a Revealed account.
