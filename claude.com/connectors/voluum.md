Connect Claude to Voluum, the performance-marketing tracking and analytics platform. Query campaigns, offers, landers, and traffic sources; run and break down reports; manage automation rules; and optimize paid-traffic campaigns — all in natural language. Uses your own Voluum OAuth login and acts on your behalf against the live Voluum API.
