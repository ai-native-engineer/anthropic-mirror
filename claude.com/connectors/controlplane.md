Control Plane's official MCP server, letting you deploy containerized apps across AWS, GCP, Azure, Oracle Cloud, and your own hardware as one platform. Ship a service and get a live HTTPS URL, add domains, databases, and autoscaling, right size workloads to cut cost, and diagnose failures from live logs, metrics, and events.
