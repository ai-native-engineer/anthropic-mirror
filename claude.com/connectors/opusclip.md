OpusClip turns long videos into ready-to-post short clips, right inside Claude. Submit a video file or YouTube URL for AI clipping, then read the resulting clips and transcripts, refine edits (reframe, auto-captions, censor sensitive moments), and organize clips into collections. Generate platform-ready social copy and schedule or publish clips to your connected social accounts - all from chat. It works on the footage you provide and supports complete workflows, from raw upload to a published short.
