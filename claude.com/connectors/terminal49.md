Terminal49 helps users find and monitor ocean shipments and containers, inspect routes and transport events, check supported shipping lines, and create new tracking requests.

## Tools

* get\_container
* get\_container\_route
* get\_container\_transport\_events
* get\_shipment\_details
* get\_supported\_shipping\_lines
* list\_containers
* list\_shipments
* list\_tracking\_requests
* search\_container
* track\_container

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
