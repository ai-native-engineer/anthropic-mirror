Connect Claude to your ElevenLabs workspace to build with ElevenAgents without leaving your chat.

ElevenAgents: create AI natural-sounding voice and chat agents for customer support, phone answering, appointment scheduling, billing questions, and lead qualification by describing what you want.

Update prompts, voices, languages, and first messages. Compare configurations, duplicate your best performers, and get shareable widget links to deploy. Review conversations and read transcripts, explore the topics your agents handle, and estimate LLM usage and cost before making changes.

Sign in with ElevenLabs to get started.
