Connect your Webull account to Claude and explore global financial markets through natural conversation. Ask about account balances, positions, real-time quotes, historical price bars, K-line charts, futures and options data, analyst ratings and price targets, or manage your watchlists — all without leaving the chat.

Webull MCP brings institutional-grade market intelligence to your fingertips, combining real-time data, deep analytics, and portfolio visibility with bank-level account security, so you can research, monitor, and act with confidence.
