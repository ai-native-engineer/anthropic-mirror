Wispr Flow brings your meetings into Claude.

Connect Wispr Flow to let Claude work directly with everything you capture — recorded meetings, transcripts, scratchpad notes, calendar events, and action items. No copy-pasting, no switching tabs. Just ask.

What you can do:

- Search and recall meetings — "What did we decide about pricing last week?" Claude finds the meeting, reads the transcript, and answers.

- Summarize and extract — Pull action items, decisions, and open questions from any recording or series of meetings.

- Stay on top of your calendar — Look up upcoming meetings, resolve calendar invites, and prep before you walk in.

- Capture and track tasks — Create, update, and search action items so follow-ups never slip.

- Work across your notes — Search and reference scratchpad notes alongside your meeting history.

How it works:

Once connected, Claude can query your Wispr Flow account in natural language — surfacing the right meeting, transcript, or note exactly when you need it. Everything stays tied to your account, accessed only when you ask.

Whether you're recapping a standup, prepping for a 1:1, or chasing down a decision from three weeks ago, Wispr Flow gives Claude the context to actually help.

Get started with Wispr Flow at wisprflow.ai.
