Metricool connects Claude to your social media accounts, letting you schedule and manage posts across 13 networks — including Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Bluesky, and Threads. Get AI-powered best time to post recommendations based on your historical data, pull detailed analytics and reports on followers, reach, engagement, and impressions, manage Facebook and TikTok Ads performance, and handle multiple brands and accounts — all through natural conversation. Analyze competitors, get insights, create content and schedule to be published directly on Social Media.

You can also use Claude Code to generate carousels and assets and send it directly to Social Media Platforms.

Automate your tasks, for better Social Media Management.

## Tools

* createScheduledPost
* getAnalyticsAvailableMetrics
* getAnalyticsDataByMetrics
* getBestTimeToPostByNetwork
* getBrandSettings
* getScheduledPosts
* updateScheduledPost

Only use connectors from developers you trust. Anthropic does not control which tools developers make available and cannot verify that they will work as intended or that they won’t change.
