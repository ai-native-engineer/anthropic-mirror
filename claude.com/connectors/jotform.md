Create, edit and manage forms, view and analyze form submissions through Claude.

Instead of complex builders, simply describe what you need and watch the form take shape. Build quizzes, manage RSVPs, collect feedback, and more without hassle.

You can search and retrieve forms, view submissions in real time, filter responses using natural language, and get instant insights. This enables faster analysis and more efficient form management: all without leaving your chat.

## Tools

* form\_list
* create\_form
* edit\_form
* create\_submission
* get\_submissions
* search
* fetch
* assign\_form
* list\_submissions
* display\_form
* analyze\_submissions
