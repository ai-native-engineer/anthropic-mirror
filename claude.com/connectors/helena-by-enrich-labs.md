Helena by Enrich Labs connects Claude to an autonomous AI marketing agent that plans and executes growth marketing across connected channels from a single chat.

What Helena does:

- Paid ads: analyzes performance and, when requested, launches, manages, and optimizes campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, and TikTok Ads.

- SEO: researches keywords and drafts or publishes blog posts and landing pages.

- Social media: drafts, schedules, and publishes posts for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, and Facebook.

- Analytics: tracks cross-channel performance and reports on traffic, leads, conversions, CPA, and ROAS.

Helena works with the brand and integrations already connected to your Enrich Labs account. Each reply returns the final answer, relevant links, actions and tools used, and a sessionId to continue the conversation.

The connector does not provide AI image, video, or audio generation.

Helena is the default agent. Enterprise plans can also route requests to Angela for email, Kai for social listening, Sam for SEO and content, or Paul for paid advertising.

The connector can also execute arbitrary shell commands in an isolated sandbox with outbound network access.
