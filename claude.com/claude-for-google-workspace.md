FAQ

### What is Claude for Google Workspace?

Claude for Google Workspace is an add-on to use Claude in Google Slides, Google Docs, and Google Sheets that allows you to bring in Claude to Google Workspace apps, or you can use your files directly in Claude.

### How does this compare to Claude for Microsoft 365?

If your organization uses both suites, the sidebar will feel familiar. Claude for Google Workspace covers Docs, Sheets, and Slides, and Claude for Microsoft 365 covers Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook. Teams can use Claude in whichever suite they work in.

### How do we install it, and who gets access?

You can install Claude from the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/claude/12459801340), or your Google Workspace admin can deploy it to your domain or selected groups from the Google Admin console. Once it’s installed, open a file and choose Extensions, then Claude, then Open Claude.

### What can Claude access?

Claude can read the file you open it in, including any text, cells, or slides you’ve selected, plus connectors your organization has enabled. Its access matches your existing Google sharing permissions, so it only reaches what you already can.

### How is our data handled?

Our [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and [Commercial Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms) cover how we handle your data, including what comes from Google Workspace. They’re the best place to see what applies to your organization, and your account team can walk you through them.
