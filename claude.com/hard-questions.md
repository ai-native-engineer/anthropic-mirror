# There’s hope in hard questions.

Anthropic exists to answer the hardest questions about AI: safety, governance, its societal and economic impacts. We build Claude so that people and organizations can answer their own. As a public benefit corporation, we do this work in the open by listening to the hard questions people have, and [seeking to answer them](https://www.anthropic.com/path-to-hope). These questions belong to everyone, and so does the future we're building towards.

We traveled across the United States to hear people’s questions, concerns, and hopes about AI. This is what they shared.
