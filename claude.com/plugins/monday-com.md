* "What's overdue on my sprint board - show a status breakdown and flag any blockers"
* "Create a project tracker for the product launch with groups for Planning, In Progress, Review, and Done"
* "Add a high-priority task to the tracker, assign it to me, and move it to In Progress"
* "Summarize this week's activity on the Product board and flag anything that's stuck"
* "Build a request form for the design team - project name, deadline, and type of asset needed"
