What is ISO 27001?
ISO 27001 is an international standard for information security management systems. It provides requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an organization's information security management.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=nb41ha1o8bz8ceszpjvn5
What can customers assume about Anthropic based on this ISO 27001 certificate?
Customers can have confidence that Anthropic takes a proactive, structured approach to information security. ISO 27001 certification validates that we have implemented a comprehensive information security management system to protect our own and our customers' information assets.  Refer to our published Statement of Applicability on the [Trust Portal](https://trust.anthropic.com/) for the full list of controls in scope for this ISO standard.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=1r5f2mjxhj3rh7bdw952ov
How will Anthropic maintain its ISO 27001 certification over time?
The ISO 27001 certification requires ongoing maintenance and recertification audits to ensure continued adherence to the standard. Anthropic is committed to maintaining this certification as a long-term demonstration of our focus on information security. We have established internal teams and processes to monitor our information security management systems, conduct regular internal audits, and drive continuous improvement. We'll undergo annual surveillance audits and recertification audits every three years by an accredited third-party certification body to maintain our ISO certification. As AI technology rapidly evolves, we'll keep enhancing our management systems to address new risks and requirements. Maintaining ISO certification is not a one-time exercise but an ongoing commitment - one we believe is essential for building and maintaining trust with our stakeholders.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=6t9dw4u98kmxjml5y95q0v
How can stakeholders learn more about Anthropic's ISO 27001 certification?
We'll be publishing information about our ISO 27001 certification on our website at [anthropic.com](https://anthropic.com), as well as our [Trust Portal](https://trust.anthropic.com/). This will include the issued certificate, an overview of the standard, what it entails, and how it reinforces our commitments to information security. For those interested in the details of the [ISO 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) standard, it is available for purchase on the [International Organization for Standardization (ISO) website](https://www.iso.org/standards.html). We encourage all organizations to review the standard and consider how it can be applied to enhance information security practices. Achieving this certification is a major milestone for Anthropic, but it's just one step in our ongoing commitment to information security. We'll keep engaging with stakeholders, sharing our progress and lessons learned, and working to maintain the highest standards as we strive to shape the future of AI in a positive direction.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=tmbufxmg90j9jbjy5pltj

What is ISO 42001?
ISO 42001 is an international management system standard that outlines requirements and controls for organizations to promote the responsible development and use of AI systems. It helps organizations establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an AI management system.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=f6wc05rqu8mlk3eegq1ffk
How does Anthropic’s ISO 42001 certification relate to our Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)?
Our achievement of an ISO 42001 certification serves as a natural extension of our [Responsible Scaling Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy), reinforcing our commitment to responsible AI development and governance. Our RSP asserts our commitment to establishing, monitoring, and responding to AI safety thresholds. ISO 42001 validates Anthropic, in its role as an AI developer and AI service provider, has met a verifiable standard in its work to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve its responsible development and use of AI systems. Since the RSP is a voluntary commitment, there are no independent bodies following an accepted accreditation standard to certify our effective implementation of the RSP. The ISO 42001 certification bridges the gap between our internal diligence and commitment to AI safety and the public’s ability to view our effective implementation of this commitment. By aligning our existing RSP efforts with ISO 42001 requirements, we invite independent scrutiny of our practices and strengthen our commitment to continuous improvement.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=8k57j06vtm1ag9zwbomnn

How long is data retained?
By default, Anthropic retains all Customer Data within the Claude for Work platform until the data is deleted by the customer. After deletion, we may retain for up to 30 days in our systems. Prompts/responses that have been flagged as violating our Acceptable Use Policy may be retained longer.

What is Claude for Government (C4G)?
Claude for Government (C4G) is Anthropic's FedRAMP High authorized solution specifically designed for U.S. federal agencies and regulated environments. C4G implements NIST 800-53 security controls at the High baseline and offers the core capabilities as Claude for Enterprise, but with additional security measures to meet federal compliance requirements. Any customers who operate in regulated environments that have requirements aligned with FedRAMP High or NIST 800-53 can use C4G
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=wzlwnhafz2jsazfmz7ang
Are Claude’s models FedRAMP authorized?
FedRAMP and similar federal compliance frameworks apply to Infrastructure (IaaS), Platform (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS) offerings, not to the AI models that operate within them. Claude models are components rather than cloud service offerings, and therefore aren't subject to FedRAMP authorization requirements. For FedRAMP High compliance, customers can either use Claude for Government (C4G), Anthropic's FedRAMP-authorized SaaS product, or access Claude models through partner cloud environments that maintain their own FedRAMP authorizations, such as AWS GovCloud or Google Cloud with Assured Workloads enabled.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=rdmttek91vydppjk1y2xib
How do I find Claude for Government (C4G) in the FedRAMP Marketplace?
To find Claude for Government in the FedRAMP Marketplace: 1. Visit the FedRAMP Marketplace website (marketplace.fedramp.gov) 2. Search for "Palantir" in the search box 3. Find and click on "Palantir Federal Cloud Service (PFCS) - FedRAMP Cloud" in the search results 4. Scroll down in the PFCS record to the services section 5. You'll see Claude for Government (C4G) listed as one of the recently FedRAMP High authorized services under the Palantir Federal Cloud Service This listing structure reflects our partnership with Palantir's FedStart program for FedRAMP authorization.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=t9b2k7v60659sh49bra0h
Why is Claude for Government (C4G) listed under Palantir in the FedRAMP Marketplace?
Claude for Government is listed under Palantir because Palantir serves as our FedRAMP managed service provider (MSP) through their FedStart program. This arrangement works similarly to how major cloud providers offer GovClouds for government services. In this model, C4G is deployed within Palantir's FedRAMP Platform as a Service (PaaS), running on Google’s FedRAMP High Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS). We've implemented all required FedRAMP High Software as a Service (SaaS) security controls for C4G. Our System Security Plan (SSP) and complete Authorization to Operate (ATO) package are included within the Palantir Federal Cloud Services (PFCS) authorization. All documentation is readily available to authorized federal customers via the FedRAMP Marketplace.
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=xyg4eshqru059hvdnnxfq

How does Anthropic’s Frontier Compliance Policy relate to our Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)?
The [Responsible Scaling Policy](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/872c653b2d0501d6ab44cf87f43e1dc4853e4d37.pdf) (RSP) is our voluntary safety framework, reflecting what we believe best practices for managing catastrophic risks should be, even when that goes beyond current regulatory requirements. What is the Frontier Compliance Framework?
As AI governance frameworks emerge globally, we are committed to transparency about how our safety practices align with regulatory expectations. To formalize how we meet our obligations under these emerging regulations, we have developed the Frontier Compliance Framework (FCF). The FCF documents our current technical and organizational protocols for systemic risk assessment and mitigation across key risk categories, including cyber threats,  CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear) risks, harmful manipulation, and sabotage and loss of control risks.  The FCF is our public compliance framework for various applicable regulatory regimes, including:  - In the United States, the FCF serves as our Frontier AI Framework under California's Transparency in Frontier AI Act (TFAIA), documenting Anthropic PBC's technical and organizational protocols to manage, assess, and mitigate catastrophic risks. - In the European Union, Anthropic Ireland Limited has signed the General-Purpose AI Code of Practice (the EU Code), and the FCF serves as the publicly available summarized version of our Safety & Security Framework, describing how we assess and mitigate systemic risks and ensure adequate cybersecurity protection for in-scope models under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the EU AI Act).
https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=iv303n8xzs12d287m0jode
Is Anthropic willing to sign a BAA?
After review of HIPAA-related compliance items and the customer's use case, Anthropic may provide a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) covering use of eligible products. Our BAA only covers eligible products defined in the [Implementation Guide for HIPAA Entities](https://trust.anthropic.com/resources?s=rgirr4qe8u7ek8c2igx3&name=claude-for-enterprise-hipaa-ready-offering-implementation-guide), including Claude Enterprise Chat, Anthropic’s native 1P API, and (for qualified accounts) Claude Code with Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enabled. Our BAA does not cover beta products or Free, Pro, Max, or Team plans. See our [Implementation Guide for HIPAA Entities](https://trust.anthropic.com/resources?s=rgirr4qe8u7ek8c2igx3&name=claude-for-enterprise-hipaa-ready-offering-implementation-guide) for more information.
