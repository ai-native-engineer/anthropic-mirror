# How data retention works when using Claude

[![How data retention works when using Claude](https://img.youtube.com/vi/PQGxYvkMobQ/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQGxYvkMobQ)

[00:00]
Mythos-class models like Claude Fable 5.1
represent a major increase in
intelligence and agentic capabilities.
With that increase comes the potential
for misuse from typical forms of
abuse such as fraud, all the way
to sophisticated cyberattacks.
The most sophisticated misuse can
spread across many sessions and
accounts, which is difficult to
detect one interaction at a time.
Seeing that pattern requires storing
data for a meaningful period of time.
For this reason, starting with
Claude Fable 5, the last thirty
days of prompts and Claude's outputs
are stored for safety monitoring.
Automated systems then analyze
that rolling window of traffic
for signals of serious misuse.
Any conversation history a product
keeps for you still follows your
organization's own retention settings.
Anthropic does not train on enterprise
data without explicit permission,
and by default, no Anthropic personnel
can read the data used for monitoring.

[00:01]
Human review can happen
only in rare cases,
for example when an automated
safety system flags content as
potentially harmful, and only by
a small set of approved reviewers.
Any access is recorded
in a tamper-proof log.
A new solution called Enterprise Frontier
Safeguards combines the privacy of zero
data retention with state-of-the-art
safeguards for detecting misuse.
It was developed in collaboration
with more than a hundred customers,
and it gives eligible organizations
the option to store the data used
for monitoring in their own cloud
infrastructure, not Anthropic's.
The data can be stored under
your own encryption keys, access
policies, and audit logging.
When automated monitoring detects
a pattern that needs attention, an
alert goes directly to your team with
no Anthropic human review required.
Enterprise Frontier Safeguards
will roll out to customers in
phases starting later this fall.

[00:02]
To make the transitions smooth,
eligible organizations will receive
zero data retention on Claude Fable
5 and Fable 5.1 until
Enterprise Frontier Safeguards is ready.
For the details and to request access,
read the full announcement below.
