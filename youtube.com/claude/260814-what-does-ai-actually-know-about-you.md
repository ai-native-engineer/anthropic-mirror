So what does AI
know about you?
Every day people type
things into AI tools
they wouldn’t broadcast
publicly at work.
Medical questions,
money worries.
You should be able
to see and control
what your AI provider
retains about you.
You can think of them
as four different uses.
The first use is just
for this conversation.
It only knows what
you choose to put
in front of it.
The second use
is product memory.
Many AI tools can now
save useful details from
one chat to the next.
The third use is the
provider’s own systems.
Close the chat and
it’s kept on the
provider’s timeline under
the provider’s policy.
The fourth use is
training future models.
Your conversations aren’t
stored in the model to be
read back to other users.
Where providers train
future models on conversations
many let you opt out.
A few minutes
plus a little thought
about what you share
gets you most
of the way to using
these tools comfortably.
