explore the earth, born too soon to

afraid of AI? What's it going to do to
design?" One of the ways I like to
describe AI is AI makes writer's block
optional. You know, you discover
something really hard and personal
through writer's block and sometimes you
shouldn't even try to erase that. But
sometimes you should not be stuck and
often times the AI isn't giving you the
idea you used. But once you get some
feedback that helps you realize things
that you didn't yet have an opinion
