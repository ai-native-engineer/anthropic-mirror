## About Rosetree Solutions

Rosetree Solutions is an enterprise AI consultancy. Our work starts with understanding how a business actually runs - where the revenue stalls, where the work backs up, and what the team has already tried and set aside. From there we form a point of view on where AI can provide real value. Then we build it, own it through to production, and measure what changed.

That point of view comes from senior people, not a template. Our Forward Deployed Engineers work inside our clients' organizations rather than adjacent to them, with a decade of enterprise delivery behind the recommendations they make. The people in discovery are the people who build, and they stay through production.

Within our Claude practice, we work in three areas: Helping employees adopt AI, Changing how software gets built, and Deploying digital labor. Underneath the AI work sits a platform practice: enterprise data and Salesforce implementations across sales, service, marketing, and beyond, including customer data unification, and the integration work that makes an agent's context trustworthy. Agents fail more often on bad data and undefined process than on model capability, so we focus on getting the foundation right.

**What we believe:** Senior people from discovery through delivery. Small teams that stay with the engagement. Production is the finish line, not the pilot. And an honest read on where AI helps and where it may not - clients get our informed perspective, not a menu. From roadmap to ROI, we're here for the whole agentic journey.

## Services

Helping employees adopt AI.

Most of the value in enterprise AI shows up in the daily work of people already doing the job. We find where AI meaningfully changes efficiency, quality, and speed for specific roles, then build the workflows, guardrails, and habits that make adoption hold past the pilot.

Changing how software gets built.

We rebuild the software development lifecycle around AI-assisted engineering, with Claude Code in the middle of the loop. Teams ship faster, with better coverage and clearer review, because the mechanical parts of the work stop consuming senior engineering time.

Deploying digital labor.

We design, build, and operate AI agents that carry business processes end to end - qualifying and nurturing inbound demand, resolving customer inquiries, keeping systems of record accurate. These are production systems with monitoring, escalation paths, and clear accountability.
