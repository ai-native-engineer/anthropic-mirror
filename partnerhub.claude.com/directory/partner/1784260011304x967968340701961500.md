## About Praecipio

Praecipio is a technology consulting firm founded in 2006 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. We help enterprise organizations put AI to work, not as an experiment, but as a production capability that accelerates how they deliver. With more than 2,000 clients served, a 92 NPS, and a repeat business rate above 95%, our reputation is built on results that last.

We are a Select Partner in the Claude Partner Network Services Track, a distinction Anthropic extends to a small number of firms with the technical depth to deploy Claude at enterprise scale. Claude is the backbone of our own practice: We build our Praecipio Intelligence Framework on Claude, a proprietary knowledge engine refined continuously through hundreds of live engagements, on every project, every day. That framework powers much of what we do for our clients to deliver human expertise in AI service lines that cover strategy, delivery, engineering, and training using Claude.

That AI capability sits on top of a deep bench of enterprise technology expertise. We're also North America's largest pure-play Atlassian Platinum Solution Partner and a seven-time Atlassian Partner of the Year, with more than 474 certifications and specializations across Cloud, ITSM, and Agile at Scale. Clients including Netflix, Toyota, Walmart, Rivian, Zillow, and Kraft Heinz trust us with both their AI initiatives and their Atlassian ecosystems — often the same engagement.

With teams across North America and four consecutive years as a Great Place to Work, Praecipio pairs technical depth with a people-first culture. Whether you're building an AI practice from scratch, scaling one you've already started, or modernizing the platforms it runs on, we bring the experience to get it done right.

Turn AI potential into a plan your organization can actually execute.

- AI readiness assessments
- Strategy workshops
- Maturity evaluations
- Roadmap development

AI Delivery

Hands-on implementation that puts AI to work in your environment, with humans in the loop throughout.
Our delivery work is built around a simple principle: agents do the analysis, people own the output. We configure, integrate, and deploy AI capabilities within your existing toolchain, starting with the highest-leverage workflows and building from there. Every engagement is backed by The Praecipio Intelligence Framework, built on Claude, which means we're not starting from scratch, and you're not paying for us to figure things out on the job.
Typical Engagements:
- AI-augmented workflow design
- Tool configuration
- Agent deployment
- Delivery acceleration
