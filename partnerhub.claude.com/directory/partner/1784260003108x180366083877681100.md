## About Grid Dynamics

Grid Dynamics (Nasdaq: GDYN) is a digital engineering and enterprise AI transformation partner founded in 2006 and headquartered in San Ramon, California. With roughly 5,000 technical professionals across 19 countries, the company pairs the personal attention of a boutique consultancy with the delivery scale of a global engineering organization, serving over 30 Fortune 100 enterprises.

Services span artificial intelligence and agentic systems, cloud platform and product engineering, digital commerce and customer experience, AI and data platforms, and physical AI/robotics. Its GAIN platforms and Forward Deployed Engineering model embed AI-native delivery directly inside client teams, driving outcomes across retail, financial services, manufacturing and CPG, technology/media/telecom, healthcare and pharma, and insurance.

Core values center on customer-focused delivery, engineering rigor, and continuous co-innovation with clients — turning hard technology and business problems into scalable, revenue-driving results with predictable quality, speed, and cost from initial concept through enterprise-scale production.
