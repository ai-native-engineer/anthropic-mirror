## About The Agile Monkeys

**Production AI for industries where wrong answers aren't bugs — they're liabilities.**

The Agile Monkeys is an AI engineering studio and venture builder: 80 engineers and creatives, 14 years shipping enterprise software, Claude Partner Network Select partner, 37 Claude Certified Architects on staff — CEO included. We specialize in regulated industries: healthcare, insurance, banking, fintech, and financial services, plus public data and civic technology. These are environments where compliance, security, and speed can't be traded off — they have to hold up together.

**﻿How we engineer for regulated environments.** Compliance-as-Code: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and DORA treated as functional requirements — encryption, access control, and audit logging live in the codebase, secure by design and audit-ready by default. Traceable Reasoning: every agent decision backed by an explainable chain of thought a human auditor can follow, aligned with the EU AI Act. Governed Memory: agents that accumulate context with privacy by design, honoring PII residency. We were the first external vendor approved under Credicorp's AI governance framework and have passed two enterprise AI review boards on the first attempt.

**Proof in production.** Six-year relationship with UnitedHealth Group / Optum (Fortune 5, HIPAA), 30+ projects shipped and 500+ AI-assisted PRs shipping weekly; an idea-to-production platform that took delivery planning from weeks to minutes. A conversational pre-sale agent for Pacífico Seguros (Credicorp) that lifted funnel conversion +79% and cut days-to-close from 3 to 1.25. The public-facing AI assistant for USAFacts, adversarially tested for accuracy and bias. We stood up an emergency payments service in nine days during the largest healthcare cyberattack in US history. Flagship clients stay six years on average — three times the industry standard — and 90% of our proposals are accepted with zero outbound sales.

**Engage us the way your project needs.** Staff augmentation with senior engineers who act as force multipliers inside your teams. Forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) and architects embedded on-site or remote, turning signed contracts into board-reported outcomes. Autonomous delivery pods that own a workstream end to end. Fixed-budget, end-to-end project delivery — scoped, priced, and owned, from discovery to production. Training and enablement: Claude Code workshops for engineering teams, Claude and Cowork workshops for business teams, executive AI readiness programs. Ongoing support and managed evolution of what we ship. Engagements are built to scale: from one embedded engineer to full product teams, from a 24-hour prototype to multi-year platforms carrying Fortune 5 production load.

**Where we fit in Anthropic's capability map.** We deliver across all three pillars, with deep specialization in the regulated end of each. Smarter employees (Claude Code + Cowork + Claude Enterprise): Adopt — deployment, enablement, and change management to roll Claude out to the workforce; Transform — redesigning how employees work through SDLC modernization, knowledge-worker workflows, and MCP server, Skills, and plugin builds. Faster processes (Developer Platform API + Agent SDK): Adopt — upskilling engineering teams on the Claude API with onboarding, evals, RAG, and agent development bootcamps; Transform — rebuilding business processes with agent-powered KYC, claims, underwriting, and finance ops, measured on cycle time. Transformative products (Developer Platform API + Agent SDK): Build — designing and shipping Claude inside the client's own product, AI-native features and experiences that reach their end users, co-built with the client or as partner-owned productized IP.
