## About adesso SE

**adesso: turning Claude into production systems for European enterprises**

Founded in 1997 and headquartered in Dortmund, Germany, adesso SE is one of the largest IT service providers in the German-speaking market. The adesso group employs more than 11,300 people at over 65 locations across Europe and generated revenue of around EUR 1.47 billion in 2025. adesso optimises the core business processes of its clients through consulting, custom software development and its own software products — with deep vertical expertise in insurance, banking and financial services, healthcare and life sciences, the public sector, automotive, manufacturing, retail, utilities and transport.

Artificial intelligence is at the centre of adesso's strategy. In its dedicated Data and AI business line, specialists guide clients along the entire AI transformation — from AI maturity assessment and use case identification to GenAI assistants, agentic systems, data platforms and production operations. Complementary assets include adesso AI Hub, an AI governance platform for organisations with digital sovereignty requirements, and adSCAILE (adesso Smart Cycle for AI-enhanced Lightweight Software Engineering), adesso's own process model for agent-based software development following the principle that agents execute while humans steer — shortening delivery cycles from weeks to days.

adesso is Partner in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network and among the first companies worldwide admitted to this tier. Claude is used both internally and in client projects for code analysis and generation, architecture work and process automation, with Claude Code embedded in adSCAILE delivery cycles. More than 200 adesso AI experts are qualifying as Claude Certified Architect Foundation (CCA-F), covering model and deployment platform selection, agentic versus single-step architectures, and evaluation, cost and responsible use from day one.

As a vendor-neutral integrator, adesso deploys Claude on the platform that fits each client's compliance profile — including EU-hosted and sovereign options. With ISO 27001 certification, its own German-hosted business cloud and hands-on experience with BaFin, DORA, NIS2, TISAX and the EU AI Act, adesso brings Claude into environments where regulation, auditability and data protection are non-negotiable.
