## About LightCI

LightCI is a product engineering firm that builds production AI inside operating companies, most of them private equity-backed. Our work splits into two service lines.

**Product engineering:** we build Claude-powered products that become commercial revenue lines — autonomous agents, domain-specific models, AI-native platforms — taken from zero to commercial release inside a quarter.

**PRISM, our operating model for internal functions**: we install AI into finance, IT, legal, and operations on a 90-day arc — baseline agreed in writing, agents in production on real workflows by week eight, a full cycle measured against the baseline, and expansion only if the numbers earned it. Enablement, governance, and telemetry are built into every program, so adoption sustains after we leave and every agent action is logged and attributable.

Our core values shape how we deliver: senior teams only; engineers, product managers, and designers; production over pilots — we ship systems that run the business, not demos; and measured outcomes — every engagement starts with a written baseline and ends with results the CFO can verify.
