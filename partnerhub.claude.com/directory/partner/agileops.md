## About AgileOps

AgileOps is an Anthropic partner based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. We help businesses move from AI pilots to Claude running inside daily operations. We are also an authorized partner of Atlassian, Salesforce and HubSpot. This means we connect Claude to the systems your teams already use: Slack, Google Drive, Jira, Confluence, HubSpot, Salesforce and QuickBooks.

**How we help**

We cover the full Claude journey: choosing the right plan, deployment and governance, workflow integration, custom agents, and role-based training. After go-live, we stay on to monitor results and improve the solution as your needs change.

**Outcomes we bring to our clients**

- **Sales & Proposal Assistant:** drafts proposals and answers technical product questions from a curated knowledge base. Proposal time drops from 2–3 hours to 30 minutes, and inquiry handling is ~60% faster.

- **AI Project Manager:** collects data from task systems, documents and financials, then writes status and financial reports on a schedule. Reporting work drops from 4+ hours to almost zero.

- **Content Hub & Publishing Engine:** drafts blogs, social posts and eBooks from a single source of product truth. Teams produce 3–5× more content with the same headcount.

- **Organisational Knowledge Assistant:** answers staff questions from SharePoint, OneDrive and shared drives, with source references, so years of institutional knowledge become searchable in seconds.

- **Finance Workflow Automation:** automates tax declarations, invoice data extraction and ledger sync, which removes a high-risk source of data-entry errors.

- **Document Intelligence Engine:** reads architectural drawings, technical specs and long reports, then extracts measurements and quantities. It reached 91% accuracy on trial documents.

**Why AgileOps**

- 50+ AI and SaaS experts, including 10+ with the CCA-F certification.

- 100,000+ delivery hours and 500+ projects delivered.

- Clients across education, retail, technology and non-profits, including Meta, Oxfam and Haiha.

## Services

Claude Deployment & Governance

Get your team running on Claude in days. We set up workspaces, users, connectors (Google Drive, Slack, Microsoft 365, Jira), admin controls and a governance framework so you can scale use safely.

Workflow Integration

Bring Claude into the tools your teams already use: Slack, Jira, Confluence, JSM, HubSpot, Salesforce and QuickBooks. Claude works with your real data, inside your real processes.

Custom AI Agents & Apps

We build Claude-powered assistants and agents with the Claude API and MCP. Examples: proposal drafting, project reporting, knowledge search, content production and document data extraction.

AI for Business Training

A hands-on 12-hour program for non-technical teams, built on Anthropic's AI Fluency framework. Covers Projects, Connectors, Research and Cowork, plus an optional Skills track for power users.

Claude Licensing Advisory

Choose the right Claude plan (Team, Enterprise, Claude Code or API) for your needs and budget. We provide local invoicing and onboarding support.
