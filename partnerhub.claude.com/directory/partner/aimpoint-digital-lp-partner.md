## About Aimpoint Digital

Aimpoint Digital is an enterprise AI and data consultancy founded in 2017 and operating across the US, UK, and Colombia. Our teams combine AI engineers, data engineers, operations researchers, and management consultants, so a single engagement can move from opportunity identification through architecture, build, and sustained operation without handoffs between firms.

Our AI strategy work starts where value is measurable. We map enterprise AI opportunities against real value streams, decompose workflows to find the decision points where an agent changes the economics of a process, and build the ROI case before writing code. That work carries into operating model design. We build AI-native organizational structures that define where humans stay in the loop, how adoption is sustained, and how the workforce is enabled through structured training and change management, including prompt engineering workshops, Claude skills authoring, and Claude Code plugin development. The result is an actionable, value-centric roadmap that changes how an organization operates.

Our AI engineering practice takes those decisions into production. We build agent and multi-agent systems, RAG architectures, and context and memory engineering, integrated with the enterprise systems of record where operational decisions are made. Every system is built with the evaluation, observability, and guardrail infrastructure needed to operate it responsibly, as well as token, caching, and latency optimization to keep run costs manageable at scale.

As an Anthropic Select Partner, a three-time Databricks Digital Native Partner of the Year, and a Snowflake Elite Services Partner, we build the governed data foundation and the intelligence layer for Claude as one program.

## Services

AI Strategy, Transformation, & Roadmap

Enterprise AI opportunity mapping against real value streams, workflow decomposition to the decision points where an agent changes process economics, and ROI modeling before build begins, carried through into AI-native operating model design, Center of Excellence setup, human-in-the-loop workflows for regulated processes, and the change management that keeps adoption compounding.

Claude Agent & Agentic Workflow Engineering

Production agents and multi-agent systems on the Claude API, from subagent orchestration and task decomposition through rebuilding high-friction processes as governed Claude workflows, integrated via MCP into core enterprise systems of record.

EvalOps, AgentOps & Claude ThriftOps

A suite of EvalOps, AgentOps, and ThriftOps offerings for operating AI agents in production, including evaluation pipelines using AI-as-a-judge and human-in-the-loop review, continuous quality and drift monitoring, end-to-end observability, guardrails, and prompt, token, caching, and latency optimization to control costs at scale.

Enterprise Claude Activation

Rollout accelerator of Claude, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork with governance, RBAC, and FinOps controls established from day one, paired with practitioner led Claude enablement and training that covers 101 and 201 topics across core Claude products, as well as persona-based trainings across core business functions.

Decision Intelligence & Optimization

Claude agents that call production optimization and simulation solvers to make optimal decisions rather than plausible ones and paired with our operations research practice to build agentic systems integrated into your core decision flows.

Semantic Layer, Retrieval & Context Architecture

Semantic models, metric definitions, ontologies, and knowledge graphs that give Claude a governed vocabulary for the business, with RAG architecture, retrieval optimization, and context and memory engineering on your core data platform.

AI-Ready Data Platforms & Infrastructure

Databricks and Snowflake foundations backed by three consecutive Databricks Digital Native Partner of the Year awards (2024–2026) and Snowflake Elite Services Partner status. Design of the data architecture, governed pipelines and the data quality, lineage, and access controls that make enterprise data safely reachable by agents across the direct Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex, and Microsoft Foundry.

Forward Deployed Claude Engineering

Senior engineers embedded directly into client teams as the delivery vehicle for the practices above, bringing optimization, semantic, and platform expertise alongside internal staff and transferring capability as systems ship.
