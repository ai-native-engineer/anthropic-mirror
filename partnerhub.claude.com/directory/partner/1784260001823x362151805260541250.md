## About First Line Software

**First Line Software is an AI engineering company.**

We build, deploy, and run AI systems for enterprise clients in real estate, healthcare, and hospitality.

Most of our work falls into three practices.

**Race Mode** is our rapid AI delivery practice, built on a methodology we call RACE: Requirements (intent captured as executable specifications, not a loose brief), Architecture (senior engineers own the design, it isn't left to the model), Command/Execution (an AI-native digital workforce does the implementation against that spec), and Evaluation (automated testing and QA built in from day one, not bolted on after). The compression comes from parallelizing and reusing components — not from skipping the steps that make a system production-ready, which is the usual failure mode of a fast AI pilot.**MAIS (Managed AI Services)** is what happens after launch: monitoring, retraining, and operational ownership, since an AI system's behavior shifts over time and someone needs to be watching it, not just shipping it.**AI Enablement** is the practice of getting a client's own team working directly with Claude and agentic tooling — through training, custom Skills, and engineers embedded alongside their staff — so the capability doesn't stay locked inside a vendor relationship.

How We Deliver

**Engineering Track** — we turn intent into executable specs, our AI Product and Principal Engineer lock the architecture up front, and an AI-native workforce builds, tests, and documents against that spec — quality by design, not bolted on after.

We've applied this across real estate (portfolio analysis, lease intelligence, deal underwriting), healthcare (via our Clinovera subsidiary, on FHIR modernization and clinical workflows), and hospitality operations. As an Anthropic Select Services Partner, our reference point for success is whether a system is still running and being used three months after launch — not whether the demo landed well.
