## About Exadel

Exadel is an AI and engineering company with 25+ years of delivery and 2,000+ engineers in 30 countries. We help enterprises take Claude into production: from AI readiness and use-case selection to building agentic systems, integrating Claude with the systems they already run, and rolling it out to whole teams.

What we do with Claude:

Rollout and activation of Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude for Enterprise, with governance and enablement for engineering and business teams.Agentic platforms and customer-facing products built on the Claude API, with MCP integrations into the client's own systems.Process redesign and legacy modernization with Claude in the delivery loop.Token spend optimization and cost governance.Compliant deployments for regulated industries, including on Amazon Bedrock.

Our engineers own outcomes: production-grade engineering, compliant deployments, and the governance that regulated industries expect.
