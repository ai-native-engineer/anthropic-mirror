## About Bold Tech

Bold Tech is an AI consultancy specializing in Claude-powered internal tools and organization-wide AI enablement. We advise on and deliver secure, AI-driven internal systems that integrate with your existing data and UIs with role-based access, guardrails, and enterprise-grade governance built in from the start.

Our work spans four areas: application build-outs with AI primitives, custom MCP server development and integration, Anthropic product rollout and enablement (team provisioning, API integration, governed deployment), and standing up AI-first internal tool Centers of Excellence.

With 75,000+ hours of internal tools experience across 100+ clients - including Stripe, Ramp, Vimeo, and Chegg - we know where AI moves the needle, and we move fast.

## Services

AI Center of Excellence & Embedded Team Support

With deep experience running center of excellence engagements across 100+ clients of all sizes, we know how to build the systems, workflows, and habits that make AI genuinely useful inside an organization.
We advise on best practices, integrations, and secure data access, and build systems that scale with your growth. Our team embeds deeply into yours to support, build, and advise along the way.

AI Enablement & Team Training

We get your whole company productive with AI.
Fundamentals training and hands-on sessions run as breakouts for each business unit - so every function leaves knowing exactly how AI fits their day, not just the theory.

Use-case Identification & Roadmapping

We work with your teams to find the workflows worth automating and map them into a clear, prioritized plan. You come away knowing where AI moves the needle - and where it doesn't.

Custom AI Application Build-Outs

We design and ship production AI applications on the Anthropic API - agents, RAG systems, and internal tools that integrate with your existing data and infrastructure. Secure, governed, and built to run in the enterprise, not just demo well.
