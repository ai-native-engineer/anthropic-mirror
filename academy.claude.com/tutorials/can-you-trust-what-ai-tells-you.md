Ask an AI a question and the answer comes back confident, well-organized, maybe even cited. Can you trust it? This video asks you to reframe the question from a simple “yes” or “no” to something more nuanced. For example, you wouldn't question your doctor if they recommended eating more carrots, but you'd probably want a second opinion before surgery. Trust in AI works the same way. It's a dial you turn, not a switch you flip. You'll see the two ways answers go wrong: hallucination and sycophancy, and how to set your own level of scrutiny.
