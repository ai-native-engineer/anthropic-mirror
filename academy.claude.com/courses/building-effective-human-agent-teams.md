What you’ll learnBy the end of this course, you’ll be able to

* Explain how work changes when your team moves from single-player AI to multiplayer AI
* Distinguish the capabilities of a multiplayer agent from a traditional chatbot
* Identify the specific team norms that help multiplayer agents support your team effectively
* Apply the four principles of healthy human-agent teams: clear roles, a written north star, gradual release, and the right information access

Who it’s for

This course is for team leads and innovators whose organizations already use AI in single-player mode and want to make the jump to multiplayer AI. No technical background needed.

## About this course[](#about-this-course)

Most of us are using AI in single-player mode. We ask AI to help us complete tasks and it's a trusty assistant. As agents become more capable, many teams are moving from single-player AI to multiplayer AI. This looks a lot less like "donning an AI super suit" and more like building a human-agent team. Building human-agent teams requires intention and skill. This short course follows a team moving from single-player to multiplayer AI. This course gives you a front row seat into what the shift looks like so you can lead the way in your own organization when it comes to multiplayer AI. Of note, this course was created by a human-agent team. Humans wrote and researched all of the content and agents helped with refining animations and exercise ideas.
