# What a strong human-agent team looks like

Lesson 38 min

In this lessonBy the end, you’ll be able to

* Apply the four principles of healthy human-agent teams: clear roles, a written north star, gradual release, and the right information access
* Explain how work changes when your team moves from single-player AI to multiplayer AI

The best human-agent teams use very intentional team practices and norms.

## A human-agent team in action[](#a-human-agent-team-in-action)

The best human-agent teams have clear roles, a written north star, the right information access, and trust. While all of these things also support teams without agents, adding agents to the team makes them even more important. Because the rate of task completion on human-agent teams tends to be higher, the cost of misalignment and error is also higher. Setting the stage for success is critical.

## Try it: run your own launch[](#try-it-run-your-own-launch)

The north star is set. Decide who does each job and see how the week goes.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* **Work in public and give agents broad context.** For an agent, if it isn't written down and accessible, it doesn't exist.
* **Every human and agent gets a defined role with the right tools for the job.** One roster, one set of artifacts, and one working space, with human judgment on the most important decisions.
* **Set a north star to make agents more proactive.** Humans always set it. An agent that knows the goal can bring the team useful work toward it.
* **Build trust over time.** Grant autonomy in proportion to demonstrated reliability, then expand it deliberately.
