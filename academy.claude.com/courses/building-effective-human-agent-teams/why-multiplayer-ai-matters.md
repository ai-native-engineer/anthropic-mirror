# Why multiplayer AI matters

Lesson 17 min

In this lessonBy the end, you’ll be able to

* Explain how work changes when your team moves from single-player AI to multiplayer AI
* Identify the specific team norms that help multiplayer agents support your team effectively

Meet the Lantern team, a group about to launch a health tracker app. To date, they've been using AI in single-player mode. See how that impacts their work, especially as the team grows.

## From super suit to shared room[](#from-super-suit-to-shared-room)

Single-player AI is like putting on a super suit at work: it makes you faster on your own tasks, but information can get siloed easily. When agents and humans on a team work in a shared space together, that dynamic changes.

When someone asks AI to summarize the decisions from the meeting or update the calendar, every human and agent in the shared space can see it.

Over time, this becomes knowledge the team can build on. It allows humans and agents to work proactively in service of a common goal.

Drag the team size and switch how the team uses AI. Watch what gets duplicated.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* **Single-player AI speeds up an individual, but it can also silo information from the team.** Multiplayer AI ensures that humans and agents work side by side with shared context.
* **The faster a team grows,** the greater the impact siloed context can have on agent performance.
* **When agents and humans work together in a shared space,** agents are able to be more proactive and personalized.
