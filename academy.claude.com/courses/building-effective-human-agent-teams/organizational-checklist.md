# Organizational checklist

Lesson 48 min

In this lessonBy the end, you’ll be able to

* Identify the specific team norms that help multiplayer agents support your team effectively
* Apply the four principles of healthy human-agent teams: clear roles, a written north star, gradual release, and the right information access

Before you bring in multiplayer AI, take an honest look at how ready your team is.

## Five questions to assess your team's readiness for multiplayer AI[](#five-questions-to-assess-your-teams-readiness-for-multiplayer-ai)

Before you bring agents into the team's work, check five things. Is what people and agents need open and searchable, rather than sitting in private documents and DMs? Is it written down who owns what, agents included? Does every teammate have the tools for the job, agents too? Can an agent's key work be checked by a rubric, a test, or a second agent before a person sees it? And is there a written north star everyone can point to? Understanding where your team is at can help you plan a successful multiplayer AI launch in your organization.

## Try it: how ready is your team?[](#try-it-how-ready-is-your-team)

Rate five statements about your team. Your answers build a prompt you can take to Claude to turn the gaps into a plan.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* **Open and searchable.** If most of your team's work lives in private documents and DMs, agents will struggle to stay up to date.
* **Clear owners, agents included.** Without role clarity, work gets duplicated.
* **The right tools for every teammate.** An agent without access does thin work, guesses, or makes mistakes.
* **A check before a person sees the work.** A rubric, a test, or a second agent reviewing cuts the time it takes to finalize an agent's work.
* **A north star everyone can point to.** Without a clear goal, neither people nor agents can be proactive about what's needed next.

## Lesson reflection[](#lesson-reflection)

* Which of the five questions would your team disagree about most?
* What is private by habit on your team that could be open on purpose?
