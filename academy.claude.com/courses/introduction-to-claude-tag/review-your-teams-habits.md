# Review your team's habits

Lesson 1110 min

In this lessonBy the end, you’ll be able to

* Check one channel against the habits from each lesson
* Post the message that gets the missing habits started

After the course, use this with one of your channels in mind, ideally the one where your team hands Claude the most work: tick what you and your team already do with Claude there. It is fine not to have gotten to everything. Each "no" shows one thing to try and the lesson that covers it, and once every row is answered the checklist builds a message you can post to Claude in that channel to get the rest started with you.
