# AI roles as Delegation outcomes

Lesson 730 min

In this lessonBy the end, you’ll be able to

* Distinguish five common Delegation decision outcomes
* Make more intentional decisions about whether AI-generated material appears in the final work the audience encounters
* Map your own AI touchpoints to the roles they occupy

## AI Roles as Delegation Outcomes[](#ai-roles-as-delegation-outcomes)

AI Roles as Delegation Outcomes · 7 min

SummaryTranscript

This video names the five shapes a Delegation decision tends to take. They split along one clear line: whether AI-generated material appears in the final work the audience encounters. No AI, Consultant, and Tool Builder sit on one side of the line. Co-Creator and Medium sit on the other.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* Five outcomes: No AI, Consultant, Tool Builder, Co-Creator, Medium.
* The dividing line: does AI-generated material appear in the final work the audience encounters?
* The roles are a vocabulary for decisions, and each role has its own Diligence profile.

## Exercises[](#exercises)
