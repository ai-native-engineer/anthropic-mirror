## Description and Discernment for Your Practice[](#description-and-discernment-for-your-practice)

Description and Discernment for Your Practice · 9 min

SummaryTranscript

This video examines your own creative practice using the inner loop of Description and Discernment: articulating what you do, how you do it, and how you work, and using that articulation to create explicit standards to evaluate work against.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* AI cannot serve a practice you have not described, and you cannot evaluate output against standards you have not made explicit.
* Each layer of your practice, Product, Process, and Performance, runs its own Description and Discernment loop.
* Process standards live at the step level: that is what lets you judge whether AI at a given step served the work or undermined it.
* Performance here means your behavior as a working creator, and is the layer most affected by AI over time.

## Exercises[](#exercises)

### Practice Articulation Workbook[](#practice-articulation-workbook)

Put your own practice into words across what you make, how you make it, and how you work, then convert those descriptions into standards a peer could apply to work that is not yours. Finally, calibrate: test your standards against one of your older pieces to make sure they can actually tell adequate work from excellent.
