## Putting It All Together[](#putting-it-all-together)

Putting It All Together · 12 min

SummaryTranscript

This video assembles the full analytical flow using one illustrator's work and workflows to demonstrate how to move from articulating your practice, to analyzing your work using the lenses, to applying Delegation and Diligence to land at different outcomes for different pieces of work.

## Key takeaways[](#key-takeaways)

* There is no single position on AI usage; it is decided at the level of mode and task.
* Three questions anchor the policy: where AI fits and does not, what you disclose and to whom, and when you will revisit.
* A policy without revision triggers goes stale silently.

## Exercises[](#exercises)

### Your Personal AI Use Policy[](#your-personal-ai-use-policy)

Assemble everything you have built into one living policy. Decide the role AI plays across your modes of work, set your disclosure commitments by audience, name the triggers that will make you revisit it, then stress-test it against three real situations and sign it.

## Lesson reflection[](#lesson-reflection)

* Which single decision in your policy would have been different, or absent, without the analysis behind it?
* From your Starting Position Snapshot, what has changed in your stance, and what stayed the same? Did the policy answer, manage, or draw a line for the named concern?
