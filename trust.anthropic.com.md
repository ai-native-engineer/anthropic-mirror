Updated Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) for Claude Enterprise iOS App

Published May 21, 2026

We've published an updated Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) for the Claude Enterprise iOS app, dated May 2026. This update reflects accessibility improvements across the app, including:
 • Screen reader support: Improved VoiceOver reading order through conversations, screen titles are announced on navigation, and controls across the app are more consistently named and labeled.
 • Touch targets and text scaling: Interactive elements meet minimum target-size requirements, and the app better supports enlarged text sizes.
 • Visual presentation: Improved color contrast and more consistent navigation patterns throughout the app.
The updated ACR is available in the Trust Center document library. We're committed to continuing to improve accessibility across Claude, and we welcome feedback from customers and end users.
