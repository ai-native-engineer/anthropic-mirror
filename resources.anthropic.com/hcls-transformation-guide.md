Guide

The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide for Healthcare & Life Sciences

While [75% of healthcare companies](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/life-sciences-and-health-care/articles/generative-ai-in-healthcare.html) are experimenting with GenAI this year, the path to transformation remains uniquely challenging in an industry where patient safety, regulatory compliance, and clinical workflows demand a measured approach. Whether you're just getting started or accelerating toward org-wide AI adoption, our Enterprise AI Transformation Guide for Healthcare & Life Sciences delivers a proven three-step blueprint for driving AI impact while prioritizing patient safety and regulatory compliance.

Drawing from implementations at Pfizer, Novo Nordisk, and more organizations, this guide shows how to build your foundation with robust governance and stakeholder alignment, launch carefully selected pilots in lower-risk applications like scientific documentation and revenue cycle management, and scale what works through structured training programs and centers of excellence.
