Building Effective AI Agents

Engineering leaders building AI systems face a critical decision: when does a simple prompt-response pattern fall short, and when do you need autonomous agents that can reason, use tools, and adapt their approach?

Building Effective AI Agents: Architecture Patterns and Implementation Frameworks shares best practices and guidelines for building production-ready agentic systems. We'll cover single-agent designs, multi-agent orchestration, and agentic workflows, with real examples from companies like Coinbase, Intercom, and Thomson Reuters who are building and deploying these systems at scale.

In this guide, we'll share practical guidance for organizations building agentic systems, including:

How to choose between single-agent, multi-agent, and workflow-based architectures

When to use sequential, parallel, and evaluator-optimizer patterns

How to match technical complexity to business value using our decision framework

Best practices for context management, modular design, and using Skills.

Real-world use cases across coding, customer support, legal, and financial services
