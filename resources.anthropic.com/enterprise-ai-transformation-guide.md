The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

[92% of companies](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full-potential-at-work) plan to invest in AI over the next 3 years, yet only 1 percent believe their investments have reached full maturity.

Whether you're just getting started or well on your path to org-wide AI literacy, our Enterprise AI Transformation Guide delivers a proven three-step blueprint for accelerating AI adoption. Drawing from our work with organizations like NBIM, Thomson Reuters, and Cox Automotive, this guide shows how to lay your AI foundation with clear governance and stakeholder alignment, launch targeted pilots that demonstrate value, and drive impact through structured training programs. We'll also share how teams at Anthropic deploy Claude across engineering, legal, and finance—plus a readiness assessment to determine your optimal AI transformation path.

In this guide, we'll share practical guidance for accelerating enterprise AI transformation, including:

How to build executive alignment and assemble effective AI steering committees

Tips for choosing pilots that demonstrate value in 30-60 days

How to measure success across AI adoption, efficiency, quality, and satisfaction

Guidance for assessing your organization's AI readiness

Real-world AI implementations from Anthropic's own teams, including Legal, Finance, Marketing, and more
