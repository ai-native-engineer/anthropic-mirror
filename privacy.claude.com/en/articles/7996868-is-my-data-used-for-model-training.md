# Is my data used for model training?

*This article is about our commercial products such as Claude for Work and the Anthropic API. For our consumer products such as Claude Free, Pro, Max and when accounts from those plans use Claude Code, see* *[here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training).*

By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products *(e.g. Claude for Work, Anthropic API, Claude Gov, etc.)* to train our models.

If you explicitly report feedback or bugs to us (e.g. via our thumbs up/down feedback button), or otherwise choose to allow us to use your data, then we may use your chats and coding sessions to train our models.

To find out more information regarding your use of an Anthropic commercial offering, or if you would like to know how to contact us regarding a privacy related topic, see our [Trust Center](https://trust.anthropic.com/) and [Commercial Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms).

## Feedback

When you provide us feedback via our thumbs up / down button, we will store the entire related conversation, including any content, custom styles, conversation preferences, or model settings, in our secured back-end for up to 5 years. Feedback data does not include raw content from [connectors](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/10168395-setting-up-integrations-on-claude-ai) (e.g. Google Drive), including remote and local MCP servers, though data may be included if it’s directly copied into your conversation with Claude.

We de-link your feedback from your user and customer IDs before it’s used by Anthropic. We may use your feedback to analyze the effectiveness of our services, conduct research, study user behavior, and train our AI models as permitted under applicable laws. We do not combine your feedback with your other conversations with Claude.
