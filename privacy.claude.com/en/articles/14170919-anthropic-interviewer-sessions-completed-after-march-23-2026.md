# Anthropic Interviewer sessions completed after March 23, 2026

March 26, 2026

*This article is about Anthropic Interviewer, which is a research survey tool running on Claude Free, Pro, Max. Anthropic Interviewer is not currently available for our commercial products such as Claude for Work and the Anthropic API.*

If you completed an Anthropic Interviewer session on or after March 23, 2026, this article describes how we collect and use your responses. For sessions completed in December 2025, see [this article](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/14170926-anthropic-interviewer-sessions-completed-in-december-2025).

When you participate in an Anthropic Interviewer session, we collect the responses you provide during the interview, any associated survey or study metadata, associated account data, and metadata about your participation (such as timestamps and session identifiers). We refer to this information collectively as “Study Participation Data”. You can learn more about how we handle this data in the [Supplemental Anthropic Interviewer Privacy Policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/14139821-supplemental-anthropic-interviewer-privacy-policy).

We may publish and share aggregated or de-identified research summaries. We may also publish and share your de-identified responses, consistent with the choices you make during a session, where applicable. Your responses and account information may also be used for research and improvements.

In particular, to support open research, and to inform public understanding of these issues we may share Study Participation Data with select third parties such as external researchers or journalists, again, consistent with the choices you make during a session, where applicable.

To better understand how the perspectives you share relate to the ways people use Claude, we may combine your interview responses with aggregated, privacy-preserving summaries of your Claude usage data. This analysis is done using [automated techniques](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10807912-how-does-clio-analyze-usage-patterns-while-protecting-user-data) that group related responses together. Additionally, if you participate in more than one Anthropic Interviewer study, we may analyze your responses together to understand how perspectives change over time.

We may email you to share published findings or insights from research interviews you've participated in with you.

Anthropic workers with a need to know will be able to access your Study Participation Data. Additionally, as with any conversation with Claude, Anthropic Interviewer sessions may be reviewed by our Trust and Safety team if flagged for review under our [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup).
