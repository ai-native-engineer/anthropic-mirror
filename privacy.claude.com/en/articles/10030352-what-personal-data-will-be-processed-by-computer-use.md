# What personal data will be processed by Computer use?

*This article is about our commercial products such as Claude for Work and the Anthropic API. For our consumer products such as Claude Free, Pro, Max and when accounts from those plans use Claude Code, see* *[here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/collections/10663362-consumers).*

## What is Computer Use?

Computer use is a beta feature included in our new models.

With computer use, developers can direct Claude to use computers the way people do—by interpreting screen content, moving a cursor, clicking, and inputting text.

Computer use is currently only available to our commercial customers using the Anthropic API, and must be built and enabled by a developer.

##

## What Personal Data May Be Processed by Computer Use?

When prompted by a user, computer use will process and collect screenshots from the computer's display that Claude uses to interpret and interact with the interface, along with the user’s Inputs and Outputs. Anthropic will not collect any additional data from users' computer interface.

When using our commercial products (e.g. Claude for Work, Anthropic API, etc.), Anthropic does not use your Inputs or Outputs to train our models, unless you explicitly report materials to us, or have otherwise opted in to training (e.g. [Development Partner Program](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11174108-about-the-development-partner-program)). [Read more here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training).

By default, Anthropic will automatically delete all screenshots from our backend within 30 days, unless the customer and Anthropic have agreed to different terms. Anthropic retains this data to help enforce our [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup). [Learn more](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-personal-data).

For more information about our computer use, please see our [Announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/research/developing-computer-use) and [Docs](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/computer-use).
