# Where are your servers located? Do you host your models on EU servers?

June 15, 2026

*This article is about our commercial products such as Claude Enterprise and the Anthropic API. For our consumer products such as Claude Free, Pro, Max and when accounts from those plans use Claude Code, see* *[here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/collections/10663362-consumers).*

Anthropic utilizes multiple cloud service providers to process customer data, as documented in our [Subprocessor list](https://trust.anthropic.com/subprocessors). By default, we may route customer traffic to select countries in the US, Europe, Asia and Australia, unless otherwise agreed upon or at your instructions. Learn more about selecting your traffic routing location for Claude Developer Platform users [here](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/data-residency), and more about selecting your traffic routing location for Enterprise Plan users on usage-based billing [here](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15422948-enable-us-only-inference-for-your-organization). Note that data is stored in the US. We may also process data for internal processes (such as safety-related review, product support, or incident response) in countries where we or our [affiliates](https://trust.anthropic.com/faq?s=xxqugau3fjgw3b04tnky5) operate.

To receive proactive updates of where we or our Subprocessors operate, please subscribe to our [Trust Center News Feed](https://trust.anthropic.com/updates).
