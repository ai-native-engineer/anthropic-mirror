# What is your approach to GDPR or related issues?

We approach data privacy and security holistically, assessing worldwide privacy laws and regulations together with our customers’ needs in the unique context of artificial intelligence and large language models. Our [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), Data Processing Addendum, and Help Center articles explain how Anthropic handles personal data, whether directly from consumers or when we act as a processor for our customers.
