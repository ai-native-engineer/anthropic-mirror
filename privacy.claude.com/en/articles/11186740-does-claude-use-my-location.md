# Does Claude use my location?

*This article is about our consumer products such as Claude Free, Pro, Max and when accounts from those plans use Claude Code. For our commercial products such as Claude for Work and the Anthropic API, see* *[here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/11186730-does-claude-use-my-team-members-location).*

## Optional Collection for Product Features

When you use product features that benefit from location data (for example, using [web search](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/10684626-enabling-and-using-web-search) to ask Claude to find local results), Claude may use your IP address to determine coarse-grained location (city/region level).

You can disable usage of location data to enhance product features like web search from your [privacy dashboard](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls). Claude's access to location data for non-web surfaces (e.g. the mobile app and browser extension) are managed at the device level.

## Mandatory Collection for Platform Security and Anti-Abuse

Anthropic uses IP address and other signals to infer coarse-grained location (country/region level) in order to ensure usage of Claude complies with our terms, prevent abuse, and show users features available in their region. This use is necessary to protect our users and business and cannot be toggled off. Please refer to our [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) to learn more.
