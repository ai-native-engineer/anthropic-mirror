# Can you delete data sent via Claude?

*This article is about our consumer products such as Claude Free, Pro, Max and when accounts from those plans use Claude Code. For our commercial products such as Claude for Work and the Anthropic API, see* *[here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/collections/10663361-commercial-customers).*

If you use our consumer offerings (e.g. Free Claude.ai, Claude Pro plan), your prompts and conversations will remain available to you in your dashboard so that you can see your conversation history. You have control to delete your conversations, which will be immediately deleted from your conversation history and automatically deleted from our back-end within 30 days. Learn more [here](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/10023670-how-can-i-delete-or-rename-a-conversation).

Note that we also retain data in our backend systems for the amount of time specified in our [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) unless required to enforce our [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup), address Terms of Service or policy violations, or as required by law. Learn more about our retention periods by visiting our [Privacy Center](https://privacy.anthropic.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-personal-data).

For more information on data retention and deletion, see our [Trust Center](https://trust.anthropic.com/) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
