### Event information

Created for the people turning frontier AI into real companies and real products. The morning founder cohort brings together founders and CEOs of the UK's most ambitious AI-native startups for candid sessions on where the models are heading and the challenges facing founders. The afternoon builder cohort is hands-on: bring a laptop, build alongside Anthropic engineers who ship with Claude every day, and show what you made at the community showcase.

Places in both cohorts are curated, so apply below for the session that fits you. Technical founders are welcome to join both.

‍

Morning

Founder cohort

8:30 am – 1:00 pm BST

Who

Founders and CEOs of the UK's top AI-native companies.

What

A morning built to sharpen your next twelve months. Anthropic's read on where the frontier is heading next and candid insights from founders on what rebuilding their companies around AI actually took and returned.

On the agenda

**Anthropic on where Claude goes next** and why startups and Anthropic win together

**How we use Claude inside Anthropic:** an internal playbook for shipping with Claude

**Founder panels:** on building product, teams, and margins around AI

**Office hours:** connect with Anthropic's Applied AI team

**Networking:** a curated room with time built in to work it

[Apply to attend ↗](https://luma.com/claude-q3ux)

Afternoon

Builder cohort

2:00 pm – 8:00 pm BST

Who's it for

Developers, engineers and technical founders.

What

An afternoon built for shipping.

On the agenda

**Applied AI masterclass:** how to build with Claude and agents

**Guided build:** one build, waymarked to meet you at your level, from first agent to production patterns

**Community showcase:** built something with Claude? Pitch it in your application, and selected builders demo live on stage for a prize

**Networking and after party:** stay on after the showcase; some of the best conversations start once the laptops close

[Apply to attend ↗](https://luma.com/claude-hd83)
