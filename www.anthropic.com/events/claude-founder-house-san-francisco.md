## Claude Founder House San Francisco

Add to calendar

### Event information

Claude Founder House is our home base for SF Tech Week. For three days, we're taking over a two-floor warehouse at Terra Gallery and turning it into a place to work, learn, and meet other founders and operators in the ecosystem. The café is open all day, with talks, workshops, meetings and Applied AI office hours running throughout. Stop by for a single session or join us all three days.

Tuesday – Thursday

Founder House

11:00 am – 5:00 pm PDT

Who

Founders, CEOs and technical co-founders building AI-native companies.

What

A working café and a full daily program. Drop in to cowork, book time with Anthropic's Applied AI team, and hear how the people building at the frontier are doing it.

On the agenda

**Talks and firesides:** Founders from PostHog, Netlify, Simile, Gamma, Town, Retool, and OpenEvidence, investors from Sequoia and Lightspeed, and Anthropic leaders on what they're building and where AI is headed.

**Workshops:** Hands-on sessions from startups and the Anthropic team.

**Office hours:** Book time with Anthropic's Applied AI team for help with what you're building.

**Cowork and community:** Coffee, desks, and time to meet other founders, plus brunches and a founders run club.

[Apply to attend ↗](https://luma.com/claude-founder-house-sf)

Thursday, Oct 8

Founder Salon

1:45 pm – 4:45 pm PDT

Who's it for

Founders and builders shipping on Claude.

What

An afternoon and evening built to show what founders are shipping.

On the agenda

**Talks and firesides:** David Rosenthal of the Acquired podcast in conversation with Mike Krieger, plus other Anthropic leaders on what's next for startups building on Claude.

**Live demos:** Watch founders demo live on stage, then vote for your favorites. Want to show yours? Add a short video demo when you apply.

[Apply to attend ↗](https://luma.com/claude-founder-showcase)
