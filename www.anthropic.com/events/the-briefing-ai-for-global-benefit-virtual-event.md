## The Briefing: AI for Global Benefit

Add to calendar

### Event information

AI could compress decades of scientific and economic progress into years and help solve some of the hardest problems we face. But these outcomes are not guaranteed. Realizing this potential takes deliberate work and deep partnerships with the people who understand these problems best.

Anthropic was founded as a public benefit corporation, which means we have a responsibility to ensure the positive benefits of AI are widely shared. Over the past year, we've been working alongside nonprofits, philanthropic partners, governments, and mission-driven organizations to put Claude to work on some of our most pressing societal challenges.

On September 22nd, tune in for an evening of leaders discussing the progress, challenges, and opportunities in ensuring the benefits of AI are realized globally.

#### What you'll experience

* A fireside conversation with Anthropic leadership on why this work matters to us, and our approach to partnerships.
* Announcements and spotlights from partners, with shared learnings and perspectives on the possibilities ahead.

#### Who should watch

Leaders applying AI to pressing societal challenges, and ensuring its benefits reach communities with the most to gain. This includes heads of philanthropic organizations, NGO and multilateral executives, institutional funders, ministers and delegations.
