### About this event

**What you’ll gain**

Hear directly from Anthropic experts on a range of topics, with sessions tailored for both: Leaders driving Claude within their organisations and Developers and knowledge workers keen to upskill in how they use AI and Claude in their day to day.

**Who is this for?**

* For leaders: Get change management guidance, learn provisioning and governance best practices, and build the best approach to cost and value realisation for your organisation.
* For everyone: Deepen your AI fluency as the intelligence rapidly evolves and get hands-on with Cowork and Claude Code alongside Anthropic solutions engineers
