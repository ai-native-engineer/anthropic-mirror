#### Effective Context Engineering for AI Agents (AIM103-S)

This session walks through the anatomy of effective context: system prompts at the right altitude, token-efficient tools, just-in-time retrieval over pre-loaded data, and the three techniques Anthropic relies on for long-horizon work — compaction, structured memory, and sub-agent architectures. Each pattern is grounded in how Claude Code uses it. You'll leave with a practical mental model for treating context as a precious resource and a checklist for auditing your own agents.
