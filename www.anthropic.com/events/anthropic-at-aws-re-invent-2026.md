### Anthropic and AWS

Join Anthropic at AWS re:Invent 2026 to learn how customers are making employees smarter, automating complex operations, and building the next generation of AI products with Claude on AWS.

Anthropic is a frontier AI company whose mission is to steer the trajectory of AI to advance human progress. We are best known for building Claude, the intelligence platform trusted by millions of people and businesses worldwide. Anthropic is a public benefit corporation—a for-profit committed to operating in service of social and public good—and controlled by a Long Term Benefit Trust, a group of independent experts in AI safety, national security, public policy, and social enterprise.

#### Claude Keeps Building: How Coding Agents Change A Software Development

Coding agents make sustained, autonomous progress on real software. The question is what happens to the rest of the software development lifecycle. Claude Code runs for hours unattended, works beyond the terminal, and takes on more of the work around the code. We'll show where it stands and how engineering changes when the whole lifecycle becomes automatable: what engineers do, how review and testing evolve, where quality comes from when code is cheap. Live demos on AWS and numbers from our engineering, where Claude writes most of our code. This presentation is brought to you by Anthropic, an AWS Partner.

#### The Frontier Doesn't Hold Still: Running Claude Agents at Scale on AWS

Enterprises are moving faster with AI than ever. And they're falling further behind. Once the first agents land and the numbers look good, most teams turn to cost control while the frontier keeps moving. This session covers how to do both with Claude on AWS: the security and compliance controls enterprises require, execution inside your own boundary, observability and cost governance, and the safeguards that let you trust agents with more. Then how to keep expanding what agents do as new models arrive, so efficiency doesn't become the ceiling. This presentation is brought to you by Anthropic, an AWS Partner.

#### The Capability Overhang: Ask More of the Frontier

Frontier models can do more than anyone asks of them. At Anthropic, the hardest part of testing a new model is finding work hard enough to make it fail. Outside, most teams hand Claude the tasks they gave last year's model. This session closes that gap: what the newest Claude models do on AWS when you delegate the job, not the task. Multi-day autonomous work, large migrations end to end, agents that verify their results and keep going. Evals, live demos, and how our researchers keep raising the bar. This presentation is brought to you by Anthropic, an AWS Partner.
