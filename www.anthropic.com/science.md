# Science

Using AI to increase the pace of scientific progress is a core part of Anthropic’s mission. Here, we’ll explore what this really looks like. We’ll share work happening inside and outside Anthropic, our collaborations with external researchers and labs, and practical workflows for scientists using AI in their research.

## Learning more about Claude's mathematical capabilities

An unreleased research version of Claude has made strides on a problem related to the Riemann hypothesis. It improved a longstanding lower bound for the fraction of zeros of the Riemann zeta function that satisfy the hypothesis, increasing it from 41.6% to 67.2%.

[Read more](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta)
