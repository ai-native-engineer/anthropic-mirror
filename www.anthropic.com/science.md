# Science

Using AI to increase the pace of scientific progress is a core part of Anthropic’s mission. Here, we’ll explore what this really looks like. We’ll share work happening inside and outside Anthropic, our collaborations with external researchers and labs, and practical workflows for scientists using AI in their research.

Science

## Formalizing Fermat's Last Theorem

Claude produced the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem. It worked largely autonomously over 11 days, writing the proof in the Lean programming language. We describe how the formalization was done and share some thoughts about what this work could mean for research mathematics.
