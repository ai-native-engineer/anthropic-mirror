# AI Fluency

The habits that separate people who get great results from people who get average ones: what to delegate, how to describe what you want, how to judge the output, and staying accountable for it.

## Featuring

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67ed58c92cfedc451ebbbca1/6aa3271bca428e88daeac15e_Screenshot%202026-09-10%20at%202.54.22%E2%80%AFPM.png)

### Kristen Swanson

Head of AI Fluency @ Anthropic

## What you’ll learn

* Which tasks to hand to Claude, and which are better kept for yourself
* How to describe what you want clearly enough to get a useful first draft
* How to judge the output, and what to do when it is not right
* How to stay accountable for work you produced with AI
