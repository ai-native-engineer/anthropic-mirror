# Claude Security: Putting Claude to Work for Defenders

The metric that matters for security teams today is time from scan to shipped fix. Claude Security is built around closing that gap: it scans your codebase the way a security researcher would, validates each finding before it reaches your team, and generates a targeted patch you can open and work through in Claude Code.

Join us for a live demo of how Claude Security, in public beta for Claude Enterprise customers, takes you from first scan to merged fix. We’ll also share how this work connects to Anthropic's safety research, why we built for defenders first, and what we learned with hundreds of teams during the preview - followed by a Q&A.
