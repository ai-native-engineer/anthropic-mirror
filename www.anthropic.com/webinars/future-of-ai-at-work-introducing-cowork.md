# The Future of AI at Work: Introducing Cowork

Chat changed how we get answers. Claude Code transformed how developers build software. Now, [Cowork](https://claude.com/product/cowork) brings that same execution power to everyone.

Join Boris Cherny and Mikaela Grace from Anthropic for a look at the future of AI at work—where Claude doesn't just answer questions, but actually does the work  alongside you. See live demos of multi-step workflows, learn what "agentic AI” really means in practice, and discover how your team can put this technology to use today.

* **The Chat → Code → Cowork evolution** — Why Cowork exists and what problem it solves
* **What agentic AI actually looks like** — The difference between asking questions and getting work done
* **Tangible use cases** — Live demos of real workflows including research synthesis, document creation, and data extraction
