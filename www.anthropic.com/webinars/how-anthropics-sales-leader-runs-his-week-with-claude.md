# How Anthropic’s sales team run their week with Cowork

Two members of Anthropic's sales team, Travis Bryant, Head of US Mid-Market GTM, and Brittney Tong, Growth Account Executive, will show some of the Claude Cowork workflows they use every week. You'll see a daily briefing that assembles your data before your first meeting, a Friday forecast pulled from Salesforce and BigQuery in the format leadership already expects, and an overnight workflow that scored 4,000 accounts so AEs knew where to focus.

## What you’ll learn

* **How to set Cowork up for sales —** the connectors, context, and skills you wire up so Claude knows your pipeline, your accounts, and the report formats your leadership expects.
* **Three workflows to take back your week —** a daily prep stack, a Friday forecast pulled from Salesforce and BigQuery, and an overnight account-scoring routine that ships an interactive dashboard your reps prospect from.**‍**
* **How to stay in control —** plain-language prompts and human approval on every send, with Salesforce and your data warehouse still the system of record.
