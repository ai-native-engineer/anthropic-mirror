# How finance teams use Claude Cowork

Watch Claude take on the finance work that eats up your close and your quarter. We'll show how FP&A and controllership teams (including Anthropic’s own finance team!) are using Claude Cowork across forecasting, variance, close, and consolidations — and answer your questions on what it looks like to actually roll it out.

## What you’ll learn

* A hands-on demo of Cowork working through real finance artifacts — rolling forecast updates, variance commentary, flux analysis, and board-packet assembly
* Where Cowork fits in a finance workflow: handling first-pass narrative and supporting schedules so your team's time goes to the judgment calls that actually need a human
* How Anthropic’s own finance team uses Cowork
* Practical answers on data handling, controls, audit trail, and the rollout questions your IT team and Controller are going to raise
