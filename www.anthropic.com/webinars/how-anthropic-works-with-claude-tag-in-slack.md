# How Anthropic works with Claude Tag in Slack

Anthropic has run on @Claude in Slack for the past year. It now opens roughly 65% of our pull requests and sits in channels across engineering, GTM, support, and data science. In this session, the people who built and use Claude Tag show exactly how they work with it: the channels it lives in, the prompts they use.

## What you’ll learn

* How Claude Tag works in practice: a live demo of tagging in a thread, ambient triage, and long-running tasks that hand back when done
* Channels and workflows Anthropic teams use every day across eng, data science, and GTM
* What Claude can and can't see: a deep-dive into permissions and the agent-identity model
