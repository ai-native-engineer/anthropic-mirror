# Your first real workflow in Cowork

Get up and running on Cowork through hands-on guidance. We'll show you how to set up your Cowork properly, picking the right model for the job and prompting with your connectors to then run your first agent task end to end. You leave with one workflow you'll use again on Monday. Have the Cowork Desktop app downloaded before the session so you can dive right in!
