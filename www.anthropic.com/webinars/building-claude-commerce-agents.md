# Building Claude Commerce Agents

AI is rapidly changing commerce, and consumer brands and platforms are on the move to respond. In today's webinar, Ali Shazal will walk you through our new blueprints for consumer and merchant agents. He'll unpack reference implementations across retail, travel, telecom, and entertainment, and he'll give you unfiltered advice from his experience working with teams building these experiences on frontier AI. Bring your hardest questions on building, auth, latency, and more. We'll do a live Q&A during the session.
