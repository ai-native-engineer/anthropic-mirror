# How to Roadmap with Decisions Rather Than Dates

Many of the "best practices" of engineering leadership have been built around managing dates, timelines and priorities. However, as AI harnesses have improved over the past two years, it's increasingly possible to lead in a new way: running a roadmap that is constrained by decisions rather than dates. In this session, we'll discuss how to move towards operating in a decision-constrained engineering organization.

*This session is a part of the* [*Guest Lecture: Leadership in Engineering*](https://www.anthropic.com/webinar-series/guest-lecture-series-leadership-in-engineering) *series. Views expressed are the speakers' own, not Anthropic's.*

## What you’ll learn

* How to empower small teams by reducing handoffs with AI discovery
* Minimizing cross-functional approval gates with clear automatable interfaces, such as Linear issues being resolved automatically by an internal harness
* Eliminating most open decisions through rapid prototyping and feature flagging
* Escalating quickly where the above tools don't suffice
