# Cowork Workshop: Foundations

Join Anthropic's Cowork team for a hands-on introduction to Cowork — the mode of Claude that goes beyond chat to delegate real, multi-step work. In one hour, we'll go from setup to a finished deliverable, show how to teach Claude the way your team works, and walk through the tools that let Cowork gather context from your existing tools, your browser, and your documents.

## What you’ll learn

* **What Cowork is and how to hand it your first task**: installing the desktop app, pointing Claude at a working folder, connecting your tools, and delegating a piece of work end-to-end.
* **Teach Cowork how your team works**: global instructions, projects, skills, and plugins that encode your processes so Claude runs them the way you would.**‍**
* **Use Cowork wherever your work lives**: Claude in Chrome for web apps and dashboards, and Claude for M365 for in-document editing across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
