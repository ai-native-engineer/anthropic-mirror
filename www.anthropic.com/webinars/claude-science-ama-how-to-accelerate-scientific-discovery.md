# Claude Science AMA: How to accelerate scientific discovery

A scientist's work is scattered across dozens of disconnected tools, databases, and compute environments — every transition between them is where time and context are lost.

Claude Science is built to solve this toil as the AI workbench for every stage of scientific research. Join our product team for live demos and an open Q&A on how to accelerate scientific discovery.

Bonus: hear from one of our recent Claude Science hackathon winners on how she’s deploying agents into cancer research workflows and what she learned along the way.
