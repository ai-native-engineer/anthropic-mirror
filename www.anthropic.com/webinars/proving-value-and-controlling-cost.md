# Proving value and controlling cost

In this 90 minute session, we'll walk you through two sides to driving value: how to best manage costs and how to more meaningfully measure and drive toward value. We'll walk you through the levers you have to better control spend: model choice, effort levels, spend limits, and admin reporting. Then we'll guide you through our Value Realization framework to help you baseline, measure, and report on the impact your organisation is realizing.

* How to bring cost control and value measurement together into one story you can take to your leadership
