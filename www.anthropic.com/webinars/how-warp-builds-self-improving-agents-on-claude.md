# How Warp builds self improving agents on Claude

Most teams deploy an agent, watch it work, and move on. Warp built something different: agents that study how your team corrects them and rewrite their own skills to do better next time.

Join Warp founder Zach Lloyd and Anthropic Applied AI for a technical look at self-improvement loops: how to capture human feedback signals, turn them into skill updates, and expand agents from one-off helpers into systems that compound across your org. Live demos include Warp’s PR review agent and the social listening bot they use to bring agentic workflows into community management.

## What you’ll learn

* **From one-off to org-wide**: How to take a single agent use case and turn it into a repeatable automation pattern across teams
* **Self-improvement loops with skills**:  Using skills as the substrate for agents to capture, review, and learn from human feedback
* **Deep dives: PR review agent and social listening**:  eveloper-focused walkthroughs of how Warp’s agent refines its own review criteria over time**‍**
* **Frameworks you can steal**: Warp’s approach to evaluating feedback signals and deciding what the agent should learn vs. ignore
