# Virtual Claude Workshop

This Claude Workshop, presented with Tenex, is a 1.5 hour product experience for business leaders who live in documents, spreadsheets, decks, and cross-tool workflows — Finance, Operations, Marketing, HR, Legal, Sales. The focus is the product, the people who build it, and the work you do every week.

## What you’ll learn

* **See Claude handle a real workday, not a demo sandbox.** A live "Day in the Life" that follows a business leader through one continuous morning: the brief pulled from Slack and Salesforce, the variance analysis the CFO needed by noon, the board deck that built itself, the internal tool Engineering said they couldn't get to this quarter.
* **Build something real for your role in 45 minutes.** Hands-on from the first minute with Claude, Claude for Excel and PowerPoint, and connectors across Google Workspace, Salesforce, Slack, and Jira. Anthropic and Tenex teams on the call to unblock you.
* **Leave with a plan, not a pile of notes.** You walk out with a personalized "Your First Week with Claude" guide already saved — ready to open Monday morning.
